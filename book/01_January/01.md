## January 1st - Control and Focus

***“The principal task in life is this: distinguish matters and weigh them one against another, and say to yourself, ‘Externals are not under my control; moral choice is under my control. Where am I to look for the good and the evil? Within me, in that which is my own.’”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 2.5.4-5

You inherit a codebase nobody wants to touch. No docs, no tests, a commit history full of messages like “fix” and “fix again.” The first reaction is to get angry: at whoever wrote it, at the deadline, at the manager who promised it would take two days.

None of that is yours to change. The code is already written. The deadline was set before you joined the call.

What is yours is the next hour. You can read one module properly. You can write the test nobody wrote. You can tell your lead, plainly, what two days actually buys.

Epictetus was born a slave. He knew the difference between what happens to you and what you do with it better than any of us ever will. Start the year with his question, and ask it every time something breaks: which part of this is actually mine?
