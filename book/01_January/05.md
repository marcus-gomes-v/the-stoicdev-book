## January 5th - UNDERSTAND YOUR GOALS

***“Let all your efforts be directed to something, let it keep that end in view. It’s not activity that disturbs people, but false conceptions of things that drive them mad.”***

—SENECA, ON TRANQUILITY OF MIND, 12.5

In the realm of software development, entering a day or a week with a clear vision of your objectives is crucial. It's not just about being busy with tasks; it's about understanding what needs to be achieved. When you receive a project or a task, it's essential to grasp fully what the stakeholder envisions what is their definition of success, and what exactly do they aim to accomplish with this project?

Having a goal isn't just a marker to strive towards; it's a compass that guides your daily actions, decisions, and priorities. Without a clear end in mind, it's easy to drift off course, to become mired in irrelevant details or sidetracked by unimportant tasks. A clear goal provides focus and direction, ensuring that every effort you put in is meaningful and aligned with the desired outcome.

As a developer, take the time to clarify your goals at the beginning of each project. Ask questions, seek understanding, and ensure you have a vivid picture of what success looks like. This clarity of purpose will not only streamline your workflow but will also give you a sense of progress and accomplishment as you move closer to your target. Remember, a journey without a destination is merely wandering. Set your goals clearly, and let them light your path to success.
