January 6th - Discovering Your True North in Tech

“A person who doesn’t know what the universe is, doesn’t know where they are. A person who doesn’t know their purpose in life doesn’t know who they are or what the universe is. A person who doesn’t know any one of these things doesn’t know why they are here. So what to make of people who seek or avoid the praise of those who have no knowledge of where or who they are?”

—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 8.52

In the journey of a software engineer, there comes a pivotal moment of self-realization – understanding not just your role, but your true calling within the vast universe of technology. This realization often comes not in the midst of hectic projects, but in the quiet moments of reflection. Like my own journey, which led from the grassroots of development to the pinnacle of technical leadership and then to a sabbatical of self-discovery, each engineer must find their own path.

Today, take a moment to ponder the fundamental questions: What aspect of your work ignites your passion? Is it the thrill of solving complex problems, the satisfaction of crafting elegant code, or the joy of collaborating on groundbreaking ideas? Reflect on what you truly love about your role. Remember, we live two lives – the one we are born into, and the one we discover when we find what we truly love to do.

Let this day be a step towards discovering your true 'north' in your tech career. It's not just about the job title or the technical skills, but about finding what resonates with your core as an engineer and problem solver. As you progress in your career, keep this understanding close to your heart. It will guide you in making choices that align with your true passion, leading to fulfillment and excellence in your field.
