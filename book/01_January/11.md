## January 11th - If You Want to Be Unsteady

***“For if a person shifts their caution to their own reasoned choices and the acts of those choices, they will at the same time gain the will to avoid, but if they shift their caution away from their own reasoned choices to things not under their control, seeking to avoid what is controlled by others, they will then be agitated, fearful, and unstable.”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 2.1.12

Imagine a developer constantly chasing the latest trends, jumping from one buzzword to the next, always seeking greenfield projects. This image starkly contrasts with the Zen philosopher's serenity and embodies the essence of instability. Such a developer, swayed by every new framework and style, lacks the steadiness of a StoicDev.

Think about the developer, who, like a seasoned craftsman, focuses on substance over style. They might work on legacy systems, often overlooked, yet providing unparalleled stability. Today, identify one aspect of your work where you can embody this Stoic steadiness. Whether it’s refining an undervalued project or persisting with a complex code, embrace it with focus and resilience. This is your path to true professional stability, not found in fleeting trends, but in your reasoned, dedicated approach.
