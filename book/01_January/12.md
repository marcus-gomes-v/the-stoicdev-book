January 12th - The One Path to Serenity

“Keep this thought at the ready at daybreak, and through the day and night—there is only one path to happiness, and that is in giving up all outside of your sphere of choice, regarding nothing else as your possession, surrendering all else to God and Fortune.”
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 4.4.39

Today, practice the Stoic exercise of differentiating between what is within your control and what isn't. In the morning, remind yourself to focus only on what you can control. Before lunch, reflect on the fact that your true possession is your ability to make choices and use reason and judgment. In the afternoon, acknowledge that while you make your own choices, fate is not entirely in your hands. The world moves independently of your will.

In the evening, recognize the extent of what lies beyond your control, and understand the boundaries of your choices. As you go to bed, embrace sleep as an act of surrender and trust, a natural reminder of releasing what we can't control. This cycle of awareness and acceptance is your path to serenity, repeating it each day aligns you more with the Stoic way of life.
