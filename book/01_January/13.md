## January 13th - Circle of Control

***“We control our reasoned choice and all acts that depend on that moral will. What’s not under our control are the body and any of its parts, our possessions, parents, siblings, children, or country —anything with which we might associate.”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 1.22.10

As a developer, it's crucial to direct your focus towards what you can genuinely influence: your decisions, your approach to problems, and the solutions you create. The wisdom of Stoicism reminds us of the importance of distinguishing between what's within our circle of control our mind, our choices and what's outside it everything else.

Remember, the only true domain you govern is your reasoned choice. While you might face unexpected challenges, such as project changes or technological shifts, your power lies in how you respond to these changes. Embrace the simplicity in focusing solely on your mental and moral will. This not only simplifies your responsibilities but also empowers you to make impactful, positive contributions through your work.

Today, focus on this one thing: your mind. Let it guide your actions, influence your responses, and lead your decision-making process. By centering your attention on what you control, you navigate the complex landscape of software development with clarity and purpose. This focused approach is your most potent tool in building, changing, and achieving in the realm of technology.
