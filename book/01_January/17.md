January 17th - Initiating Action: Beyond Fear of Failure

“I am your teacher and you are learning in my school. My aim is to bring you to completion, unhindered, free from compulsive behavior, unrestrained, without shame, free, flourishing, and happy, looking to God in things great and small—your aim is to learn and diligently practice all these things. Why then don’t you complete the work, if you have the right aim and I have both the right aim and right preparation? What is missing? . . . The work is quite feasible, and is the only thing in our power. . . . Let go of the past. We must only begin. Believe me and you will see.” 
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 2.19.29-34

Remember the hesitation to try new things, fearing failure? As developers, the stakes now involve the quality of our work and our impact on the tech world. Yet, the guidance we need is at our fingertips, through the wisdom of the greatest philosophical minds. They teach us not only the potential within us but also the simplicity in starting. 

The initial step is not about overcoming a monumental challenge; it's about overcoming the inertia of our own apprehensions. 

Today, let's embrace the lesson to just begin. The journey of development, both personal and professional, starts with a single step. This action sets the foundation for growth, innovation, and the ability to shape the world around us. Forget fear of failure; focus on the power of starting.
