## January 19th - Choice: Your Constant Companion

***“A platform and a prison is each a place, the one high, and the other low; but your moral purpose can be kept the same, if you wish to keep it the same, in either place.”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 2.6.25

Epictetus knew both ends of this. He was born a slave, was banished from Rome along with the other philosophers under Domitian, and ended up one of the best-known teachers of his time. Platform and prison weren’t figures of speech for him.

A career has its own highs and lows, smaller ones. One year you’re on stage at a conference, demoing the feature your team spent months on. Another year you’re on the team being wound down, writing handover docs for a product nobody will maintain. Or you’re laid off on a Tuesday by a fifteen-minute calendar invite.

It’s easy to believe you’d be a different person in each of those places: generous and relaxed on the stage, bitter and checked out in the bad year. Epictetus says that part is up to you. The place changes. What you choose to be inside it can stay the same.

You can write a careful handover doc. You can be decent to the manager who has to deliver the bad news. You can keep learning on the dull project. And when you’re back on a stage, you can remember who helped you get there.
