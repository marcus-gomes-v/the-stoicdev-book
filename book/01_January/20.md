## January 20th - Renewing Your Perspective

***“Your principles can’t be extinguished unless you snuff out the thoughts that feed them, for it’s continually in your power to reignite new ones. . . . It’s possible to start living again! See things anew as you once did—that is how to restart life!”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 7.2

Have recent challenges led you astray from your core beliefs and values? It's natural. Life's trials can sometimes dim our guiding lights, but Marcus Aurelius reminds us that our principles stand immutable, awaiting our return. Perhaps he penned these words in a moment of self-reflection, amid the trials of leadership or personal turmoil. It's a universal experience losing sight of our ideals in the face of adversity.

Yet, here lies a powerful truth: the past, with all its disappointments, is behind us. Our principles endure, ready for us to rekindle and embrace them anew. This moment offers an opportunity for renewal, to view our lives with fresh eyes and recommit to our values. Why not seize this chance to reset and revive your journey? Let today be the day you renew your perspective and rediscover the path to your true north.
