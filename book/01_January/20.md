## January 20th - Renewing Your Perspective

***“How can our principles become dead, unless the impressions [thoughts] which correspond to them are extinguished? But it is in thy power continuously to fan these thoughts into a flame... To recover thy life is in thy power. Look at things again as thou didst use to look at them; for in this consists the recovery of thy life.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 7.2

Maybe you started this year with good intentions. Write the tests first. Stop answering messages at midnight. Be more patient in code review. Three weeks in, a deadline landed, and all three went out the window.

Marcus would say the principles aren’t dead. A principle only dies when you stop thinking the thoughts that keep it alive, and those thoughts are yours to fan back into a flame whenever you like. He wrote these notes to himself, many of them during long years of war on the northern frontier, so he clearly needed the reminder too.

So don’t wait for next January, or a new job, to start over. Look at your work again the way you looked at it when you cared most, before the cynicism set in, when a green test run still felt like a small win. That’s what he means by recovering your life. It can start this afternoon, with the next pull request you open.
