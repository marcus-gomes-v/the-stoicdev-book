## January 21st - Crafting Your Dawn Reflections

***“As soon as you get up in the morning bethink you,
‘What do I yet lack in order to achieve tranquillity?
What to achieve calm?
What am I? I am not a paltry body, not property, not reputation, am I? None of these.
Well, what am I? A rational creature.’
What, then, are the demands upon you? Rehearse your actions.
‘Where did I go wrong?’ in matters conducive to serenity?
‘What did I do’ that was unfriendly, or unsocial, or unfeeling?
‘What to be done was left undone’ in regard to these matters?”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 4.6.34-35

Most developers start the day by reacting. You open the laptop, and before you’ve decided anything, the inbox, the alerts and the overnight Slack threads have decided for you.

Epictetus suggests a few minutes of something else first. His questions sound old-fashioned, but they translate well. What would help me stay calm today? What am I, apart from my title and my contribution graph? Then comes the review, borrowed from the old Pythagorean verses: where did I go wrong yesterday, what did I do that was unkind or careless, and what did I leave undone?

You don’t need a journaling app or an hour of silence for this. Five minutes with a coffee, before the laptop opens, is enough. Answer honestly, and the day starts from your side instead of everyone else’s.
