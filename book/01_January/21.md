## January 21st - Crafting Your Dawn Reflections

***“Ask yourself the following first thing in the morning:***
What am I lacking in attaining freedom from passion?
What for tranquility?
What am I? A mere body, estate-holder, or reputation? None of these things.What, then? A rational being.
What then is demanded of me? Meditate on your actions.
How did I steer away from serenity?
What did I do that was unfriendly, unsocial, or uncaring?
What did I fail to do in all these things?”
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 4.6.34-35

Embracing a morning ritual is a common thread among those who navigate life with purpose and clarity. Whether through meditation, exercise, or journaling, the essence lies in dedicating time for introspection. Stoics, like Marcus Aurelius with his Meditations, exemplify the profound impact of such practices, meticulously examining their thoughts, actions, and the essence of their being away from the public eye.

Initiate your day with Epictetus's reflective prompts, allowing them to illuminate your path toward self-improvement. This isn't just about routine; it's an opportunity to align your daily actions with the core of who you are a rational being striving for tranquility and virtue. Let this daily contemplation be your guide, shaping a life rich with purpose and serenity, one morning at a time.
