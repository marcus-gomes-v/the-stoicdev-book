## January 22nd - Reflecting to Forge Ahead

***“I will keep constant watch over myself and—most usefully—will put each day up for review. For this is what makes us evil—that none of us looks back upon our own lives. We reflect upon only that which we are about to do. And yet our plans for the future descend from the past.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 83.2

In his guidance to Novatus, Seneca reveals a powerful ritual of self-examination, a practice that serves as a bridge between past actions and future intentions. Each night, posing reflective questions about our habits, justice in our actions, and areas for personal growth turns introspection into a tool for self-improvement. This practice echoes through the ages in Marcus Aurelius's Meditations, a personal document of Stoic exercises aimed at self-betterment.

Embrace this ritual in your own life. Keep a journal digital or traditional to ponder daily on your actions, thoughts, and the lessons learned. Be honest in your self-assessment, identifying both strengths and shortcomings. This isn’t just about accountability; it’s a proactive step towards evolving your character and enhancing your life’s journey. Through regular reflection, you not only keep track of your progress but also set a foundation for meaningful change, one day at a time.
