January 24th - Cultivating Depth in Understanding

“From Rusticus . . . I learned to read carefully and not be satisfied with a rough understanding of the whole, and not to agree too quickly with those who have a lot to say about something.” 
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 1.7.3

Marcus Aurelius begins his Meditations with heartfelt appreciation for those who shaped his journey towards wisdom, notably Quintus Junius Rusticus. Rusticus instilled in Marcus a passion for thorough understanding and critical engagement, far beyond superficial learning. This pursuit of depth led Marcus to the teachings of Epictetus, whose lectures he studied not merely to acquaint but to fully integrate into his life and leadership.

This meticulous approach to learning is vital for developers as well. In a field rapidly evolving and expanding, the temptation to skim the surface is strong. Yet, true mastery and innovation lie in deep comprehension and critical analysis, not in swift consumption. Let’s embrace this method of slow, deliberate study, allowing the wisdom of each line of code, each new technology, to profoundly transform our approach to problem-solving and creativity. Like Marcus, let us seek not just to learn but to embody the lessons that resonate, shaping our professional ethos and personal philosophy.
