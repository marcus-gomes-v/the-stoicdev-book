## January 24th - Cultivating Depth in Understanding

***“From Rusticus... I learned... to read carefully, and not to be satisfied with a superficial understanding of a book; nor hastily to give my assent to those who talk overmuch.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 1.7

Rusticus was one of Marcus’s teachers, a Stoic whose advice he relied on for years, and the same passage says Rusticus lent him his own copy of the discourses of Epictetus. Marcus didn’t skim them. You can still hear Epictetus all through the Meditations.

Developers skim for a living. We skim docs for the one code sample that compiles, skim pull requests for anything obviously wrong, and skim a forty-message thread before replying to the last message. Most days that’s fine. But when something really matters, skimming is how you end up confidently wrong.

So slow down where it counts. Take the error message you’ve been pasting into a search box and read all of it, including the part below the stack trace. Read the RFC before you argue about the protocol. Read the design doc to the end before you agree with whoever talks most in the meeting. Rusticus warned Marcus about that too. The person who has said the most is not always the person who is right.
