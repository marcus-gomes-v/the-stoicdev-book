## January 28th - Emulating the Virtuous

***“Examine men’s ruling principles, even those of the wise, what kind of things they avoid, and what kind they pursue.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 4.38

Seneca once wrote to a friend that you can’t straighten something crooked without a ruler. Marcus comes at the same idea from another side. If you want to know how to live, look at what wise people actually run toward and what they run away from. Not their quotes. Their choices.

On most teams you can find one or two people worth watching this way. Notice what they avoid. Maybe it’s gossip about other teams, or promising dates they haven’t checked, or merging on a Friday afternoon. Then notice what they go after: the boring root cause instead of the quick patch, or the junior’s question even on a busy day.

You don’t have to admire everything about them, and you shouldn’t copy their habits blindly. A brilliant debugger can still be terrible at estimates. The point is to see principles at work, close enough to learn from them.

Then turn the question around. If someone had been watching you this week, what would they say you avoid, and what would they say you chase?
