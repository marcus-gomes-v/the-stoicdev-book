## January 28th - Emulating the Virtuous

***“Take a good hard look at people’s ruling principle, especially of the wise, what they run away from and what they seek out.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 4.38

In the journey of personal and professional growth, identifying and observing the wise those whose principles and actions we admire can be transformative. Seneca's insight, “Without a ruler to do it against, you can’t make crooked straight,” underscores the importance of having role models. In the tech world, this could be a pioneering developer, a visionary leader, or even a colleague whose work ethic and problem-solving skills inspire you.

Choose your model wisely. It might be someone from your immediate environment or a historical figure whose values resonate with yours. Reflect on their decisions, what drives them, and how they navigate challenges. This isn't about imitation for its own sake but about learning from their example to refine your own principles and actions.

As you progress in your career, remember that observing the wise is about more than just admiration it's about actively integrating their virtues into your life and work, striving to embody the qualities that make them exemplary.
