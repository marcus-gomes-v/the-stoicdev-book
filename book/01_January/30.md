## January 30th - Embracing Selective Ignorance

***“If you wish to improve, be content to appear clueless or stupid in extraneous matters—don’t wish to seem knowledgeable. And if some regard you as important, distrust yourself.”***
—EPICTETUS, ENCHIRIDION, 13a

In a world where being constantly updated is seen as a virtue, there's profound strength in admitting, "I don’t know," or even, "I don’t care." The pressure to stay informed about every news piece, to binge-watch every acclaimed series, and to always have an opinion, can be overwhelming and, frankly, unnecessary. This perpetual race for information not only dilutes our focus but also our essence, distracting us from what truly enriches our lives and careers.

For developers, where deep work and concentration are key, the wisdom of choosing what to ignore can be liberating. Imagine the peace, focus, and creativity you could reclaim by freeing yourself from the compulsion to keep up with everything. Today, grant yourself the permission to prioritize your mental space and energy. Focus on what genuinely matters to your personal growth and professional expertise. Let selective ignorance be your tool for deeper understanding and innovation in the areas that truly captivate you.
