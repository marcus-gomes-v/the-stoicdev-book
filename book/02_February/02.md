## February 2nd - Mastering Emotional Independence

***“Consider thus: Thou art an old man; no longer let this be a slave, no longer be pulled by the strings like a puppet to unsocial movements, no longer be either dissatisfied with thy present lot, or shrink from the future.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 2.2

Developers love their autonomy. We push back on micromanagement, we want to pick our own tools, we bristle when someone tells us how to structure a module. And then a single comment on a pull request ruins the afternoon. A green build makes us cheerful, a red one makes us short with everyone in the channel.

For people so proud of their independence, we are remarkably easy to steer.

Marcus wrote this note to himself, an emperor telling himself he was old enough to stop being yanked around. The strings he meant are inside: the irritation, the sulking about the current project, the dread about next quarter’s reorg. Nobody else is pulling them.

You don’t have to feel nothing. You just have to notice the tug before you act on it. Read the review comment twice. Let the red build be a red build. Then decide what you’ll do, the way you’d decide anything else in your work.
