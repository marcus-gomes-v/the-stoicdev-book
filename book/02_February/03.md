February 3rd - Unraveling the Roots of Anxiety

“When I see an anxious person, I ask myself, what do they want? For if a person wasn’t wanting something outside of their own control, why would they be stricken by anxiety?” 
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 2.13.1

Anxiety often visits us in the guise of concern for things beyond our grasp—the security of loved ones, the unpredictability of travel, the fluctuations of the market. Epictetus points out the common thread: a desire for control over the uncontrollable. This futile yearning not only undermines our serenity but also reveals our vulnerability to circumstances we cannot command.

In the realm of software development, where variables and outcomes frequently exceed our control, recognizing the source of our anxiety is the first step toward regaining composure. Instead of succumbing to worry over a delayed project or a challenging code review, let's question the utility of our anxiety. Does it aid in resolution, or does it merely amplify the turmoil?

Today, confront your anxieties with Epictetus's wisdom. Identify what's truly within your control and what is not. By understanding the roots of your anxiety, you can shift focus from fruitless worry to actionable, constructive efforts, harnessing your energy where it can truly make a difference.
