## February 5th - Balancing Impulses with Wisdom

***“Do not be whirled about, but in every movement have respect to justice, and on the occasion of every impression maintain the faculty of comprehension [or understanding].”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 4.22

Some weeks the whole team spins. Monday it’s a rewrite in a new language because someone read a blog post. Wednesday it’s a panic about a competitor’s launch. Friday everyone wants to drop the roadmap and chase one loud customer. You can feel yourself getting pulled along, agreeing with whoever spoke last.

Marcus gives two checks for that. First, for every impulse to act, ask whether it is fair: to the users, to the teammates who will maintain this, to the people you promised something last month. Second, when an impression hits you (the scary metric, the angry email), hold on to your understanding of what is actually going on before you react.

Neither check takes long. A minute before you reply-all. A night’s sleep before you propose the rewrite. Most impulses survive that minute just fine, and you can follow them with a clear head. The ones that don’t were never worth following.
