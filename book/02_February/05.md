February 5th - Balancing Impulses with Wisdom

“Don’t be bounced around, but submit every impulse to the claims of justice, and protect your clear conviction in every appearance.” 
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 4.22

In the dynamic and often unpredictable world of software development, impulses can lead us astray as much as they can drive us forward. Marcus Aurelius advises a judicious approach: not to be swayed by every fleeting urge, but to weigh our actions against the scales of justice and reason. This disciplined scrutiny ensures that our decisions align with our core values and principles, rather than the whims of the moment.

The highs and lows experienced by those around us serve as a reminder of the importance of stability. By adopting justice, reason, and philosophy as our guides, we can sift through our impulses, identifying which to follow and which to temper. Before taking action, pause to consider who's in command—your transient emotions or your enduring principles. This reflective pause not only steadies your course but also ensures that your professional and personal actions are rooted in thoughtful deliberation.
