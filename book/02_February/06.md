## February 6th - Choosing Peace Over Conflict

***“I part ways with those who head out into the middle of the waves and, approving of a stormy life, wrestle bravely every day with the hardships of their affairs. The wise man will bear such things, not choose them, and he will prefer to be at peace rather than in battle.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 28.7

Every company has someone who seems to need a crisis. They volunteer for every fire, they’re in every war room, and when things go quiet they get restless and start one. Some of us admire that person. Some of us are that person.

Seneca doesn’t admire it. He says the wise man will put up with a stormy life when it comes, but he won’t go looking for it. He would rather be at peace than in a fight.

That’s worth thinking about the next time you reach for the most chaotic project on the board. Is it the work itself that pulls you, or the adrenaline, the visibility, the feeling of being needed? Some fights do need you. A production outage at 2 a.m. is not optional. But a career built on permanent emergency usually means the boring, quiet work of preventing emergencies is getting skipped. Sometimes it also means you’re a little afraid of what you’d notice if things went calm.
