## February 6th - Choosing Peace Over Conflict

***“I don’t agree with those who plunge headlong into the middle of the flood and who, accepting a turbulent life, struggle daily in great spirit with difficult circumstances. The wise person will endure that, but won’t choose it—choosing to be at peace, rather than at war.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 28.7

The valor of being in the thick of action, as extolled in Theodore Roosevelt's "Man in the Arena" speech, has its allure. Yet, Seneca and the Stoics prompt us to question our motivations for seeking such engagement. Is it a pursuit of purpose and meaning, or an escape from stillness and reflection?

In the tech industry, the drive to constantly innovate, compete, and outdo can mirror Roosevelt's relentless quest for action. However, true wisdom lies in discerning when to engage and when to seek serenity. Not all battles are worth fighting, nor is constant strife the path to fulfillment.

Today, let's reflect on our choices and motivations. Are we choosing challenges that align with our values and contribute to our growth, or are we simply afraid of what silence might reveal? Embracing peace and thoughtful action can lead to a more meaningful and centered existence, both personally and professionally.
