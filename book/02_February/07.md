## February 7th - Breaking the Cycle of Fear

***“To many their very fear is bane; for many have come upon their doom while shunning doom.”***
—SENECA, OEDIPUS, 992

A team gets burned by one bad release. So they add a second approval step. Then a change freeze before every holiday. Then a rule that nothing ships on Fridays, then nothing on Thursdays either. A year later releases are so big and so rare that every one of them is risky, and the outage they were so afraid of arrives anyway, bigger than the first.

Seneca knew how fear does this. He spent years at Nero’s court, close enough to see what suspicion does to a man with power. In this play his chorus says it plainly: many have walked into their doom while running from it.

Fear has its uses. It tells you where to look. The trouble starts when it makes the decisions. Before you add the next safeguard, ask whether it lowers the risk or only lowers your anxiety for a week. Sometimes the safer move is the one that feels scarier: ship smaller, ship more often, and let yourself find out what actually breaks.
