## February 9th - The Freedom of Neutrality

***“It is in our power to have no opinion about a thing, and not to be disturbed in our soul; for things themselves have no natural power to form our judgments.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 6.52

Open any developer forum and there’s a war going on. Tabs against spaces. Monolith against microservices. Whichever framework came out this month against whichever one came out last month. At work it’s the same in miniature: the new office policy, a rumor about a reorg, a thread with eighty replies about naming conventions.

You’re allowed to sit it out. Marcus points to something we forget: the thing itself can’t force an opinion on you. You form the opinion, and you can decline to form it.

This isn’t ignorance or indifference. You can read the thread, understand both sides, and still decide it doesn’t need your judgment today. Save your opinions for the decisions you’ll actually have to make and live with. The rest can go by without taking a piece of your attention, and the week gets a lot quieter.
