## February 10th - The Toxicity of Anger

***“There is no passion that is more frantic, more destructive to its own self; it is arrogant if successful, and frantic if it fails. Even when defeated it does not grow weary, but if chance places its foe beyond its reach, it turns its teeth against itself.”***
—SENECA, ON ANGER, 3.1.5

Some developers run on spite. The manager said it couldn’t be done, so they did it in a weekend to prove a point. The other team mocked their design, so they rewrote it out of pride. It works for a while, and the stories get told at the bar.

Seneca’s description of anger is worth reading slowly. Arrogant when it wins, frantic when it loses, never tired even in defeat. And when it runs out of enemies, it turns its teeth on itself. Anyone who has kept a grudge alive through two reorgs knows what he means. The person who wronged you left the company a year ago. You’re still angry. Now the anger has nowhere to go but into you.

Anger burns hot and it burns the person carrying it. Look for fuel that lasts longer: the pleasure of a clean solution, the users who’ll have a better day, your own standards. They won’t feel as dramatic. They’ll still be there in five years.
