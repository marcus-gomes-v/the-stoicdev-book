## February 12th - Safeguarding Your Inner Sanctuary

***“Keep constant guard over your perceptions, for it is no small thing you are protecting, but your respect, trustworthiness and steadiness, peace of mind, freedom from pain and fear, in a word your freedom. For what would you sell these things?”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 4.3.6b-8

In the relentless pace of the tech world, it's easy to be caught in a cycle of stress, reacting to each challenge with a toll on our mental and emotional well-being. Stoicism offers tools to navigate these storms with composure, yet Epictetus prompts a deeper reflection: Why immerse ourselves in environments that perpetually test our limits?

Consider the cost of constant agitation be it from a high-stress job, toxic relationships, or the ceaseless demands of public life. Our capacity to endure is not infinite; like our adrenal glands, our resilience has its limits. Stoicism teaches us not only to endure but also to question and choose our battles wisely.

As you employ Stoicism to find tranquility amidst chaos, also ponder the life you're choosing each day. Is it one that aligns with your true values and aspirations? Remember, every moment of frustration is a moment of life expended. Choose how and where you invest your most precious resource. If necessary, be bold enough to make changes that realign with your peace of mind and freedom.
