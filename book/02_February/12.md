## February 12th - Safeguarding Your Inner Sanctuary

***“Pay attention, therefore, to your sense-impressions, and watch over them sleeplessly. For it is no small matter that you are guarding, but self-respect, and fidelity, and constancy, a state of mind undisturbed by passion, pain, fear, or confusion—in a word, freedom. What are the things for which you are about to sell these things?”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 4.3.6-8

Epictetus talks about your peace of mind as if it had a price tag. Self-respect, keeping your word, steadiness, a mind that isn’t thrown around by fear and confusion: he puts all of it under one word, freedom, and then asks what you’re selling it for.

It’s a good question to put to your job. Maybe it’s a team where every week is a fire drill and every message is marked urgent. Maybe it’s a manager who praises you in private and blames you in public. Maybe it’s you, saying yes to every request because saying no feels dangerous. You can use Stoic tools to stay calm in all of these, and you should. But endurance has limits, and every tense standup costs you a little.

So look at the trade honestly: what are you getting in exchange? A salary and a title, maybe the promise of a promotion next cycle. Sometimes the trade is worth it for a while. Sometimes it stopped being worth it a year ago and you haven’t looked. Guard your impressions day to day, as he says. And now and then, step back and check whether the whole arrangement is one you would still choose.
