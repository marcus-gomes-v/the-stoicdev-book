## February 14th - Harnessing Rational Thought

***“For to be wise is only one thing—to fix our attention on our intelligence, which guides all things everywhere.”***
—HERACLITUS, QUOTED IN DIOGENES LAERTIUS, LIVES OF THE EMINENT PHILOSOPHERS, 9.1

Regret often follows in the wake of actions taken without forethought, leading us to question, "Why did I do that?" The answer usually lies in the oversight of our most powerful tool: our reason. Within us, we possess the capacity for wisdom and discernment, yet the challenge lies in ensuring these faculties govern our decisions. It's about ensuring that our intellect, rather than fleeting emotions or immediate desires, steers our course.

For developers, this principle is crucial. The complexity of coding and the pressures of project timelines demand a disciplined mind. Before acting, pause to consult your inner guide. Allow your intelligence, your reasoned understanding of the task at hand, to lead. This conscious alignment with rational thought not only minimizes regret but also enhances the quality of our work and our lives. Let today be a reminder to let your intelligence do its thing.
