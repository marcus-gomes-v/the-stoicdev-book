## February 25th - The Futility of Anger and Ambition

***“Constantly bring to thy recollection those who have complained greatly about anything, those who have been most conspicuous by the greatest fame or misfortunes or enmities or fortunes of any kind: then think where are they all now? Smoke and ash and a tale, or not even a tale.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 12.27

Think of the people in our industry who were furious about something ten years ago. The flame wars on mailing lists, the bitter feuds over which language would win, the founders who were going to crush their rivals. Think of the famous ones too, the products everyone used and the companies everyone feared.

Where are they now? Mostly smoke and ash, as Marcus says, and a story. Often not even that.

He kept this exercise for himself as a cure for taking things too seriously. Your current fight, with the other team, with the architect who won’t approve your design, with the colleague who got the credit, will look the same in ten years. You won’t remember most of it. What will matter is whether you did your work well and treated people decently while you were at it.
