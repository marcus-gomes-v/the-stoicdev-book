## February 26th - Focus on What Matters

***“Another has done me wrong? Let him see to it. He has his own tendencies, and his own affairs. What I have now is what the common nature has willed, and what I endeavor to accomplish now is what my nature wills.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 5.25

Marcus Aurelius and Abraham Lincoln both understood the futility of immediate retaliation to perceived wrongs. The stoic approach, as practiced by these leaders, involves recognizing that others' actions and reactions are their own responsibility. Our focus should remain on our actions, aligned with our principles and the greater good. This lesson is crucial for maintaining professional integrity and personal peace, reminding us to prioritize constructive responses over fleeting emotions.
