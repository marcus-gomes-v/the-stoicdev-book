## February 26th - Focus on What Matters

***“Does another do me wrong? Let him look to it. He has his own disposition, his own activity. I now have what the universal nature now wills me to have; and I do what my nature now wills me to do.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 5.25

A teammate presents your design at the all-hands as if it were theirs. Your first instinct is to correct them in front of everyone, or to stew about it for a week.

Marcus’s answer is short: let him look to it. Their behavior belongs to them. Your job is the work your own nature calls for right now. You can still raise it later, calmly and in private. The retaliation can wait forever.
