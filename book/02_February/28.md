February 28th - Regaining Equilibrium Amidst Turmoil

"The soul is like a bowl of water, and our impressions are like the ray of light falling upon the water. When the water is troubled, it appears that the light itself is moved too, but it isn’t. So, when a person loses their composure it isn’t their skills and virtues that are troubled, but the spirit in which they exist, and when that spirit calms down so do those things." 
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 3.3.20-22

In the realm of development, where pressures can mount and the pace is relentless, it's not uncommon to feel out of sorts—to experience days when you're not quite yourself, when the calm waters of your soul seem to churn and roil. Epictetus's analogy reminds us that these moments of turbulence don't reflect a loss of our abilities or virtues but a disturbance in our inner state. Our true essence remains intact, untouched by the storm.

This realization is liberating. It means that amidst the chaos, by seeking tranquility within, we can realign with our core principles and regain our sense of self. The turbulence is temporary; our character and skills remain. Next time you find yourself caught in a tempest of doubt or confusion, remember to pause, breathe, and allow the waters to settle. Your composure, your reasoned choice, is always within reach, waiting for you to reclaim it and continue your journey with renewed clarity and purpose.
