## March 1st - Igniting the Flame of Self-Awareness

***“This, then, is a starting point in philosophy—a perception of the state of one’s own governing principle.”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 1.26.15

Most of us start something new by reaching for the biggest version of it. A course with forty hours of video. A rewrite of the whole service. A reading list that would take a decade to get through.

Epictetus points somewhere smaller: the state of your own mind. Not your skills on paper, but how your judgment actually behaves right now. Whether you approve pull requests because you read them or because the author is senior. Whether one bad standup quietly ruins the whole afternoon.

In the same passage he mocks students who can’t swallow a single mouthful and so buy an entire treatise to eat. You’ve seen the developer version. Someone skips the basics and goes straight to building a distributed system. Someone else takes a lead role without ever noticing how badly they handle disagreement. Both choke on it, and both are surprised.

So start with the honest check. Watch how your reasoning holds up under small pressure, like a terse review comment or a plan that changes at the last minute. Once you see where it’s weak, you stop handing it the big decisions before it’s ready, and you finally know what to work on first.
