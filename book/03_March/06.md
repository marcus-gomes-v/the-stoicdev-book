## March 6th - Resisting the Lure of Personal Narratives

***"In public avoid talking often and excessively about your accomplishments and dangers, for however much you enjoy recounting your dangers, it’s not so pleasant for others to hear about your affairs."***
—EPICTETUS, ENCHIRIDION, 33.14

The allure of shaping our experiences into compelling narratives is a common human trait. However, as Epictetus cautions, indulging in this tendency, especially when it centers around self-promotion or recounting past achievements, can alienate others and detract from genuine connection. This insight aligns with modern understandings of the "narrative fallacy," highlighting the dangers of imposing false coherence on the chaos of our pasts a practice that not only misleads but can also isolate us from others.

In the context of professional environments, especially within collaborative fields like technology, the impulse to weave personal success stories can create barriers. It shifts focus from collective achievement and learning to individual ego, undermining team spirit and mutual respect.

Today, challenge yourself to step back from crafting and recounting these personal sagas. Instead, focus on the present moment and the people you're with. Listen more than you speak. Share the stage of conversation and foster an environment where connections are built on authentic interactions rather than performances. This shift not only enhances your relationships but also keeps you grounded in the reality of now, where true growth and understanding occur.
