March 8th - Guarding the Fortress of Your Mind

"If a person gave away your body to some passerby, you’d be furious. Yet you hand over your mind to anyone who comes along, so they may abuse you, leaving it disturbed and troubled—have you no shame in that?" 
—EPICTETUS, ENCHIRIDION, 28

Our society is inundated with distractions and demands on our attention, from the constant pings of social media notifications to the allure of endless entertainment options. Epictetus's admonition serves as a stark reminder of the value of mental autonomy. While we vigilantly defend our physical space, we often leave the gates of our minds unguarded, allowing external influences to dictate our thoughts, feelings, and, ultimately, our sense of peace.

For those in the tech industry, this warning is especially pertinent. The very tools and platforms we develop or work with can also become conduits for distraction and disquiet. Recognizing this, we must make a conscious effort to protect our mental space. This involves setting boundaries around technology use, being mindful of the content we consume, and cultivating moments of quiet reflection.

By reclaiming control over our minds, we not only safeguard our well-being but also enhance our capacity for deep work and creativity. Let's treat our mental space with the same respect and protection as our physical selves, recognizing it as the foundation of our freedom, productivity, and inner peace.
