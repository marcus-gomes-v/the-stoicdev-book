## March 9th - Curating Your Circle for Growth

***"Above all, keep a close watch on this—that you are never so tied to your former acquaintances and friends that you are pulled down to their level. If you don’t, you’ll be ruined. . . . You must choose whether to be loved by these friends and remain the same person, or to become a better person at the cost of those friends . . . if you try to have it both ways you will neither make progress nor keep what you once had."***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 4.2.1; 4-5

The company we keep shapes us more profoundly than we often acknowledge. Echoing through history, from Epictetus and Musonius Rufus to contemporary thinkers like Jim Rohn and James Altucher, is the wisdom that our closest associates influence our paths towards either growth or stagnation. This holds especially true in the tech industry, where innovation, creativity, and perseverance are paramount.

As we navigate our careers and personal lives, it's crucial to assess the impact of our social circles. Do they inspire us to reach new heights, or do they anchor us to past versions of ourselves? Cultivating a supportive, ambitious, and positively challenging environment is key to unlocking our fullest potential.

Today, take a moment to reflect on your relationships. Consider aligning yourself with those who not only share your aspirations but also push you to exceed them. It's not about elitism but about choosing a path that fosters development and fulfillment. Remember, the scenes we choose and the company we keep can propel us toward becoming the best versions of ourselves.
