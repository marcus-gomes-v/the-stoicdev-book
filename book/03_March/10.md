## March 10th - The Guiding Light of Exemplary Figures

***"We can remove most sins if we have a witness standing by as we are about to go wrong. The soul should have someone it can respect, by whose example it can make its inner sanctum more inviolable. Happy is the person who can improve others, not only when present, but even when in their thoughts!"***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 11.9

In the vast arena of technology and development, where ethical dilemmas and personal challenges frequently arise, the wisdom of Seneca beckons us to find our moral compass in the form of a mentor or an exemplar, akin to Cato the Younger. Cato's legacy, characterized by steadfast principles and a refusal to compromise on his values, serves as a beacon for contemporary professionals navigating the complexities of the tech industry.

Having a "Cato" in our lives be it a historical figure, a contemporary mentor, or even a construct of ideal characteristics provides us with a reference point for integrity, excellence, and resilience. This mental guide acts as a silent observer, encouraging us to reflect on our actions and choices, steering us towards the higher ground.

As we strive to make impactful contributions to our field, let us also aspire to embody the virtues that could make us someone else's "Cato." By living a life worthy of emulation, we not only elevate our own standards but potentially inspire and uplift those around us, fostering a community of integrity and innovation.
