March 11th - The True Currency of Freedom

"The unrestricted person, who has in hand what they will in all events, is free. But anyone who can be restricted, coerced, or pushed into something against what they will is a slave." 
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 4.1.128b-129a

In the pursuit of success, especially within the ever-evolving tech industry, it's easy to lose sight of what truly constitutes freedom. The allure of wealth, influence, and recognition can ensnare us, dictating our actions and choices. Yet, as Epictetus reminds us, true freedom is not found in external achievements but in the ability to live without restriction, according to our values and principles.

This notion challenges us to reconsider what we sacrifice in the name of success. Are we trading our autonomy for a gilded cage? Are we conforming to expectations at the expense of our authenticity? The most valuable currency we possess is not fame or financial wealth but the liberty to make choices that align with our deepest convictions.

As developers and professionals in the digital realm, where opportunities and pressures abound, let us not forget the importance of scrutinizing the cost of success. By valuing freedom above all, we ensure that our achievements are not just markers of external success but also affirmations of our commitment to living unrestricted, fulfilling lives.
