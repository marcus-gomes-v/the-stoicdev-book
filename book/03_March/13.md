March 13th - Trusting the Larger Design

"Whenever you find yourself blaming providence, turn it around in your mind and you will see that what has happened is in keeping with reason." 
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 3.17.1

It's a common human trait to focus intently on our immediate plans and desires, so much so that any deviation can feel like a cosmic misstep against us. Yet, as Epictetus reminds us, there's often a broader logic at play, one that transcends our immediate understanding and individual circumstances.

This perspective is especially pertinent in the realm of technology and development, where the pace of change and the complexity of systems mean that setbacks and surprises are all but guaranteed. What seems like a project failure or a missed opportunity can, with time, reveal itself as a crucial learning moment or the catalyst for a new direction we never considered.

Adopting a mindset that is open to the "bigger plan" requires patience and a degree of humility. It means acknowledging that our view is limited and that the unfolding of events might serve a greater logic or purpose. Today, let's practice looking beyond the immediate impact of the unexpected. By trusting that there's reason in the unforeseen, we open ourselves to the possibility that, in hindsight, everything aligns more harmonically than we could have initially imagined. This shift not only helps us navigate the vicissitudes of life with more grace but also empowers us to find meaning and opportunity in the midst of uncertainty.
