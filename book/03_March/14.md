March 14th - The Barrier of Ego

"Zeno would also say that nothing is more hostile to a firm grasp on knowledge than self-deception." 
—DIOGENES LAERTIUS, LIVES OF THE EMINENT PHILOSOPHERS, 7.23

In the quest for personal and professional development, particularly within the tech industry, self-deception stands as a formidable adversary. This subtle foe masquerades as confidence, yet its essence is ego—a barrier to the humility required for genuine learning and growth.

Epictetus's insight that one cannot learn what they believe they already know illuminates the peril of ego. It locks the door to new knowledge, innovation, and improvement, asserting that we have reached the pinnacle of our capabilities. This mindset not only hinders our advancement but also alienates us from the collaborative, ever-evolving nature of technology.

Today, let us confront our ego with the recognition of its deceit. By acknowledging our continuous need for learning and the infinite scope of what remains unknown, we can dismantle the barriers ego erects. In doing so, we open ourselves to endless possibilities for growth, collaboration, and innovation, ensuring our journey is one of perpetual advancement and shared success.
