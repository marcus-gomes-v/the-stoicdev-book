## March 15th - Embracing the Gift of Now

***“Though thou shouldest be going to live three thousand years and as many times ten thousand years, still remember that no man loses any other life than this which he now lives, nor lives any other than this which he now loses. The longest and shortest are thus brought to the same. For the present is the same to all... For a man cannot lose either the past or the future: for what a man has not, how can any one take this from him?”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 2.14

There’s always a “once.” Once the migration is done, things will calm down. Once the promotion comes through, you’ll finally have time to learn Rust. Once this launch is out, you’ll start taking weekends again. Then the migration ends, the next one begins, and the “once” slides forward another quarter.

Marcus has a plain answer for this. Live three thousand years or thirty thousand, and the only life you can lose is the one you are living right now. The past is already gone and the future isn’t yours yet, and nobody can take from you what you don’t have. So the long life and the short one come down to the same thing, this present moment, which is the same size for everyone.

Plan all you like. Just don’t postpone your life until the roadmap is finished. The pairing session this afternoon and the dinner with your family tonight are both happening in the only time you actually have.
