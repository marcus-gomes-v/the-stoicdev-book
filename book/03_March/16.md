## March 16th - Cherishing Your Inner Compass

***"Hold sacred your capacity for understanding. For it is all, that our ruling principle won’t allow anything to enter that is either inconsistent with nature or with the constitution of a logical creature. It’s what demands due diligence, care for others, and obedience to God."***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 3.9

In a world bustling with noise and distractions, the ability to think, reason, and understand stands as a sanctuary of tranquility and purpose. Marcus Aurelius reminds us of the profound sanctity of our inner reasoning power a tool that not only navigates us through life's tumultuous waters but also enables us to influence our environment and those within it positively.

As professionals in the tech industry, where logic and creativity are paramount, acknowledging and valuing our cognitive capabilities is crucial. It's these capabilities that allow us to solve complex problems, innovate, and make ethical decisions that affect millions. Today, let's take a moment to recognize this extraordinary ability within us. Let's commit to using our understanding not just for personal or professional advancement, but for the greater good, guided by diligence, empathy, and a deep respect for the interconnectedness of all things.

By honoring our capacity for understanding as the sacred part of ourselves, we not only amplify our potential for positive impact but also align with the deepest values of humanity and the essence of stoic philosophy.
