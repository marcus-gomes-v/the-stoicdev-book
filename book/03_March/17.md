## March 17th - Essence of True Beauty

***“You are not flesh, nor hair, but moral purpose; if you get that beautiful, then you will be beautiful.”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 3.1.39-40

A young student of rhetoric once came to see Epictetus with his hair elaborately dressed and the hair on his body plucked, done up to impress. Epictetus teased him at length, and then told him what actually makes a person beautiful. You are not your flesh or your hair. You are your moral purpose, the part of you that chooses. Get that right and you will be beautiful.

We have our own versions of the elaborate hairstyle. The contribution graph without a single gap. The perfectly themed terminal. The personal site with parallax scrolling and a row of neat technology badges. The job title you mention a little too early in every introduction.

None of these are bad, and some of them are fun. The trouble starts when they become what you think you are. Then a quiet month on GitHub feels like a crisis, and a layoff feels like losing yourself instead of losing a job.

What you are shows up in smaller places. Whether you tell the client the truth about the delay. Whether you take the blame in the postmortem when it really was yours. Those choices are the part of you nobody can lay off, and they are where the beauty Epictetus talked about either exists or doesn’t.
