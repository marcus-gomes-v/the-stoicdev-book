## March 18th - Mastering Internal Triggers

***“To-day I have got out of all trouble, or rather I have cast out all trouble, for it was not outside, but within and in my opinions.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 9.13

You wake up with a knot in your stomach. The architecture review is at ten, the principal engineer will be there, and you’ve been rehearsing their objections in the shower. By half past nine you’ve lived through that meeting four times, and each time it went worse.

Then the meeting happens. Two questions, one fair suggestion, done in twenty minutes.

Marcus wrote this line in a private notebook, and you can hear the relief in it. He didn’t escape his troubles. He threw them out, because he finally saw where they had been living: in his opinions, not in the events.

Most of the weight we carry into hard days works the same way. The deadline is real and so is the review, but the dread is something we build on top. You can’t always skip the meeting. You can notice when you are carrying it four times instead of once, and put the extra three down.
