## March 19th - The Power of Perspective

***“For here are the two principles that you ought to have ready at hand: Outside the sphere of the moral purpose there is nothing either good or bad; and, We ought not to lead events, but to follow them.”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 3.10.18

Launch day. At 9:40 the cloud provider posts a status update: degraded performance in your region. Your dashboards go yellow, then red. Nothing you wrote is broken, and nothing you can do will bring their data center back a minute sooner.

Epictetus gives his students two rules to keep within reach for moments like this. The first is that outside your own choices there is nothing good or bad. The outage is just an event. What is good or bad is how you respond: whether you keep people informed, or make a panicked change at 9:45 that you’ll spend a week untangling. The second is that we ought not to lead events but to follow them. You don’t get to decide the region is up. You get to decide what to do now that it isn’t.

He applies the same rules to people. “My brother ought not to have treated me so.” Maybe not, Epictetus says, but that is his business. Yours is how you behave toward him. Swap the brother for the colleague who blamed you in the incident channel and it still holds.

Following events still leaves you plenty to do. Post the status update, fail over what you can, and let the rest be what it is.
