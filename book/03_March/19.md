March 19th - The Power of Perspective

"For there are two rules to keep at the ready—that there is nothing good or bad outside my own reasoned choice, and that we shouldn’t try to lead events but to follow them."
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 3.10.18

The convergence of wisdom across cultures and epochs highlights a universal truth: the power of perspective shapes our experience of the world. Anthony de Mello, an Indian Jesuit priest with a diverse background, echoed a sentiment akin to Epictetus's teachings, underscoring that the roots of our irritation or displeasure lie not in external circumstances but within ourselves.

In the ever-evolving landscape of technology, where rapid changes and unforeseen challenges are par for the course, this timeless wisdom remains incredibly relevant. As developers and professionals in the tech industry, recognizing that our perception dictates our reality empowers us to approach problems with resilience and adaptability. By understanding that our reactions and interpretations hold the true power over our peace of mind, we can navigate the complexities of our work with a steadier hand and a clearer vision.

Today, let's embrace the wisdom that transcends time and culture, reminding ourselves that in the face of any situation, our reasoned choice determines our path forward. Let's choose to adapt, to learn, and to grow, following the flow of events with the confidence that comes from inner clarity and control.
