March 22nd - The Essence of True Education

“What is it then to be properly educated? It is learning to apply our natural preconceptions to the right things according to Nature, and beyond that to separate the things that lie within our power from those that don’t.” 
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 1.22.9-10a

Holding a degree doesn’t necessarily mean you’re truly educated, just as owning running shoes doesn’t make you a runner. True education extends beyond academic credentials and into the realm of practical wisdom—especially in the tech industry. It involves discerning what is within our control and what isn’t, and focusing our efforts accordingly.

For developers and tech professionals, this means understanding the scope of our projects, the limitations of our tools, and our personal capacity to influence outcomes. It's about aligning our actions with what can actually be changed or affected—whether it's optimizing code, managing team dynamics, or navigating career paths.

Today, challenge yourself to apply this principle. Reflect on your current projects: identify what aspects are within your control and focus your energy there. This disciplined approach not only streamlines your workflow but also deepens your true education—turning theoretical knowledge into applied wisdom, essential for any StoicDev striving for excellence in the chaotic world of technology.
