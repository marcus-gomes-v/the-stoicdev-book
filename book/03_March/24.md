March 24th - Living Philosophy Daily

“Eat like a human being, drink like a human being, dress up, marry, have children, get politically active—suffer abuse, bear with a headstrong brother, father, son, neighbor, or companion. Show us these things so we can see that you truly have learned from the philosophers.” 
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 3.21.5-6

Philosophy isn’t just a subject to study; it's a way to live. Epictetus challenges us to apply philosophical lessons to everyday activities and relationships. It's in the ordinary—eating, dressing, engaging in family and social life—that the true test of learning emerges. Philosophy becomes visible not through words but through action.

Each moment is an opportunity to practice these lessons, whether you're interacting with colleagues, choosing a leader, or handling personal challenges. Today, as you navigate the complexities of life, remember: philosophy is not just in the understanding, but in the doing. Every choice and action is a chance to demonstrate the depth of your philosophical understanding and its practical application in the real world.
