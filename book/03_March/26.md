## March 26th - Mastering the Mind's Guardians

***“How does the ruling faculty make use of itself? for all lies in this. But everything else, whether it is in the power of thy will or not, is only lifeless ashes and smoke.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 12.33

After an incident, a good team asks how the decision got made: who approved the change, what they could see at the time, what pressure they were under. The outage is over. The process that produced it is still running.

Marcus turns that question on himself and says everything depends on it. How is my ruling faculty using itself? Everything else, he says, whether it’s in your power or not, is ashes and smoke.

So run a small retro on your own thinking now and then. Take one decision from this week and look at how you made it. Notice whether you were rested or wired, and whether you were working from the facts or from wanting the director to like you. You’ll find bugs. The decision has already shipped, but the faculty that made it will be making the next one too.
