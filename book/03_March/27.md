## March 27th - Evaluating True Worth

***“Diogenes of Sinope said we sell things of great value for things of very little, and vice versa.”***
—DIOGENES LAERTIUS, LIVES OF THE EMINENT PHILOSOPHERS, 6.2.35b

In the realm of technology and leadership, the concept of value can often become distorted. A gadget’s price tag or a software’s market share might dictate its perceived value, but as leaders and developers, it’s crucial to discern the true utility and long-term benefits these tools bring.

Diogenes’ reflection on the true worth of things serves as a poignant reminder for those steering technological advancements. Are we investing in technology that genuinely enhances user experience and solves real problems, or are we swayed by flashy trends with minimal impact? This Stoic perspective encourages us to reassess priorities and invest in innovations that offer substantial, enduring value rather than transient appeal. This clarity in judgment is what defines the true worth of our projects and pursuits in the tech industry.
