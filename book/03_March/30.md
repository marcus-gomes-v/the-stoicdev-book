March 30th - The Guiding Force of Reason

“Hurry to your own ruling reason, to the reason of the Whole, and to your neighbor’s. To your own mind to make it just; to the mind of the Whole to remember your place in it; and to your neighbor’s mind to learn whether it’s ignorant or of sound knowledge—while recognizing it’s like yours.” 
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 9.22

In the fast-paced world of tech, where decisions can have wide-reaching implications, it's vital not to let impulse or external pressures dictate our actions. Marcus Aurelius urges us to align our actions with reason—our own, the universal, and that of our colleagues. This approach not only ensures justice and appropriateness in our personal actions but also reminds us of our connectedness in larger projects and initiatives.

Consider today how your decisions are formed. Are they the product of thoughtful deliberation or hasty reactions? Each choice we make in development, leadership, or collaboration should pass the test of reason to ensure it contributes positively to our goals and respects the contributions of others. This conscious alignment reduces regrets and enhances the effectiveness and harmony of our professional endeavors.
