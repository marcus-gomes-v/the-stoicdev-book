## March 31st - Cultivating Discipline

***“To seek what is impossible is madness: and it is impossible that the bad should not do something of this kind.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 5.17

Every security engineer learns it early: never trust user input. Most users mean no harm, but some do, and expecting all of them to behave is how you end up with an injection bug in the news.

Marcus says the same thing more bluntly. Seeking the impossible is madness, and it is impossible that bad people won’t do the kind of things bad people do. Being shocked every time is a choice, and a tiring one.

It reaches well beyond security. The vendor who has missed every deadline this year will probably miss the next one. The stakeholder who changes the requirements the day before launch will likely do it again. You can stay outraged, or you can plan around it, with a buffer in the schedule and a written sign-off.

That is a kind of discipline too. Stop wishing people were different, and build something that still holds up when they aren’t.
