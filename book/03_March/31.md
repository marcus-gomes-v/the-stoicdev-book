March 31st - Cultivating Discipline

“Chasing what can’t be done is madness. But the base person is unable to do anything else.” 
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 5.17

Consider a software that’s coded without standards or a developer who skips the planning phase—chaos is inevitable. Marcus Aurelius highlights the futility in chasing the unattainable without proper preparation and discipline. In tech, as in life, disciplined training and adherence to principles are essential.

Today, reflect on the systems and habits you've developed in your professional life. Are they fostering your growth and helping you achieve feasible goals, or are they unchecked impulses leading nowhere? Implementing rigorous, thoughtful practices in coding, project management, or team leadership can transform chaotic pursuits into structured achievements. Start today by setting clear, attainable objectives and training yourself to approach them with discipline. This is the foundation for any successful career in technology—and a well-lived life.
