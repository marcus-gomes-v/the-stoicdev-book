## April 1st - THE COLOR OF YOUR THOUGHTS

***“Such as are thy habitual thoughts, such also will be the character of thy mind; for the soul is dyed by the thoughts.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 5.16

Spend six months on a team where every incident turns into a hunt for someone to blame, and you start reading every Slack ping as an accusation. You open your own pull requests already braced for the worst comment. The thoughts you repeat stop feeling like thoughts. They become the color of the whole day.

Marcus uses the image of dye. Cloth takes on whatever it soaks in, and so does the mind. So pay attention to what yours sits in all day: the doom-scrolling while the build runs, the argument with a coworker you replay on the drive home. You can choose a better dye.
