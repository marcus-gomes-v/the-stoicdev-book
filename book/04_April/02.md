## April 2nd - BE WARY OF WHAT YOU LET IN

***“Mimi, war, astonishment, torpor, slavery, will daily wipe out those holy principles of thine. How many things without studying nature dost thou imagine, and how many dost thou neglect?”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 10.9

“Mimi” were the cheap farces of the Roman stage. Marcus puts them on the same list as war and slavery, as things that wear down a person’s principles a little more each day.

You have your own versions. The thread where two seniors have argued about state management for three weeks. The manager who makes every request sound like an emergency, until you can’t tell which one really is.

None of it takes your principles in one go. It works daily, while you aren’t looking. You wouldn’t leave a port open on a server just because nobody has attacked it yet. Mute the thread. Ask which of the five urgent things is actually urgent.
