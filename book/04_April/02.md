## April 2nd - BE WARY OF WHAT YOU LET IN

***“Drama, combat, terror, numbness, and subservience—every day these things wipe out your sacred principles, whenever your mind entertains them uncritically or lets them slip in.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, Meditations, 10.9

In tech, just as in life, the environment influences our mental and operational frameworks significantly. Surrounding yourself with negativity, conflict, or drama can lower standards and divert focus from critical tasks. For developers and tech leaders, this might mean managing how much time is spent on unproductive debates over tools versus shipping code, or resisting the culture of constant urgency that burns out teams. Just like you'd defend your network against security threats, defend your mind against external chaos that threatens your principles and focus. You control the gate; filter what influences you allow to shape your thoughts and actions.
