## April 3rd - DECEIVED AND DIVIDED

***“It is things that trick us, so learn to tell them apart. We cling to bad things in place of good ones. We long for the opposite of what we once longed for. Our prayers fight against our prayers, our plans against our plans.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 45.6

In January the team agreed: this year we pay down the tech debt. By March there are four new features in flight, the refactor ticket has slid to next sprint six times, and everyone is annoyed that the codebase is still a mess.

Nobody lied. Things fooled us, the way Seneca says they do. A new feature looks like progress and a refactor looks like a cost, so we grab the feature. Then the wish from one month fights the wish from the next.

Before you take on another goal, look hard at the ones you already have. If two of them can’t both win, decide which one loses, and say so in the planning meeting. A team at war with its own roadmap ships neither.
