April 4th - HUMILITY AMIDST SUCCESS

“Make sure you’re not made ‘Emperor,’ avoid that imperial stain. It can happen to you, so keep yourself simple, good, pure, saintly, plain, a friend of justice, god-fearing, gracious, affectionate, and strong for your proper work. Fight to remain the person that philosophy wished to make you. Revere the gods, and look after each other. Life is short—the fruit of this life is a good character and acts for the common good.” 
—MARCUS AURELIUS, Meditations, 6.30

In the tech industry, rapid success can be intoxicating and disorienting. Whether you're a developer who's just pushed a groundbreaking software, a startup founder whose idea has finally caught fire, or a leader stepping into a new role, it's vital to stay grounded. Marcus Aurelius, once the most powerful man on earth, continually reminded himself to stay true to his values despite his immense power. Let this be a lesson to maintain humility, uphold ethical standards, and remain committed to the common good, no matter how high you rise. Success should enhance your character, not compromise it.
