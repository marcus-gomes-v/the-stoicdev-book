## April 5th - ANALYZE BEFORE YOU ACT

***“To begin with, be not swept off your feet, I beseech you, by the vividness of the impression, but say, ‘Wait for me a little, O impression; allow me to see who you are, and what you are an impression of; allow me to put you to the test.’”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 2.18.24

The dashboard goes red. Error rate up four hundred percent. Within thirty seconds someone in the channel says it must be this morning’s deploy, and someone else is already typing the rollback command.

Maybe it is the deploy. Or maybe a third-party API is having a bad day, and rolling back takes your fix for last week’s bug with it. The impression arrives vivid and complete, with a story already attached. That is exactly when Epictetus wants you to say: wait a little, let me see what you are.

Open the logs. Check what actually changed. Separate what you know from what you’re guessing. It costs a couple of minutes, and those minutes are usually cheaper than the second incident caused by the fix for the first.
