## April 5th - ANALYZE BEFORE YOU ACT

***“First off, don’t let the force of the impression carry you away. Say to it, ‘hold up a bit and let me see who you are and where you are from—let me put you to the test’... .”***
—EPICTETUS, Discourses, 2.18.24

In the fast-paced world of technology, where decisions can have far-reaching implications, the ability to pause and critically assess a situation is invaluable. As leaders, developers, or entrepreneurs, we encounter numerous impressions and must make decisions quickly. However, it's crucial to temper speed with scrutiny. Question your first impressions: Are they based on facts or biases? What do you truly know about this issue? This disciplined approach helps avoid pitfalls of snap judgments and ensures decisions are well-founded. Remember, a moment of evaluation can prevent a multitude of problems. Embrace the wisdom of "trust, but verify" to navigate the complexities of professional and personal life effectively.
