## April 6th - EXPECTING THE UNEXPECTED: NAVIGATING PROFESSIONAL PITFALLS

***“Begin the morning by saying to thyself, I shall meet with the busybody, the ungrateful, arrogant, deceitful, envious, unsocial. All these things happen to them by reason of their ignorance of what is good and evil. But I who have seen the nature of the good that it is beautiful, and of the bad that it is ugly, and the nature of him who does wrong, that it is akin to me... I can neither be injured by any of them, for no one can fix on me what is ugly, nor can I be angry with my kinsman, nor hate him. For we are made for co-operation, like feet, like hands, like eyelids, like the rows of the upper and lower teeth.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 2.1

Some mornings you already know how the day will go. The client who changes the spec after sign-off. The teammate who answers every bug report with “works on my machine.” The person in the retro who takes credit for the fix you stayed late to ship.

Marcus didn’t pretend these people away. He listed them before breakfast. Then he reminded himself that they act this way out of ignorance, and that they are still his kin. You work with them the way feet work with hands. Staying angry at them only breaks the team you both belong to.
