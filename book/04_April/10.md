April 10th - DISPELLING ILLUSIONS IN TECH LEADERSHIP

“Tt isn’t events themselves that disturb people, but only their judgments about them.” 
—EPICTETUS, Enchiridion, 5

In the tech industry, where rapid change is the norm, leaders often face crises: server outages, security breaches, or project failures. It's not these events that unsettle teams, but the judgments and meanings we ascribe to them. A seasoned CTO or project manager knows the difference between observing and perceiving. The observing eye objectively assesses: "The server is down." The perceiving eye catastrophizes: "This will ruin us!" By training to see situations with clarity and devoid of added narrative, tech leaders can prevent unnecessary turmoil and lead their teams through challenges with a calm, focused approach. This discipline helps in making pragmatic decisions quickly, ensuring that responses are measured and effective, not reactive or fear-driven.
