## April 11th - CULTIVATING HUMILITY IN TECH LEADERSHIP

***“Throw out your conceited opinions, for it is impossible for a person to begin to learn what he thinks he already knows.”***
—EPICTETUS, Discourses, 2.17.1

In the ever-evolving field of technology, the moment a leader assumes they know everything is the moment they fall behind. Epictetus, with his rigorous approach to teaching, emphasizes the necessity of humility for true learning. This lesson is critical for tech leaders, developers, and innovators. Whether you’re a CTO grappling with emerging technologies, a developer learning new programming paradigms, or a manager adapting to shifting market dynamics, the ability to shed preconceived notions and openly embrace new information is what leads to genuine progress and innovation. The tech industry thrives on rapid adaptation and learning qualities that require an acknowledgment of one's own knowledge gaps. Approach every discussion, every project, every challenge with the mindset that there is something valuable to be learned, and you cultivate an environment where knowledge flows freely and creativity flourishes.
