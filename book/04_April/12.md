## April 12th - BEYOND THE GLITTER: TECH LEADERS' TEMPTATIONS

***“Atreus: Who would reject the flood of fortune’s gifts? Thyestes: Anyone who has experienced how easily they flow back.”***
—SENECA, Thyestes, 536

In tech, where the next big thing always seems just around the corner, it's easy to be lured by the glitter of seemingly fortuitous opportunities new projects, the latest technologies, or even venture funding offers. These are the modern "gifts of fortune" that leaders in the tech industry often find hard to reject. Seneca’s play Thyestes serves as a powerful allegory for this dilemma, illustrating how such enticing offers can sometimes lead to unforeseen consequences. For tech leaders, the lesson here isn’t to shun opportunity, but to approach each with cautious optimism. Just as Thyestes learned the hard way, not every opportunity that glitters holds gold some may disguise pitfalls that could derail one’s moral compass or strategic goals. It’s critical to evaluate whether these gifts align with long-term objectives and ethical standards. Like General Sherman’s refusal of the presidency, sometimes the strongest show of leadership is to say no, prioritizing long-term vision over short-term gain.
