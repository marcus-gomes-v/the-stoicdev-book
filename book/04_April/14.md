## April 14th - MASTERING LIFE'S ESSENTIALS

***“Believe me, it is better to understand your own mind than to understand the corn-market.”***
—SENECA, ON THE BREVITY OF LIFE, 18.3

You can tell anyone the p99 latency of every service you own. You know which queries are slow, which dashboards lie and how many story points the team burned last sprint. But ask yourself why you’ve been snapping at your partner for a month, or what you actually want from the next five years, and there’s a long pause.

Seneca wrote this to Paulinus, the official in charge of Rome’s grain supply. Paulinus did the job well and honestly, and Seneca didn’t tell him the work was worthless. He told him it was taking all of him, and that a mind like his was made for more than keeping track of grain.

Most of us monitor our systems far better than we monitor ourselves. Give yourself some of that attention. Notice when you’re tired, what drains you, what you keep postponing. Write it down if it helps, the way you’d write up an incident. You are the one system you can’t hand over to another team.
