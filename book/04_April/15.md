April 15th - THE INEVITABLE COSTS OF LIFE

“Nothing will ever befall me that I will receive with gloom or a bad disposition. I will pay my taxes gladly. Now, all the things which cause complaint or dread are like the taxes of life—things from which, my dear Lucilius, you should never hope for exemption or seek escape.” 
—SENECA, Moral Letters, 96.2

In the realm of tech, just as in all aspects of life, there are unavoidable costs. Like taxes, these are the prices we pay for the privileges and benefits we enjoy. For tech professionals, these might be the long hours spent debugging code, the stress of pushing a new product to market, or even the social sacrifices made for career advancement. Seneca reminds us to embrace these inevitable aspects not with resentment but with acceptance, as the natural dues for our chosen paths. Whether it’s the literal tax deducted from our paychecks or the metaphorical taxes of professional setbacks and personal challenges, each is a payment toward something greater. By recognizing and accepting these 'taxes', we can focus more on what we retain rather than what is taken, finding contentment and purpose in what remains. This perspective doesn’t just minimize frustration; it enhances our resilience, reminding us that these payments are not hindrances but parts of a greater investment in our lives and careers.
