April 17th - NO HARM, NO FOUL

“Do away with the opinion I am harmed, and the harm is cast away too. Do away with being harmed, and harm disappears.” 
—MARCUS AURELIUS, Meditations, 4.7

In the world of tech, miscommunications and misunderstandings are bound to happen, whether it’s in code reviews, team meetings, or client interactions. However, the power of perception and interpretation can significantly influence the outcomes of these interactions. A comment on your code might feel like a personal attack, but what if it was intended as constructive feedback?

Marcus Aurelius reminds us that harm is often a matter of perception. By altering our interpretation, we can cast away the perceived harm. When faced with potentially upsetting remarks or actions, pause and consider alternative interpretations. Is there a possibility that the intent wasn’t to harm, but to help or to communicate a different perspective?

In your professional life, especially in high-stakes environments, choosing to see comments and actions through a neutral or positive lens can reduce conflict and foster better collaboration. It’s about training your mind to default to constructive interpretations rather than negative ones. By doing so, you’ll find that many sources of frustration and hurt simply disappear, allowing for smoother, more productive interactions and a more resilient mindset.
