## April 20th - REAL GOOD IS SIMPLE

***“Here’s a way to think about what the masses regard as being ‘good’ things. If you would first start by setting your mind upon things that are unquestionably good—wisdom, self-control, justice, courage—with this preconception you’ll no longer be able to listen to the popular refrain that there are too many good things to experience in a lifetime.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, Meditations, 5.12

Is it controversial to question whether wealth and fame are all they are cracked up to be? Seneca observed, “If only the hearts of the wealthy were opened to all! How great the fears high fortune stirs up within them.” For centuries, people have assumed wealth would cure their unhappiness, only to find it didn’t deliver the peace they sought.

The Stoics advocate for simpler, more straightforward virtues: wisdom, self-control, justice, courage. Achieving these brings a lasting satisfaction that wealth and status cannot. In the tech world, focus on these virtues can guide your decisions and actions, leading to genuine fulfillment and success.
