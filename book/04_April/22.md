## April 22nd - THE MARKS OF A RATIONAL PERSON

***“These are the properties of the rational soul: it sees itself, analyzes itself, and makes itself such as it chooses; the fruit which it bears itself enjoys... it obtains its own end, wherever the limit of life may be fixed.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 11.1

Marcus gives three marks of a reasoning mind. It sees itself. It analyzes itself. It makes itself into what it chooses.

For a developer that looks like an honest retro with yourself. What did I actually do this week, as opposed to what I planned? Where did I cut corners, and why? Then a choice about next week that is yours, not one the hype cycle made for you. Whatever comes of it is yours to keep.
