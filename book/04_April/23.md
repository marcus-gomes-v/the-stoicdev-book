## April 23rd - THE MIND IS ALL YOURS

***“The things are three of which thou art composed: a little body, a little breath [life], intelligence. Of these the first two are thine, so far as it is thy duty to take care of them; but the third alone is properly thine.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 12.3

Your wrists ache after a long crunch. Your back hurts from the chair. Some day you’ll get sick in a way no health plan fixes. The body and the breath are yours to look after, but you don’t own them the way you think. They can be taken.

Marcus wrote much of this book on campaign and in poor health, and he kept coming back to the one thing that couldn’t be taken: the mind that decides what to think and what to do. Guard it. Don’t rent it out to every feed and every outrage. It is the only part that is fully yours.
