April 23rd - THE MIND IS ALL YOURS

“You have been formed of three parts—body, breath, and mind. Of these, the first two are yours insofar as they are only in your care. The third alone is truly yours.”
—MARCUS AURELIUS, Meditations, 12.3

The body can be ravaged by disease, injured, or disabled. Our breath can cease suddenly due to circumstances beyond our control. But up until the very end, our mind remains ours. While our body and breath are essential, they are not entirely ours. Our mind, however, is our true possession. For developers and leaders, this means investing time and effort into nurturing and protecting our mental faculties. It's the one part of us that remains free, so let's treat it with the care and respect it deserves.
