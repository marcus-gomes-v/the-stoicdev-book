## April 24th - SEEING THROUGH THE ILLUSIONS

***“You have been formed of three parts—body, breath, and mind. Of these, the first two are yours insofar as they are only in your care. The third alone is truly yours.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 12.3

One useful Stoic exercise involves breaking down things that seem grand or desirable into their simplest components. This can help developers and leaders maintain perspective and avoid getting caught up in superficial allure. For example, consider the perfect job or impressive title. Is it really as magical as it seems? Look closely at the sacrifices and stresses behind the scenes. That highly curated social media post? Imagine the effort and potential insecurities behind staging it.

By seeing things as they really are, we can reduce their power over us and maintain a balanced, objective view. This doesn't make us cynics; it makes us realistic and grounded, able to make better decisions free from unnecessary bias.
