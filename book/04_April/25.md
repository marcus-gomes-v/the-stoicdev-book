April 25th EMBRACING CORRECTION

“If anyone can prove and show to me that I think and act in error, I will gladly change it—for I seek the truth, by which no one has ever been harmed. The one who is harmed is the one who abides in deceit and ignorance.” 
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 6.21

In the tech industry, where innovation and learning are constant, changing your mind when faced with new information is a sign of strength, not weakness. Cicero’s willingness to live from day to day, adapting his views as new truths emerged, is a powerful example. Similarly, great leaders and developers should be ready to pivot when they encounter better solutions or ideas.

Stubbornly sticking to outdated methods or beliefs can harm projects and teams. Embrace the humility to accept when you’re wrong and the flexibility to adjust your course. This mindset fosters growth and leads to better decision-making. Remember, in the pursuit of truth and improvement, there is no shame in change.
