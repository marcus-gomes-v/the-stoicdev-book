April 26th LEARN AND LET GO

“When your sparring partner scratches or head-butts you, you don’t then make a show of it, or protest, or view him with suspicion or as plotting against you. And yet you keep an eye on him, not as an enemy or with suspicion, but with a healthy avoidance. You should act this way with all things in life. We should give a pass to many things with our fellow trainees. For, as I’ve said, it’s possible to avoid without suspicion or hate.” 
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 6.20

Every day at work is like a training session. Mistakes happen, conflicts arise, and sometimes, you might feel like you've taken a hit. Instead of reacting with anger or suspicion, see these moments as opportunities for growth. Your colleagues and team members are also learning and improving.

Approach each interaction with patience and understanding. This attitude will not only help you develop resilience but also foster a collaborative and supportive work environment. When setbacks occur, remind yourself: This is part of the process. We’re all here to learn and grow together.
