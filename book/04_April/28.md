## April 28th DESIRE MAKES YOU A SERVANT

***“Tantalus: The height of power is—
Thyestes: Naught, if nothing thou desirest.”***
—SENECA, THYESTES, 443

Thyestes says this to his son on the way home from exile. Young Tantalus is dazzled by the throne they’ve been offered. His father has held power before and knows what it costs.

A lot of the power other people have over you comes from what you want from them. You stay with a manager who belittles you because you want the promotion they control. You laugh at the founder’s jokes because you want the next round to close. You ship the dark pattern the client asked for because you want the renewal. Each want is a handle someone else can hold.

You don’t have to want nothing. But it helps to see which handles you’ve given out. Keep enough savings that a bad job can’t trap you. Don’t let your sense of your own worth hang on one person’s review. The less you need from someone, the less they can make you do.
