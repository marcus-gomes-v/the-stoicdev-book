April 28th DESIRE MAKES YOU A SERVANT

“Tantalus: The highest power is—
Thyestes: No power, if you desire nothing.” 
—SENECA, THYESTES, 440

In the modern tech world, we often voluntarily surrender our freedom. We tolerate a controlling boss, though we could find another job. We alter our behavior to fit in with certain groups. We endure harsh critics or customers because we crave their approval. Their power over us stems from our desires. Change what you want, and you free yourself.

The late fashion photographer Bill Cunningham occasionally declined to invoice magazines for his work. When asked why, Cunningham’s response was epic: “If you don’t take money, they can’t tell you what to do, kid.”

For developers, managers, and tech leaders, remember: Desiring what others have makes you their servant. By being indifferent to those desires, you reclaim your freedom. Focus on what truly matters—your work, your ethics, and your personal growth. Don’t let external desires dictate your path.
