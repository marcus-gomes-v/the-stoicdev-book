## April 29th FINDING PERSPECTIVE IN THE STARS

***“Watch the stars in their courses and imagine yourself running alongside them. Think constantly on the changes of the elements into each other, for such thoughts wash away the dust of earthly life.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 7.47

It's almost impossible to stare up at the stars and not feel something profound. As cosmologist Neil deGrasse Tyson has explained, the cosmos fills us with complex emotions. We feel an infinitesimal smallness compared to the vast universe and, simultaneously, a deep connectedness to this larger whole.

In our daily grind, it's easy to think our work and problems are the most important things in the world. But when we look at the stars, we gain perspective. For developers, managers, and tech leaders, this is a reminder to step back and view the bigger picture. The mundane worries and stresses of work life pale in comparison to the grandeur of the universe. Use this perspective to wash away the dust of earthly concerns and focus on what truly matters.
