## April 29th FINDING PERSPECTIVE IN THE STARS

***“Look round at the courses of the stars, as if thou wert going along with them; and constantly consider the changes of the elements into one another, for such thoughts purge away the filth of the terrene life.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 7.47

At the end of a bad day it can feel as if the whole world is the ticket you couldn’t close. The flaky test, the stakeholder who wanted it yesterday, the review comment you keep rereading in your head. It fills the screen, and then it fills the evening.

Marcus had a remedy he used on himself. Look up. Picture yourself moving along with the stars on their slow paths. Think about how everything turns into something else: water into air, the whole into its parts, this year’s crisis into next year’s footnote.

He isn’t saying your work doesn’t matter. It does. But it is small against the sky, and seeing it at that scale scrubs off some of the grime the day left on you. You come back to the ticket tomorrow cleaner than you left it.
