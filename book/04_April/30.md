## April 30th ALIGN YOURSELF WITH YOUR TRUE CHARACTER

***“Just as what is considered rational or irrational differs for each person, in the same way what is good or evil and useful or useless differs for each person. This is why we need education, so that we might learn how to adjust our preconceived notions of the rational and irrational in harmony with nature. In sorting this out, we don’t simply rely on our estimate of the value of external things, but also apply the rule of what is in keeping with one’s character.”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 1.2.5-7

It's easy to get wrapped up in our opinions and the expectations of others. We follow invisible scripts, adhering to patterns we don’t fully understand. By questioning these scripts and testing them against our education and values, we become our own compass. This process helps us develop convictions and beliefs that are truly our own.

In the tech industry, where trends and pressures can easily sway us, maintaining a strong character is crucial. When you know what you believe and why you believe it, you are better equipped to navigate the challenges and temptations of your professional and personal life. This inner compass helps you avoid toxic environments, make ethical decisions, and build a career that aligns with your true self. This is the essence of your education and the work you do every day.
