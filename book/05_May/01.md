## May 1st CHARACTER ABOVE ALL ELSE

***“Philosophy does not lie in the rough cloak or the long hair. It lies in thinking what one ought to think, and reasoning it through.”***
—MUSONIUS RUFUS, LECTURES, 16

In Musonius’s Rome you could spot a philosopher from across the forum: the rough cloak, no tunic underneath, the long hair. He told a young man who wanted to study philosophy that none of it was needed.

Our version of the cloak is easier to buy. The conference hoodie, the mechanical keyboard, the dotfiles repo with four thousand stars, the job title with “Staff” in front of it. None of that is bad. None of it tells anyone how you think.

What does tell them is harder to fake. How you treat a reviewer who is right and rude about it. What you say in the retro when the outage was partly yours.

Nobody on your team will remember your setup a year from now. They will remember whether you could be trusted on the day it mattered.
