## May 1st CHARACTER ABOVE ALL ELSE

***“For philosophy doesn’t consist in outward display, but in taking heed to what is needed and being mindful of it.”***
—MUSONIUS RUFUS, LECTURES, 16.75.15-16

A monk dresses in his robes. A priest puts on his collar. A banker wears an expensive suit and carries a briefcase. A Stoic has no uniform and resembles no stereotype. They are not identifiable by look or by sight or by sound. The only way to recognize them? By their character.

In the world of tech and leadership, it's easy to get caught up in titles, achievements, and outward success. But true leaders are defined not by their credentials but by their actions and integrity. Make your character your loudest statement. Let your commitment to ethical behavior, fairness, and right action be what sets you apart. In doing so, you not only embody Stoic principles but also inspire those around you to follow suit.
