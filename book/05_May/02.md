## May 2nd DEFINE AND PURSUE YOUR GOALS

***“Tell yourself, first of all, what kind of man you want to be; and then go ahead with what you are doing. For in practically every other pursuit we see this done. The athletes first decide what kind of athletes they want to be, and then they act accordingly.”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 3.23.1-2

A distance runner eats, sleeps and trains differently from a sprinter. Epictetus picks the example because it’s obvious. Nobody trains for running in general.

Plenty of developers do, though. A Rust course because everyone was talking about it, a weekend with a new frontend framework, a side project abandoned at eighty percent. Each one made sense at the time. Put together, they don’t add up to anyone in particular.

So decide first. Maybe you want to be the person who can take a system apart and say exactly where it will break. Maybe you want to be the one who turns a vague request into something the team can ship. Either is a good answer. Once you’ve picked, a lot of the noise sorts itself out, and you know which talks to skip and which dull book to finish.

Then do the work that kind of developer does, even on the days it bores you.
