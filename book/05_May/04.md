## May 4th TRUE IMPRESSIVENESS

***“And yet how much more glorious is it to do good to many people than to live in a lavish house? How much nobler to spend on human beings than on timber and stone?”***
—MUSONIUS RUFUS, LECTURES, 19

Every so often a startup posts photos of its new office: the slide between floors, the espresso bar, the neon sign with the company motto. A year later the same company announces layoffs from that building.

Musonius taught in a Rome where rich men competed over colonnades, gilded ceilings and stone shipped from far away. His question still works. What else could that money have done?

You probably don’t control a company budget. You do control where your own effort goes. Another week polishing a personal brand, or an hour with the junior developer who has been stuck since lunch. The people you helped will remember you long after everyone has forgotten the office.
