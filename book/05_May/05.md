## May 5th YOU ARE THE PROJECT

***“The subject-matter with which the good and excellent man has to deal is his own governing principle, that of a physician and the masseur is the body, of a farmer is his farm.”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 3.3.1

A doctor works on bodies. A farmer works on a field. If you want to be a good person, Epictetus says, the material you work on is your own mind: how you judge, decide and react.

Developers already understand material. You have a codebase and you spend years learning its moods. You put tests around the fragile parts and leave it a little better each week.

Your judgment deserves the same attention. The habit of snapping in code review, the reflex to agree to any estimate, the panic when the dashboard goes red. Those are parts of the system too, and they can be refactored. Nobody will ever assign you that ticket. It still affects everything else you ship.
