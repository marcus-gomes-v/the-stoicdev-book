## May 7th THE SECRET TO A GOOD DAY

***“This is the law which God has ordained, and He says, ‘If you wish any good thing, get it from yourself.’”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 1.29.4

Much of what makes a workday good is out of your hands. The build goes green or it doesn’t. The client is pleased or isn’t.

Epictetus points to the one source nobody can switch off: your own actions. Write the clear commit message. Help the person who asked. That supply never runs out.
