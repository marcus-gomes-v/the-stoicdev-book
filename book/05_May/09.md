## May 9th SEIZE THE DAY

***“So let us put our whole mind to this. Let us drop whatever pulls us aside and labour at one thing alone, or we will grasp how fast time runs, and that nothing can hold it back, only when it has already left us behind. Let each day, as it comes, be welcomed as the best and be made our own. What is running away has to be caught.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 108.27-28

Seneca wrote his letters late in life, after years at Nero’s court. He knew how much time a busy, important life can swallow.

Your day will fill up whether you plan it or not. A meeting that could have been a message, a quick look at the metrics that turns into forty minutes. By five o’clock it’s gone, and the one thing that mattered is still sitting in the backlog.

Pick that thing before you open anything else, and give it your best hours. Time is running either way.
