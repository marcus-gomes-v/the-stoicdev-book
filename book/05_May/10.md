May 10th BE THE INSPIRATION

“Let us also produce some bold act of our own—and join the ranks of the most emulated.” 
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 98.13b

In Greek and Roman times, as now, politicians often pander to their audience, praising ancestors and past victories to gain favor. We hear the same today—political candidates lauding the greatness of their country to garner applause.

But flattery achieves nothing. The admiration of past exploits can distract us from their true purpose. Demosthenes reminded the Athenians that their ancestors’ trophies were not meant to be gazed at in wonder, but to inspire them to emulate those virtues.

Apply this to your life as a developer, leader, or entrepreneur. Don’t just admire inspirational words or accomplishments. Use them as motivation to take bold actions and become an inspiration yourself.
