## May 10th BE THE INSPIRATION

***“Let us, too, do something bold ourselves, and take our place among the examples.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 98.13

Just before this line, Seneca lists Romans who turned down what everyone else chased: a general who refused riches, a man who declined a senator’s rank when Julius Caesar offered it. Then he turns to his reader. Enough admiring. Do something yourself.

Developers are very good at admiring. We read the postmortem of a famous outage and nod along. We bookmark talks by engineers we would love to be like.

Admiration is a fine place to start, as long as at some point it turns into a decision of your own. Deprecating the service everyone has been afraid to touch. Giving the honest estimate that will disappoint someone. A newer person on the team is watching how you handle those moments. Whether you planned it or not, you’re already somebody’s example.
