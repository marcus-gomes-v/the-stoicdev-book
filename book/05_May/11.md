May 11th THE PRISON OF GUILT

“The greatest portion of peace of mind is doing nothing wrong. Those who lack self-control live disoriented and disturbed lives.” 
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 105.7

Imagine the burden of a fugitive, constantly on the run, always looking over their shoulder. Some eventually surrender not due to capture, but because the mental toll—guilt and paranoia—becomes unbearable. It’s like living in an invisible jail.

This feeling isn't confined to criminals. It surfaces in everyday life when we compromise our values. A lie told, a trust betrayed, or a shortcut in work can imprison us in guilt, impacting our mental well-being and our relationships.

For tech leaders and developers, maintaining ethical integrity isn't just about avoiding legal repercussions; it’s about ensuring peace of mind and job satisfaction. It’s easier to push forward in your career and personal life when you’re not weighed down by guilt. Ethical living isn’t just good philosophy—it’s good practice.
