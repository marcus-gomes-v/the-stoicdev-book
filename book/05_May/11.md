## May 11th THE PRISON OF GUILT

***“A large part of living free from worry is never acting unjustly. People who cannot govern themselves lead lives of confusion and turmoil.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 105.7

You told the product manager the feature was tested when you had only clicked through the happy path. Now every notification makes your stomach drop. Is this the one where somebody finds out?

Seneca spent years at Nero’s court, where plenty of people lived like that. In the same letter he notes that the guilty don’t need to be caught to be punished. They tremble after the act, and their conscience keeps putting them on trial, even in their sleep.

The cure is boring. Don’t cut the corner, or if you already did, say so before anyone asks. Write in the ticket that the edge cases weren’t covered. Tell your lead the migration skipped a step. It costs you an awkward five minutes. Keeping it hidden costs you every quiet evening until it comes out, and it usually does.
