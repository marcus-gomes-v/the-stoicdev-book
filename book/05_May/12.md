## May 12th THE STRENGTH OF SINCERE KINDNESS

***“A good disposition is invincible if it be genuine, and not an affected smile and acting a part. For what will the most violent man do to thee, if thou continuest to be of a kind disposition towards him, and if, as opportunity offers, thou gently admonishest him and calmly correctest his errors at the very time when he is trying to do thee harm...”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 11.18

Someone in the thread is being unfair to you. A senior engineer from another team has decided your design is naive, and says so in every comment with a little more edge each time.

The usual move is to match the tone. It feels like strength and it rarely works. Now there are two people being sharp, and everyone reading has to pick a side.

Marcus suggests something harder. Stay kind, and mean it. Answer the points that are fair. Where they’re wrong, say so plainly and calmly, without the sarcasm you’re tempted to add. He insists the kindness has to be genuine, not a polite smile over clenched teeth, and people can tell the difference.

Hostility needs something to push against. Give it nothing, and most of the time it runs out of steam.
