## May 14th ACTION AS THE TRUE SOURCE OF FULFILLMENT

***“Those obsessed with glory attach their well-being to the regard of others, those who love pleasure tie it to feelings, but the one with true understanding seeks it only in their own actions. .. . Think on the character of the people one wishes to please, the possessions one means to gain, and the tactics one employs to such ends. How quickly time erases such things, and how many will yet be wiped away.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 6:51, 59

In the tech industry, where innovations and accolades are often celebrated, it's easy to become fixated on external validations. However, true satisfaction and well-being are derived from the integrity of our own actions. Consider the daily tasks you undertake from coding to critical decision-making and recognize that the true value lies not in the recognition these actions might garner but in the quality and ethical nature of the actions themselves. By focusing on what you can control your actions you ensure that your professional life is driven not by fleeting accolades but by a deep, enduring sense of purpose. This alignment not only brings personal satisfaction but also builds a legacy of integrity and respect in your field.
