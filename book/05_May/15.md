## May 15th VALUING YOUR REAL ASSETS

***“Think not so much of what thou hast not as of what thou hast: but of the things which thou hast select the best, and then reflect how eagerly they would have been sought, if thou hadst them not. At the same time, however, take care that thou dost not through being so pleased with them accustom thyself to overvalue them, so as to be disturbed if ever thou shouldst not have them.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 7.27

It’s easy to list what you don’t have. The bigger salary a friend got somewhere else. The title you were promised last cycle.

Marcus asks for the other list. A lead who shields you from the worst of the chaos. Colleagues who answer your questions without making you feel slow. A stack you know well enough to fix half asleep. Picture losing them and having to find them again, and you’ll see how much you would want them back.

Then comes the second half, which people tend to skip. Don’t hold on so tightly that losing them would knock you over. Teams get reorganized and good managers leave. Be glad of what you have while you have it, and keep enough of yourself apart from it to be fine when it changes.
