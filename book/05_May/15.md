## May 15th VALUING YOUR REAL ASSETS

***“Don’t set your mind on things you don’t possess as if they were yours, but count the blessings you actually possess and think how much you would desire them if they weren’t already yours. But watch yourself, that you don’t value these things to the point of being troubled if you should lose them.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 7.27

In the world of technology and startups, where new gadgets and innovations are constantly vying for attention, it's easy to get caught up in what you lack rather than appreciating what you have. Today, take a moment to reflect on the resources, skills, and relationships you've cultivated. These are your real assets. Consider how vital they've been to your success and how much you'd strive to obtain them if you didn’t already possess them. Use this perspective to guide your professional interactions and decisions, ensuring that gratitude, not greed, motivates your actions. This mindset not only enhances personal contentment but also fosters a more supportive and collaborative work environment.
