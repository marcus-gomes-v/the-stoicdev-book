## May 18th THE SIGNIFICANCE OF THE PRESENT MOMENT

***“Attend to the matter which is before thee, whether it is an opinion or an act or a word.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 8.22

You’re in a one-on-one with someone on your team, and half your mind is on the deploy that’s running. You’re reviewing a pull request while drafting a reply to your manager in another window.

Marcus’s note to himself is short: attend to what is in front of you, whether it’s an opinion, an act or a word.

It sounds too simple to need writing down. He wrote it anyway, an emperor running a war from camps along the Danube. Close the other tabs. Give the person, or the code, all of your attention for the next twenty minutes. You’ll hear the worry they almost didn’t mention, or catch the bug you would have waved through.
