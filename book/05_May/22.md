## May 22nd SEIZE YOUR NOW

***“You get what you deserve. Instead of being a good person today, you choose instead to become one tomorrow.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 8.22

***“I don’t complain about the lack of time . . . what little I have will go far enough. Today—this day—will achieve what no tomorrow will fail to speak about. I will lay siege to the gods and shake up the world.”***
—SENECA, MEDEA, 423-425

The decision to act rightly often meets procrastination. In the tech industry, where rapid innovation and constant updates are norms, the temptation to delay essential tasks for 'better timing' can be overwhelming. Yet, the truth remains that the perfect moment is a myth. Today holds the same potential for greatness as any future date. Let's shift the focus from what we plan to do to what we are actually doing. Embrace today’s opportunities to advance your projects, refine your code, or improve your team's dynamics. Each small, immediate action builds the pathway to achieving significant goals. Why wait for tomorrow to become the leader, developer, or innovator you can be today?
