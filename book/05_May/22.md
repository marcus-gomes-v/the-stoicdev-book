## May 22nd SEIZE YOUR NOW

***“Thou sufferest this justly: for thou choosest rather to become good to-morrow than to be good to-day.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 8.22

***“But time’s short space I’ll not bewail; though brief in hours, / In consequence it stretches out eternally. / This day shall see a deed that ne’er shall be forgot. / But now I’ll go and pray the gods, and move high heaven / But I shall work my will!”***
—SENECA, MEDEA, 423-425

Marcus is hard on himself here. You’re suffering this justly, he writes, because you would rather become good tomorrow than be good today.

Developers know the technical version. You’ll write the tests after the release. You’ll clean up the module once the quarter calms down. The hard conversation with the person you manage can wait until after review season. Tomorrow is a very comfortable place to keep all of that, because tomorrow never asks anything of you.

The Seneca lines come from Medea, spoken by a woman with one day left and a terrible plan, so she is no role model. Her sense of time is still right. A single day is short, and a great deal can be done in it. Choose one thing you’ve been postponing and get it done before this one ends.
