## May 23rd LIVING FULLY TODAY

***“Show me that the good life doesn’t consist in its length, but in its use, and that it is possible—no, entirely too common—for a person who has had a long life to have lived too little.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 49.10b

In the tech industry, we often measure success by the longevity of a product or the duration of a career. However, the true value lies not in how long we or our creations last, but in how effectively we use the time we have. It's not about enduring through endless meetings or coding sessions; it's about making each moment count towards creating something meaningful or solving real problems. Whether you're a developer facing a backlog of tasks or a leader guiding a team, remember that today presents a unique opportunity to enrich your professional journey and personal growth. Make your mark not by counting days, but by making the days count. Start by asking yourself each morning: What can I do today that will make me proud, help my team, or advance our goals? Let this guide your actions, so when you look back, you'll see a life of meaningful achievements, not just a series of calendar entries.
