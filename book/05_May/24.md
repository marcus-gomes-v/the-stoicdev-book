## May 24th CREATING YOUR OWN LUCK

***“You say, good fortune used to meet you at every corner. But the fortunate person is the one who gives themselves a good fortune. And good fortunes are a well-tuned soul, good impulses and good actions.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 5.36

In the realm of technology and business, relying on luck is not a strategy. Yet, many often attribute their success to fortunate turns of events. The true driver behind lasting achievements in any field, especially in fast-paced environments like tech startups or software development, is not mere chance but the cultivation of one’s own capabilities and decision-making processes. Marcus Aurelius points us towards creating our own good fortune through disciplined habits, thoughtful actions, and a harmonious inner life. As tech professionals, focusing on continuous improvement, ethical decision-making, and proactive problem-solving can transform perceived luck into a predictable outcome of hard work and resilience. Instead of waiting for good fortune, forge it through excellence in your craft and integrity in your actions. Today, identify areas in your work or life where you've attributed success to luck and consider how your actions influenced those outcomes. This perspective empowers you to replicate success deliberately, rather than accidentally.
