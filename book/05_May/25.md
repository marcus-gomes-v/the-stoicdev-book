## May 25th DISCOVERING AUTHENTIC JOY

***“It is satisfaction to a man to do the proper works of a man. Now it is a proper work of a man to be benevolent to his own kind, to despise the movements of the senses, to form a just judgment of plausible appearances, and to take a survey of the nature of the universe and of the things which happen in it.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 8.26

Think about the best days you’ve had at work. Probably not the offsite with the open bar. More likely the afternoon you finally understood why the cache was serving stale data, or the week a new teammate went from lost to shipping their first feature because you sat with them.

Marcus keeps a short list of what he calls the proper work of a person. Be kind to your own kind. Don’t let every craving steer you. Test an impression before you trust it. Look at how the world actually works.

A surprising amount of a developer’s job fits on that list. Sitting with the new hire is the first item. Asking whether this is really the root cause, instead of shipping the first fix that turns the graph green, is the third. When a day is full of that kind of work, it satisfies even if nothing glamorous happened.
