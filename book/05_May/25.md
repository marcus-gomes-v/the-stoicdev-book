May 25th DISCOVERING AUTHENTIC JOY

“Joy for human beings lies in proper human work. And proper human work consists in: acts of kindness to other human beings, disdain for the stirrings of the senses, identifying trustworthy impressions, and contemplating the natural order and all that happens in keeping with it.” 
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 8.26

For professionals in tech and leadership, true satisfaction often arises not from fleeting pleasures or superficial achievements but from meaningful engagement and purposeful work. Marcus Aurelius guides us to find joy in acts that reflect our deepest human capabilities—kindness, rationality, and harmony with universal principles. In the tech world, this might translate into creating technologies that genuinely improve lives, fostering inclusive and supportive workplace cultures, or ensuring that our projects align with ethical standards and contribute positively to society. Today, reflect on how your daily tasks connect to these deeper, more satisfying layers of professional and personal fulfillment. By aligning your work with these principles, you cultivate a source of joy that is both sustainable and deeply rooted in your true nature as a rational and social being.
