## May 26th PRIORITIZE SELF-APPROVAL

***“I have often wondered how it is that every man loves himself more than all the rest of men, but yet sets less value on his own opinion of himself than on the opinion of others... So much more respect have we to what our neighbors shall think of us than to what we shall think of ourselves.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 12.4

You spent a week on a design doc. You thought it through, tested the risky parts, and you’re fairly sure it’s right. Then one senior engineer leaves a lukewarm comment, and suddenly you’re ready to throw the whole thing out.

Marcus found this strange. We love ourselves more than anyone else, he says, and still we trust other people’s opinion of us over our own.

Feedback matters, and some of it will be right. Read the comment properly. If it points to a real flaw, fix the flaw. If all it carries is someone’s mood, or a personal preference dressed up as a principle, you’re allowed to weigh it against your own careful judgment and keep your design.

Keep listening to people. Just stop handing your sense of whether you did good work to whoever commented last.
