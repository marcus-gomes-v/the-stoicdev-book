## May 26th PRIORITIZE SELF-APPROVAL

***“I'm constantly amazed by how easily we love ourselves above all others, yet we put more stock in the opinions of others than in our own estimation of self. . .. How much credence we give to the opinions our peers have of us and how little to our very own!”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 12.4

In the tech industry, where trends and opinions can change as rapidly as the technologies themselves, it's crucial to maintain a firm sense of self. Marcus Aurelius highlights a common paradox: we value our own opinion less than that of others, even though we know ourselves best. This dependency on external validation not only affects personal well-being but can also skew business decisions and creative innovation towards popular opinion rather than true value.

Today, challenge yourself to focus more on your own judgments and convictions, particularly in your professional actions and decisions. By valuing your own insights and maintaining integrity in your work, you strengthen your leadership and contribute more authentically to your field. Remember, real confidence and progress come not from the fleeting approval of others but from your own reasoned and steadfast self-approval.
