## May 27th VALUE IN VIGILANCE

***“Well-being is attained by little and little, and nevertheless it is no little thing itself.”***
—ZENO, QUOTED IN DIOGENES LAERTIUS, LIVES OF THE EMINENT PHILOSOPHERS, 7.1.26

According to Diogenes Laertius, Zeno came to Athens after a shipwreck and ended up teaching in a painted porch. He knew something about slow beginnings.

His point is that a good life is built from pieces too small to notice. Software is too. A codebase becomes pleasant to work in through hundreds of small acts: a clear variable name, a test added next to a bug fix, a comment explaining why the odd workaround exists. None of them gets a mention at the demo.

The bad version works the same way. Nobody decides to build an unmaintainable system. It happens one skipped review and one “temporary” hack at a time.

So the small choice in front of you right now counts more than it looks. Make it carefully.
