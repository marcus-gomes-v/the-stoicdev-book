## May 27th VALUE IN VIGILANCE

***“Well-being is realized by small steps, but is truly no small thing.”***
—ZENO, QUOTED IN DIOGENES LAERTIUS, LIVES OF THE EMINENT PHILOSOPHERS, 7.1.26

Zeno’s wise observation underscores the importance of consistency in small actions to achieve overall well-being. This principle is particularly relevant in the tech industry, where details in coding, design decisions, and daily interactions can significantly impact the end product and professional relationships.

Today, challenge yourself to pay attention to the small but critical choices you face: from how you manage your time, interact with colleagues, or tackle a challenging problem. Each decision might seem minor, but together, they shape the quality of your work and the integrity of your professional path. Embrace the diligence required in these moments; it is these small, consistent steps that lead to significant achievements and lasting well-being.
