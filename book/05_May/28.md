## May 28th DECISIONS WITH DIGNITY

***“The first thing to do—don’t get worked up. For everything happens according to the nature of all things, and in a short time you’ ll be nobody and nowhere, even as the great emperors Hadrian and Augustus are now. The next thing to do—consider carefully the task at hand for what it is, while remembering that your purpose is to be a good human being. Get straight to doing what nature requires of you, and speak as you see most just and fitting—with kindness, modestty, and sincerity.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 8.5

Marcus Aurelius offers a profound strategy for approaching decisions, emphasizing calmness and ethical principles. This approach is especially valuable in the tech industry, where rapid changes and high stakes can lead to significant pressure. 

Before you act today, pause to detach from the heat of the moment. Reflect on the broader impact of your choices, aiming to align your actions with integrity and kindness. By not letting emotions steer your decisions and focusing on being a conscientious professional, you create a foundation for decisions that not only lead to success but also foster respect and trust among colleagues.
