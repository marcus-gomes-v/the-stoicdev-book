## May 29th THE HEALING POWER OF WORK

***“Toil feeds noble spirits.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 31.5

Some weeks drag. You’ve been moved to a project you didn’t choose, the tickets feel pointless, and you spend more time reading industry gossip than writing code. It’s tempting to wait for motivation to show up first.

Seneca suggests the order runs the other way. Hard effort is what feeds a good mind. In the same letter he says that toil is not a good in itself, but that he will cheer on anyone straining uphill toward something worthwhile.

So find the steep part of your work and go at it. The gnarly migration nobody wants. The flaky test that has failed one run in twenty for a year. Pick something hard enough to need all of your attention. Restlessness tends to fade once your hands are busy with something that matters.
