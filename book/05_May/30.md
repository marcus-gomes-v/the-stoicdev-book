## May 30th MEASURING TRUE PRODUCTIVITY

***“I can’t call a person a hard worker just because I hear they read and write, even if working at it all night. Until I know what a person is working for, I can’t deem them industrious. . . . I can if the end they work for is their own ruling principle, having it be and remain in constant harmony with Nature.”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 4.4.41; 43

In the fast-paced tech industry, it's common to equate long hours with productivity. However, true effectiveness isn't about being busy; it's about being purposeful. Before diving into your next task, ask yourself: Is this aligned with my core objectives? Does it contribute meaningfully to my goals? By focusing on these questions, you can ensure that your hard work is not only industrious but also impactful and in harmony with your professional and personal growth. This shift in perspective can lead to more satisfying and productive days, where the quality of work trumps quantity.
