## May 31st FOCUS ON FUNDAMENTAL GOODNESS
***“What is your vocation? To be a good person.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 11.5

In every aspect of your work, whether you're coding, leading a team, or strategizing for your next project, remember the core of Stoic philosophy: your fundamental role is to be good ethically and professionally. This isn't about meeting deadlines or pushing out products; it's about how you handle challenges, treat your colleagues, and commit to personal and collective integrity. Embrace this as your primary obligation, and let it guide your decisions and actions every day. By aligning your professional conduct with the goal of being virtuous, you not only contribute positively to your workplace but also cultivate a fulfilling career that goes beyond mere achievements.
