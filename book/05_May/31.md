## May 31st FOCUS ON FUNDAMENTAL GOODNESS

***“What is thy art? To be good.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 11.5

Ask a developer what they do and you’ll hear a stack. Backend, mostly Go. Frontend lead. Platform team. Those are real answers, and they’ll change several times over a career.

Marcus, who held more titles than almost anyone alive, gave himself a different answer. His art was being good. Everything else was the setting he practised it in.

The technical work still matters. It just has something underneath it. The careful code review, the honest status update, the patience with a stakeholder who keeps changing their mind: that is where the art gets practised every day. Languages and frameworks come and go. Whether people could trust you is the part of a career that lasts.
