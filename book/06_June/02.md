## June 2nd EMBRACING THE BIGGER PICTURE

***“How beautifully Plato put it. Whenever you want to talk about people, it’s best to take a bird’s eye view and see everything all at once—of gatherings, armies, farms, weddings and divorces, births and deaths, noisy courtrooms or silent spaces, every foreign people, holidays, memorials, markets—all blended together and arranged in a pairing of opposites.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 7.48

In tech, where projects and deadlines can sometimes narrow our focus to the minutiae, it’s valuable to step back and adopt a broader perspective. Imagine viewing your work and challenges from a satellite’s vantage point. From this height, individual issues blend into the larger mission; personal setbacks align with collective advances. This perspective, often referred to as the "overview effect" in astronautical circles, reminds us of the interconnectedness of our actions and their wider implications. By elevating our view, we not only see the interplay of different forces and events but also foster a deeper understanding of our place within the whole. This broader outlook can inspire a more cooperative and strategic approach to both personal and professional challenges.
