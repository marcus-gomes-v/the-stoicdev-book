June 3rd EMBRACING ADAPTABILITY

“He can’t serve in the military? Let him seek public office. Must he live in the private sector? Let him be a spokesperson. Is he condemned to silence? Let him aid his fellow citizens by silent public witness. Is it dangerous to enter the Forum? Let him display himself, in private homes, at public events and gatherings, as a good associate, faithful friend, and moderate tablemate. Has he lost the duties of a socialite? Let him exercise those of a humble citizen.” 
—SENECA, ON TRANQUILITY OF MIND, 4.3

In the tech industry, adaptability isn't just a useful trait; it's a necessity. Projects pivot, technologies evolve, and markets shift. Seneca's advice is pertinent: if one path is blocked, find another. Whether you're a developer facing a deprecated tool, a startup founder navigating market changes, or a leader managing team dynamics, flexibility is key. Embracing adaptability means not only adjusting to changes but also actively seeking new opportunities to apply your skills. It's about making the most of what you can do in the situation you're in, just as Lincoln humorously noted about the man who adjusted his requests down to a pair of trousers. This mindset helps in cultivating resilience and ensuring sustained progress, regardless of the obstacles.
