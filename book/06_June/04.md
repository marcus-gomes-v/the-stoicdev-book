June 4th BUILT FOR CHALLENGES

“Why then are we offended? Why do we complain? This is what we’re here for.” 
—SENECA, ON PROVIDENCE, 5.7b-8

Life in tech, much like life itself, isn't always straightforward or forgiving. Seneca reminds us that struggle and difficulty are integral to the human experience. As developers, leaders, or entrepreneurs in the technology sector, you're constantly faced with challenges, from tight deadlines to complex problem-solving. But remember, these challenges are what you're here for. You inherit resilience from a long lineage of ancestors who survived against the odds. Embrace your challenges as they come; they're not just obstacles but opportunities to grow and prove your mettle. Just as your forebears thrived when faced with adversity, so too can you excel in your modern trials, harnessing the same strength and perseverance that brought you here.
