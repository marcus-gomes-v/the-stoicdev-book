## June 4th BUILT FOR CHALLENGES

***“Why, then, should we be angry? why should we lament? we are prepared for our fate.”***
—SENECA, ON PROVIDENCE, 5.7-8

The pager goes off at 2 a.m. again. Before you’ve even read the alert, the speech has started in your head: why always me, why this service, why does nobody fix the root cause.

Seneca cuts that speech short. Why be angry, why lament, when this is exactly what we were made ready for? Systems fail. Deadlines move. Users find the one path nobody tested. None of it is news to anyone who has done this work for more than a year.

He doesn’t expect you to enjoy it. He does expect you to stop acting surprised. You knew the job came with nights like this, and you’ve got through them before. So get up, read the logs and fix what’s in front of you. Save the complaints for the postmortem, where they might actually change something.
