## June 5th TAKE RESPONSIBILITY NOW

***“And then we say, ‘O Lord God, how may I escape anxiety?’ Fool, have you not hands? Did not God make them for you? Sit down now and pray forsooth that the mucus in your nose may not run! Nay, rather wipe your nose and do not blame God!”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 2.16.13

You’ve been waiting three weeks for the platform team to fix the flaky pipeline. You’ve raised it in standup, in the retro, in a thread with eleven thumbs-up reactions. The pipeline is still flaky.

Epictetus had a talent for being rude in a useful way. You sit there praying your nose won’t run, he says, when you could just wipe it. You have hands.

Most of us have more hands than we use. You can read the pipeline config yourself. You can open a pull request with a retry and a clear note, even if the real fix belongs to another team. Blaming the platform team may be fair. It still leaves you with a red build every morning, and the person who could start fixing it today is sitting in your chair.
