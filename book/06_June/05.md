## June 5th TAKE RESPONSIBILITY NOW

***“We cry to God Almighty, how can we escape this agony? Fool, don’t you have hands? Or could it be God forgot to give you a pair? Sit and pray your nose doesn’t run! Or, rather just wipe your nose and stop seeking a scapegoat.”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 2.16.13

In the bustling world of technology, waiting for external solutions or blaming circumstances can lead to stagnation and missed opportunities. Epictetus's words resonate powerfully here, urging us to take immediate action rather than lament our fate. Whether it’s debugging a complex codebase, addressing team inefficiencies, or navigating company politics, the principle remains the same: utilize the resources and abilities at your disposal. Instead of fixating on the unfair aspects of your environment or waiting for rescue, seize the initiative. Act now with the tools and knowledge you have. This proactive approach not only resolves issues more effectively but also empowers you, reinforcing your ability to influence your career and personal life positively.
