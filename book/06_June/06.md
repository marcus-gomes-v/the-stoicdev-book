## June 6th JUDGEMENT IN PERSISTENCE

***“Think of those who, not by fault of inconsistency but by lack of effort, are too unstable to live as they wish, but only live as they have begun.”***
—SENECA, ON TRANQUILITY OF MIND, 2.6b

In the world of technology and startups, where rapid changes and pivots are common, the wisdom of Seneca rings especially true. It's crucial to distinguish when to persist and when to adapt or pivot. Like the third shopper in Seth Godin's analogy from "The Dip," the effective tech leader, developer, or entrepreneur needs to know when a path is no longer viable when sticking with a failing project, outdated technology, or an ineffective strategy is costing more than a change would. However, this doesn't mean constantly chasing new trends without giving good ideas a chance to mature. The balance between persistence and flexibility is key; knowing when to quit a losing venture and when to stick through challenges is a critical skill in tech, where the landscape evolves rapidly. This approach minimizes waste be it time, resources, or effort and maximizes potential for success.
