## June 7th CHOOSING YOUR INFLUENCES

***“We like to say that we don’t get to choose our parents, that they were given by chance—yet we can truly choose whose children we’d like to be.”***
—SENECA, ON THE BREVITY OF LIFE, 15.3a

In the realm of technology and leadership, where the rate of change is rapid and the need for sound decision-making is critical, the concept of choosing our mentors becomes especially pertinent. We have the unique opportunity to select our influences from a vast array of sources whether it's the pioneering tech innovators, visionary leaders, or the philosophers whose strategic thinking has stood the test of time. By studying their lives and teachings, we can adopt their principles and strategies to guide our own paths in this fast-paced industry. This selective mentorship allows us to cultivate a mindset that not only embraces innovation but is also anchored in the timeless wisdom that can guide us through the complexities of technological advancement and leadership challenges.
