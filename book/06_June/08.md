## June 8th MASTERING THE PROCESS

***“You must build up your life action by action, and be content if each one achieves its goal as far as possible—and no one can keep you from this. But there will be some external obstacle! Perhaps, but no obstacle to acting with justice, self-control, and wisdom. But what if some other area of my action is thwarted? Well, gladly accept the obstacle for what it is and shift your attention to what is given, and another action will immediately take its place, one that better fits the life you are building.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 8.32

In the tech world, focusing on "The Process" is akin to the Agile methodology, where small, continuous improvements in development cycles lead to significant innovations over time. This approach is particularly effective in managing complex projects where change is constant and adaptability is crucial. By concentrating on each step's execution with precision and adaptability, tech leaders and developers can create robust systems and solutions that stand the test of time and serve as foundations for future advancements. This philosophy not only ensures productivity but also fosters a culture of persistent progression and resilience against unforeseen challenges.
