## June 8th MASTERING THE PROCESS

***“It is thy duty to order thy life well in every single act; and if every act does its duty as far as is possible, be content; and no one is able to hinder thee so that each act shall not do its duty.—But something external will stand in the way. Nothing will stand in the way of thy acting justly and soberly and considerately.—But perhaps some other active power will be hindered. Well, but by acquiescing in the hindrance and by being content to transfer thy efforts to that which is allowed, another opportunity of action is immediately put before thee in place of that which was hindered, and one which will adapt itself to this ordering of which we are speaking.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 8.32

Think of your week as a series of commits rather than one big release. Each one only has to do its own job well: this function clear, this test honest.

Marcus saw the objection coming. Something outside will get in the way. Sometimes it will. The API you relied on gets deprecated, the feature gets cut, the sprint goal changes on Wednesday. But nothing can stop you from doing the next piece fairly and carefully. When one path closes, accept it, move your effort to what’s still allowed, and another piece of work shows up in its place that fits just as well.

That’s the whole method. Small acts, each done properly, one after another.
