## June 9th PREEMPTIVE STRATEGY

***“Every vice has someone to speak for it. Each begins bashful and easy to talk round, but from there it spreads wider. Let it begin and you will not get it to stop. All passion is weak at first; then it works itself up and builds its forces as it advances. It is easier to keep it out than to drive it out.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 116.2-3

It starts with one skipped test because the release is tonight. Then a TODO that never gets a ticket. Then merging on Friday afternoons because it’s quicker. Each arrives with a good excuse and looks too small to argue about.

Seneca says every vice starts out this way: weak and easy to talk out of. Once it gets going it builds its own strength. Codebases and teams rot the same way. The cheapest moment to say no is the first pull request, before “just this once” becomes how the team works.
