## June 9th PREEMPTIVE STRATEGY

***“There is no vice which lacks a defense, none that at the outset isn’t modest and easily intervened—but after this the trouble spreads widely. If you allow it to get started you won’t be able to control when it stops. Every emotion is at first weak. Later it rouses itself and gathers strength as it moves along—it’s easier to slow it down than to supplant it.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERs, 106.2b-3a

In software development, this principle translates into proactive problem-solving and risk management. Addressing issues early in the development cycle prevents them from escalating into more significant problems that can derail project timelines and inflate costs. By implementing a preventive approach, tech professionals can maintain control over project progress and outcomes. This proactive stance ensures that teams are not merely reacting to problems as they arise but are setting the stage for smooth operations and successful project completion.
