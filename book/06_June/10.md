## June 10th YOUR POTENTIAL IS UNLIMITED

***“If a thing is difficult to be accomplished by thyself, do not think that it is impossible for man: but if anything is possible for man and conformable to his nature, think that this can be attained by thyself too.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 6.19

A colleague ships the kind of feature you’ve been circling for months, or gives the conference talk you keep saying you’ll write. The easy story is that they’re just built differently.

Marcus offers a better one. If something is hard for you, don’t conclude it’s impossible for people. And if people can do it, and it fits human nature, you can do it too.

Their feature is evidence that the problem can be solved, and that’s useful information. Ask them how they started, read what they read, and give yourself the same months of practice they had.
