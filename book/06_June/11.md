## June 11th HALT THE NEGATIVE SPIRAL

***“Consider how much more pain is brought on us by the anger and vexation caused by such acts than by the acts themselves, at which we are angry and vexed.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 11.18

A colleague force-pushes over your branch and two days of commits vanish from the remote. That’s bad. Then comes the part you add yourself: the sharp message, the cold standup, a week of replaying it in your head while you’re supposed to be working.

Marcus noticed that the second part usually costs more than the first. The act is over in a second. The anger keeps charging you interest.

Most of the damage can be undone anyway. Check the reflog, check your local copy, ask calmly what happened. A short, level conversation fixes the branch and the working relationship. The spiral makes you feel better for about ten minutes and makes everything after that harder.
