## June 11th HALT THE NEGATIVE SPIRAL

***“How much more harmful are the consequences of anger and grief than the circumstances that aroused them in us!”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 11.18.8

In the fast-paced environments typical in tech sectors, setbacks or unexpected issues are common. However, Marcus Aurelius’ advice is invaluable here: avoid exacerbating problems with a knee-jerk emotional reaction. By taking a moment to halt the negative spiral of anger or grief, you give yourself space to assess the situation logically. This pause is crucial for leaders and developers alike, enabling them to approach the issue methodically rather than reactively. It's about maintaining control over your response, which in turn prevents a small problem from becoming a crisis. This strategic pause can transform potential mistakes into opportunities for learning and growth.
