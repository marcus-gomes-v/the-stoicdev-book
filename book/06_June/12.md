## June 12th CULTIVATING MENTAL AGILITY

***“Looked at in this way it is also absurd to say, ‘Advise me.’ What advice am I to give you? Nay, say rather, ‘Enable my mind to adapt itself to whatever comes.’ ... For if circumstances dictate something different, what will you say or what will you do? Bear in mind, therefore, this general principle and you will not be at a loss for a suggestion.”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 2.2.20-21; 24-25

Junior developers often ask for a checklist. They want to know what to do when the build fails, when the client changes the scope, when a senior engineer disagrees with them in public. A checklist helps until the day the situation isn’t on it.

Epictetus wouldn’t play along. Don’t ask me for advice, he told his students. Ask for a mind that can adapt to whatever comes, and keep hold of the general principle, so you’re never stuck when things change.

That’s what experience really is. Learn how systems fail, how people behave under pressure and what you actually care about. Then you won’t need a new instruction every time the script changes.
