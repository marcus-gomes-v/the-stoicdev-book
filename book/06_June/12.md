June 12th CULTIVATING MENTAL AGILITY

“In this way you must understand how laughable it is to say, ‘Tell me what to do!’ What advice could I possibly give? No, a far better request is, ‘Train my mind to adapt to any circumstance.’ . . . In this way, if circumstances take you off script . . . you won’t be desperate for a new prompting.”
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 2.2.20b-1; 24b-25a

In the ever-evolving tech industry, clinging to rigid plans can lead to failure. The ability to adapt swiftly to new technologies, changing project requirements, or unforeseen challenges is more valuable than any preset plan. Epictetus teaches us that a well-trained mind, capable of adapting to any circumstance, offers a significant advantage. Instead of seeking specific instructions for every situation, focus on developing skills like critical thinking, flexibility, and resilience. These qualities ensure you can navigate through any professional hurdles with confidence and inventiveness, turning potential setbacks into opportunities for growth and innovation.
