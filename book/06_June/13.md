## June 13th STRATEGIES FOR DAILY VICTORIES

***“Don’t you know life is like a military campaign? One must serve on watch, another in reconnaissance, another on the front line. . . . So it is for us—each person’s life is a kind of battle, and a long and varied one too. You must keep watch like a soldier and do everything commanded. .. . You have been stationed in a key post, not some lowly place, and not for a short time but for life.”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 3.24.31-36

In the tech industry, where rapid changes and challenges are the norm, viewing daily tasks through the lens of a military campaign can be particularly effective. Just as soldiers must adapt to varying roles and responsibilities, professionals in technology must remain vigilant and prepared. The key traits that lead to success on the battlefield discipline, fortitude, courage, and clearheadedness are equally vital in navigating complex projects or leading a team under pressure. Today, as you face your professional challenges, channel these qualities to maintain focus and direction, ensuring that each action contributes to your overarching objectives.
