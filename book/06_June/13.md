## June 13th STRATEGIES FOR DAILY VICTORIES

***“Do you not know that the business of life is a campaign? One man must mount guard, another go out on reconnaissance, and another out to fight. ... So also in this world; each man’s life is a kind of campaign, and a long and complicated one at that. You have to maintain the character of a soldier, and do each separate act at the bidding of the General, if possible divining what He wishes. ... You have been given a post in an imperial city, and not in some mean place; not for a short time either, but you are a senator for life.”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 3.24.31-36

Not everyone on a team gets the interesting post. Someone is on call this week. Someone is triaging the bug backlog. Someone is writing the migration guide while the others build the feature everyone will demo.

Epictetus compared life to a campaign. One soldier stands guard, another scouts, another fights, and the army fails if everyone insists on the same job. He also told his students that their post wasn’t a minor one, and that it was for life.

So take this week’s post seriously, whichever it is. Answer the page properly, close the stale tickets, write the guide someone will thank you for in a year. To the people depending on it, it matters a great deal.
