## June 14th SHIFTING PERSPECTIVES FOR BETTER OUTCOMES

***"Every event has two handles—one by which it can be carried, and one by which it can’t. If your brother does you wrong, don’t grab it by his wronging, because this is the handle incapable of lifting it. Instead, use the other—that he is your brother, that you were raised together, and then you will have hold of the handle that carries."***
—EPICTETUS, ENCHIRIDION, 43

In the dynamic world of technology and leadership, conflicts and challenges are inevitable. When faced with difficulties, whether with a colleague, a project, or even personal career decisions, the principle of "the other handle" is crucial. It's about shifting perspectives to manage situations more effectively. For instance, instead of dwelling on a team member’s mistake as a failure, we can view it as a learning opportunity for the team, fostering a culture of improvement and support. This approach not only helps in resolving the immediate issue but also strengthens relationships and builds a resilient team dynamic. Today, as you navigate your professional environment, remember that the way you choose to perceive and handle situations can transform challenges into stepping stones for growth.
