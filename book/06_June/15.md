## June 15th THE ART OF EFFECTIVE LISTENING

***“To a stripling who was talking nonsense his words were, ‘The reason why we have two ears and only one mouth is that we may listen the more and talk the less.’”***
—DIOGENES LAERTIUS, LIVES OF EMINENT PHILOSOPHERS, 7.1.23

In most design reviews there’s someone who has the answer before the problem has been fully described. They jump in at minute two. By minute ten the room has agreed to their solution, and a quarter later it turns out they solved a different problem.

Zeno’s line to the young man who wouldn’t stop talking still holds up. Two ears, one mouth. Use them in that proportion.

Listening is slower and it looks less impressive. You let the product manager finish the whole story. You ask the support engineer what the customer actually said, word for word. You leave a pause after someone speaks, and often they fill it with the detail that changes everything. The people who listen well tend to build the right thing the first time.
