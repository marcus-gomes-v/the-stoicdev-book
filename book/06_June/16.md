## June 16th EMBRACING SUPPORT IN YOUR JOURNEY

***“Be not ashamed to be helped; for it is thy business to do thy duty like a soldier in the assault on a town. How then, if being lame thou canst not mount up on the battlements alone, but with the help of another it is possible?”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 7.7

You’ve been stuck on the same bug for six hours. One message in the team channel would probably end it in fifteen minutes. You don’t send it, because you’re senior, or new, or just tired of feeling like you should already know.

Marcus pictured himself as a soldier in an assault on a town. The job is to get onto the wall. If you’re lame and can’t climb without someone’s help, then you take the help. Nobody on the battlements asks how you got up.

Your team doesn’t either. They care that checkout works by Thursday. Send the message, share your screen, say what you’ve already tried. Then pay it back the next time someone else is stuck.
