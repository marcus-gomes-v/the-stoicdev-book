## June 17th CHOOSING YOUR BATTLES WISELY

***"Fortune doesn’t have the long reach we suppose, she can only lay siege to those who hold her tight. So, let’s step back from her as much as possible."***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 82.5b-6

In the world of technology and leadership, where the temptation to constantly seize opportunities and aggressively push for more can resemble a relentless battle, Seneca’s words remind us of the power of strategic withdrawal and thoughtful defense. Instead of grasping tightly at every fleeting chance and facing the exhausting rebounds of fortune, stepping back allows us to evaluate our goals and the sustainability of our efforts. Today, as you navigate your projects and decisions, think of building a philosophy that prioritizes resilience over aggression, sustainability over temporary gains. Reflect on whether you're engaging in battles worth fighting or merely struggling against the unpredictable whims of fortune. Let your actions today be guided by wisdom and strategic foresight, ensuring long-term success and well-being in your professional journey.
