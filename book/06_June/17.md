## June 17th CHOOSING YOUR BATTLES WISELY

***“Fortune’s arms are not as long as we think: she catches no one who is not clinging to her. So let us spring away from her as far as we can.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 82.5-6

Some people treat a career like a slot machine. Every new framework, every reorg, every opening one level up is a chance they can’t let pass. They grab at all of it, so all of it has a grip on them. A cancelled project ruins their month. A missed promotion ruins their year.

Seneca thought fortune’s reach was shorter than we imagine. It only catches the people who are clinging to it. Loosen your grip and a lot of its blows land on nothing.

You can still care about your work. Put your weight on the things luck can’t take: the skill you’re building, how you treat people, the quality of what you ship. Go for the promotion if you want it. Just don’t hang your whole sense of yourself on one committee’s decision.
