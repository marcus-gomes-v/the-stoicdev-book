## June 18th EMBRACING THE INEVITABLE

***“Let fate find us ready and eager. A great mind is one that has handed itself over to fate. A small, ignoble one wrestles against it, thinks badly of how the world is ordered, and would rather set the gods right than itself.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 107.12

Some engineers spend every incident arguing with reality. The cloud provider shouldn’t have gone down. The client shouldn’t have changed the requirements. The previous team shouldn’t have picked that database. All of that may be true, and none of it brings the service back.

Seneca had a word for that habit: small. A great mind accepts how the world is ordered and puts its effort into correcting itself. A small one would rather correct the gods.

The things you don’t control will keep breaking. Let them find you ready, with the rollback plan written and the runbook up to date. When the bad day comes, you’ll be fixing your part of it instead of filing complaints against the universe.
