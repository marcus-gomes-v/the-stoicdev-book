## June 18th EMBRACING THE INEVITABLE

***"Let Fate find us prepared and active. Here is the great soul—the one who surrenders to Fate. The opposite is the weak and degenerate one, who struggles with and has a poor regard for the order of the world, and seeks to correct the faults of the gods rather than their own."***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 107.12

In the dynamic realm of technology and leadership, unpredictability is as certain as the code that runs our systems. Today, let's embody the resilience and preparedness that Seneca champions. Accept that challenges, setbacks, and unexpected turns are not just possibilities but inevitabilities. Approach each task and decision with the mindset of readiness not merely to face what comes but to actively engage with it and adapt. By doing so, we not only fortify ourselves against the vicissitudes of fate but also grow stronger and more capable through each trial. Let's not waste energy wishing for a different reality but instead focus on shaping ourselves to meet whatever realities arise.
