## June 19th MASTERING THE MOMENT

***“Do not disturb thyself by thinking of the whole of thy life. Let not thy thoughts at once embrace all the various troubles which thou mayest expect to befall thee: but on every occasion ask thyself, What is there in this which is intolerable and past bearing? for thou wilt be ashamed to confess.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 8.36

Open the tracker on a bad day and the whole year lands on you at once: the migration, the hiring plan, the tech debt, the launch in March, the review cycle after that. It feels unbearable because you’re trying to carry all of it in one breath.

Marcus told himself not to do that. Don’t let your mind run over every trouble that might come. Take this one, right now, and ask what exactly in it is too much to bear. Most of the time you’d be embarrassed to answer.

The ticket in front of you is a function that needs a test. The meeting in an hour lasts thirty minutes. Neither of those is unbearable. Do the next small thing, then the one after it. The year will be made of those whether you worry about it or not.
