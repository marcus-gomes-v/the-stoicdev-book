## June 19th MASTERING THE MOMENT

***"Don’t let your reflection on the whole sweep of life crush you. Don’t fill your mind with all the bad things that might still happen. Stay focused on the present situation and ask yourself why it’s so unbearable and can’t be survived."***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 8.36

In the fast-paced tech industry, it's easy to become overwhelmed by the sheer volume of tasks, projects, and deadlines looming ahead. Marcus Aurelius reminds us to narrow our focus, to zone in on the immediate challenge without being paralyzed by the cumulative weight of future possibilities. This approach not only alleviates anxiety but enhances our efficiency. Today, let’s channel our energy into the present moment, dissecting our tasks with precision and mindfulness. By doing so, we fortify ourselves against distraction and unnecessary stress, ensuring that our response to each situation is measured and effective. Remember, success in technology and in life is often achieved not by simultaneously addressing the whole but by mastering each part, one at a time.
