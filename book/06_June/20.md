## June 20th EMBODYING SERENITY

***“If the things do not come to thee, the pursuits and avoidances of which disturb thee, still in a manner thou goest to them. Let then thy judgment about them be at rest, and they will remain quiet, and thou wilt not be seen either pursuing or avoiding.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 11.11

Notice how much of your stress you go looking for. You refresh the dashboard every five minutes during a launch. You reread the snippy comment on your pull request. You check the company chat on Saturday to see if anyone is still talking about the outage.

Marcus pointed out that the things we chase and run from often aren’t coming at us at all. We’re the ones walking toward them. Let your judgment about them settle and they go quiet.

On a team this spreads fast. When the tech lead is calm about the deadline, people do their best work. When the lead is frantic, everyone starts refreshing dashboards. Close the tab, decide what really needs your attention, and let the rest sit still.
