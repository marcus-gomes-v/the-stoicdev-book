June 20th EMBODYING SERENITY

"If then it’s not that the things you pursue or avoid are coming at you, but rather that you in a sense are seeking them out, at least try to keep your judgment of them steady, and they too will remain calm and you won’t be seen chasing after or fleeing from them." 
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 11.11

In the tech world, where the pace is relentless and the pressure high, maintaining a steady composure can make or break a team's spirit and effectiveness. Marcus Aurelius advises us that calm isn't just a personal virtue but a catalyst for collective tranquility and focus. As leaders or team members, our demeanor sets the tone. By embodying serenity in the face of deadlines, bugs, or system failures, we not only enhance our own resilience but also inspire and stabilize those around us. Today, let's prioritize maintaining our composure in all professional interactions and challenges. Let calm be our superpower, influencing our environment subtly but significantly.
