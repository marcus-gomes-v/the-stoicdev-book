## June 21st REFRESH YOUR MIND WITH A WALK

***“It does good also to take walks out of doors, that our spirits may be raised and refreshed by the open air and fresh breeze.”***
—SENECA, ON TRANQUILITY OF MIND, 17.8

You’ve been staring at the same stack trace for an hour. You’ve added logging, removed it, added it back. The fix is probably obvious, and your brain refuses to see it.

Get up. Go outside and walk around the block without your phone in your hand. Seneca gave this advice to a friend worn out by his own restless mind: walks in the open air, to lift the spirit and refresh it. He wasn’t writing about productivity. He simply knew that a tired mind doesn’t improve by being pushed harder.

Most developers have a story about solving a bug in the shower or halfway around the block. Plan for that instead of waiting for it. Put a walk in the middle of the afternoon, take the one-on-one outside when the weather allows, and come back to the stack trace with fresh eyes.
