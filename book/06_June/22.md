## June 22nd BREAK THE CYCLE OF UNCHANGED HABITS

***"If you are defeated once and tell yourself you will overcome, but carry on as before, know in the end you’ll be so ill and weakened that eventually you won’t even notice your mistake and will begin to rationalize your behavior."***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 2.18.31

In the fast-paced tech world, repeating ineffective strategies can lead to project failures and missed innovation opportunities. Epictetus's words serve as a critical reminder of the importance of adaptability and learning from setbacks. If a coding approach or a project management strategy didn't work as planned, simply hoping for a better outcome next time without making any changes is futile. Instead, adopt a mindset of iterative improvement evaluate what didn't work, make necessary adjustments, and apply these lessons to future endeavors. This proactive approach not only fosters personal and professional growth but also drives success in an industry that thrives on rapid evolution and problem-solving.
