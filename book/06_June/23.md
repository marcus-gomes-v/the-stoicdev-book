## June 23rd SHORTCUT TO SATISFACTION

***"You could enjoy this very moment with all the things you are praying to reach by taking the long way around—if you’d stop depriving yourself of them."***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 12.1

In the realm of tech, where the next big thing is always on the horizon, it's easy to believe that satisfaction lies in the next promotion, project, or innovation. However, Marcus Aurelius offers a compelling alternative: the realization that many of the rewards we seek through long-term ambitions like respect, satisfaction, and freedom are accessible immediately through our daily choices and attitudes. Instead of waiting for the completion of a complex software project or the success of a startup to feel accomplished, we can find fulfillment in the integrity and passion with which we engage in our work today. This perspective not only enhances our current well-being but also enriches our journey towards those larger goals.
