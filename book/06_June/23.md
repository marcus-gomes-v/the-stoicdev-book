## June 23rd SHORTCUT TO SATISFACTION

***“All those things at which thou wishest to arrive by a circuitous road thou canst have now, if thou dost not refuse them to thyself.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 12.1

There’s a version of the future you keep postponing your life for. After the launch. After the promotion. After you join a company with a cleaner codebase. Then you’ll have time for careful work, and you’ll finally feel like a good engineer.

Marcus says the things you’re trying to reach by that long road are yours now, if you stop refusing them to yourself.

Respect for your craft doesn’t need a senior title. You can write the clean version of this function today. Calm doesn’t need a quieter team; you can decide not to answer the anxious message for thirty seconds. The pride of good work is available in the most boring ticket on the board. The launch and the promotion may still come. You don’t have to wait for them to start working like the engineer you want to be.
