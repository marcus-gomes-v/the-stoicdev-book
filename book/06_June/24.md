## June 24th SEEKING PEACE, NOT VICTORY

***“The good and excellent man neither contends with anyone, nor, as far as he has the power, does he allow others to contend. ... And to be getting an education means this: To be learning what is your own, and what is not your own.”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 4.5.1; 7-8

Tabs versus spaces was funny the first time. The forty-comment thread about it on your pull request is less funny, and the two engineers in it stopped talking about code long ago. Both want to win.

Epictetus said the good person doesn’t fight, and doesn’t let others fight if they can help it. He defines education in a way that has nothing to do with degrees: learning what is your own and what isn’t.

Most of these fights are over things that aren’t really anyone’s own. A formatter can settle the style, a benchmark the performance question. Your part is to notice the fight early, move it somewhere it can actually be decided, and leave your ego out of it. Being right matters less than the team still talking to each other next week.
