June 25th SILENCE IS A SOLUTION

"This is why we say that nothing happens to the wise person contrary to their expectations." 
—SENECA, ON TRANQUILITY OF MIND, 13.3b

In the complex world of tech, where rapid changes and high demands are norms, adopting a measured approach to communication can be a game-changer. Leaders and developers alike often face the temptation to quickly solve problems as they arise. However, wisdom in the tech industry often means having the patience to listen more and speak less. By doing so, we allow room for more information, diverse perspectives, and sometimes, the problems resolve themselves or reveal simpler solutions. Today, consider embracing silence as a strategic tool. Let your team express their ideas fully, let clients state their issues without interruption, and observe how often the path to a solution becomes clearer when you listen more than you respond. This approach not only aids in solving problems but fosters a culture of respect and thoughtful communication.
