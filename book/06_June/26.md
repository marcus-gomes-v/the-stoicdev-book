## June 26th BREAK THE PATTERN

***"What assistance can we find in the fight against habit? Try the opposite!"***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 1.27.4

In the tech industry, where routines and processes can solidify into rigid habits, the principle of "trying the opposite" can serve as a catalyst for innovation and problem-solving. If your usual method for tackling a coding problem or project management issue isn't yielding results, consider what the opposite approach might look like. For instance, if a team consistently applies rigorous planning to every phase and still misses deadlines, perhaps adopting a more agile, flexible method for a project could lead to better outcomes. This technique isn't just about doing things differently for the sake of it, but about challenging underlying assumptions and discovering new solutions that might be hidden by old habits. Today, encourage your team to consider the opposite of their usual approaches in their workflow. You might find that this not only sparks creativity but also leads to more effective and streamlined processes.
