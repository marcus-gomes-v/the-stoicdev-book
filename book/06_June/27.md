June 27th CHARACTER IN CHALLENGES

"How does it help, my husband, to make misfortune heavier by complaining about it? This is more fit for a king—to seize your adversities head on. The more precarious his situation, the more imminent his fall from power, the more firmly he should be resolved to stand and fight. It isn’t manly to retreat from fortune." 
—SENECA, OEDIPUS, 80

In the fast-paced tech industry, the reaction to small setbacks can illuminate much about a leader's or team member's ability to handle significant crises. This concept is similar to the stress tests applied in fields ranging from software development to financial modeling, where systems are deliberately subjected to extreme conditions to reveal potential breakdowns. Just as in these technical assessments, personal responses to minor inconveniences can highlight resilience, flexibility, and the capacity for innovative problem-solving—traits that define effective leadership in technology. So today, when faced with minor irritations or unexpected problems, view them as opportunities to demonstrate grace and inventiveness. Your response can set the tone for your team and can turn even a small mishap into a demonstration of leadership and character.
