## June 30th FINDING OPPORTUNITY IN ADVERSITY

***“It is true that these may impede my action, but they are no impediments to my affects and disposition, which have the power of acting conditionally and changing: for the mind converts and changes every hindrance to its activity into an aid; and so that which is a hindrance is made a furtherance to an act; and that which is an obstacle on the road helps us on this road.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 5.20

The vendor drops support for the library your whole app depends on. A migration nobody wanted is suddenly mandatory, and it feels like someone blew a hole in the roadmap.

Marcus was writing about people who got in his way, but the idea reaches further. Others can block what you do. They can’t block your disposition, and the mind can turn the obstacle itself into material for the next action. What stood in the road becomes the road.

The forced migration is also your chance to delete five years of workarounds. The outage that wrecked your quarter is why you finally got budget for monitoring. You don’t have to pretend these things are good news. You only have to ask, each time, what this obstacle makes possible that wasn’t possible before.
