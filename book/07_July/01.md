## July 1st - Unwavering Integrity

***“Whatever anyone does or says, for my part I’m bound to the good. In the same way an emerald or gold or purple might always proclaim: ‘whatever anyone does or says, I must be what I am and show my true colors.’”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 7.15

The Stoics viewed the universe as a complex but orderly system where everything, including every person, has a specific role or purpose. This idea is based on the ancient understanding of the cosmos as composed of interconnected atoms, making every action part of a grander scheme. In this worldview, even those who commit wrongdoing serve the role of embodying evil, which paradoxically is a necessary aspect of existence.

For someone practicing Stoicism, your paramount duty is to cultivate virtue and wisdom, to embody the ideals that philosophy strives to instill. Today, and every day, focus singularly on fulfilling this duty. Regardless of the actions and words of others, maintain your integrity and show your true, virtuous colors in all you do.
