## July 1st - Unwavering Integrity

***“Whatever any one does or says, I must be good; just as if the gold, or the emerald, or the purple, were always saying this. Whatever any one does or says, I must be emerald and keep my color.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 7.15

Your lead presents your design at the all-hands as if it were theirs. A reviewer leaves a snide comment on a pull request you spent a week on. Each time you feel a pull to become a slightly different person: sharper, pettier, more careful to cover yourself.

Marcus wrote this note to himself as an emperor, with a court full of people who wanted something from him. An emerald doesn’t go grey because someone insults it, and it doesn’t need an audience to stay green.

Your color is the way you work when nobody is checking. You still write the honest commit message. You still review the code and not the author, and you answer the person who wronged you with facts instead of a dig. What other people do and say was never yours to control. Staying green is.
