July 4th - Guarding Your Inner Light

“Protect your own good in all that you do, and as concerns everything else, take what is given as far as you can make reasonable use of it. If you don’t, you’ ll be unlucky, prone to failure, hindered and stymied.” 
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 4.3.11

Consider your core values and virtues as a delicate flame within you. This flame represents your potential and integrity, illuminating your path through life. It is your duty to shield this light, ensuring it burns bright against the winds of external circumstances and inner doubts.

Each interaction, challenge, and decision today offers a chance to fuel this flame or let it dim. By nurturing your inner good, you not only preserve your own moral and emotional well-being but also contribute to a brighter world. Remember, while you cannot control others' flames, your light can inspire them to protect and cherish their own.
