## July 5th - The Honor in Challenge

***“Whatever a good man believes is the honorable thing to do, he will do, even when it means hard work, even when it means loss, even when it means danger. And what is shameful he will not do, even when it brings money, or pleasure, or power. Nothing will frighten him away from the honorable, and nothing will draw him toward the shameful.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 76.18

Most of us would refuse an obvious bribe. The harder cases are smaller. Saying in the incident review that the bad config was yours, when nobody would have traced it. Pushing back on the dark pattern the product team wants, with your performance review two weeks away.

Seneca’s good man doesn’t run the numbers on effort and risk first. He decides what is honorable, then does it. Whatever the shameful option pays is real, and it still doesn’t get a vote.
