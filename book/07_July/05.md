## July 5th - The Honor in Challenge

***“Good people will do what they find honorable to do, even if it requires hard work; they’ ll do it even if it causes them injury; they’ ll do it even if it will bring danger. Again, they won’t do what they find base, even if it brings wealth, pleasure, or power. Nothing will deter them from what is honorable, and nothing will lure them into what is base.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 76.18

Being virtuous isn't a path paved with ease and comfort. True character is tested and displayed not in tranquility, but in challenge. Today, remind yourself that your commitment to your principles must withstand trials and temptations. The tasks and decisions that require resilience, integrity, and courage are your personal responsibilities entrusted to you because of your unique strengths and capabilities. Embrace the rigor involved, knowing that each challenge navigated with honor is a testament to your character.
