## July 6th - Embrace Your Purpose Each Morning

***“On those mornings you struggle with getting up, keep this thought in mind—I am awakening to the work of a human being. Why then am I annoyed that I am going to do what I’m made for, the very things for which I was put into this world? Or was I made for this, to snuggle under the covers and keep warm? It’s so pleasurable. Were you then made for pleasure? In short, to be coddled or to exert yourself?”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 5.1

Rising each morning with purpose isn't just about starting your day it's about embracing your essential role in the world. Marcus Aurelius, despite his imperial responsibilities, grappled with the same reluctance we all feel sometimes. But his reflections remind us that we are designed for action and contribution, not just comfort. As you open your eyes each day, remember that you are waking to fulfill your human duties: to work, to help, to improve not only your life but also those around you. Let this thought invigorate your mornings and propel you into days filled with meaningful activity.
