## July 7th - Learning for Life, Not Just for Tests

***“You ask where Ulysses wandered, when you could be making sure that we ourselves are not forever wandering? ... Teach me this: how to love my country, my wife and my father, and how, even shipwrecked, to keep sailing toward ends so honorable.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 88.7

Seneca had no patience for scholars who argued over the exact route of Ulysses’ voyage. Interesting, he says, but what does it do about the storms in my own head?

We have our own version. Memorizing lifecycle hooks for an interview, or every flag of a tool you open twice a year. None of it is useless, and none of it tells you how to stay decent to your team in a bad quarter, or how to keep going after a launch sinks.

Learn the trivia when the job needs it. Then spend some study time on what Seneca asked for: how to treat the people you owe something to, and how to hold your course after a shipwreck.
