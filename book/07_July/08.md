## July 8th - Take Charge and Live with Integrity

***“Enough of this wretched life and murmuring and apish tricks. Why art thou disturbed? What is there new in this? What unsettles thee? Is it the form of the thing? Look at it. Or is it the matter? Look at it. But besides these there is nothing. Towards the gods then, now become at last more simple and better. It is the same whether we examine these things for a hundred years or three.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 9.37

You’ve been complaining about the same service for a year. It’s slow, it’s ugly, and whoever designed it should be ashamed.

Marcus tells himself to drop the muttering and look. What exactly is bothering you? Look at its form, then at its matter. Read the code. Profile the slow endpoint. The dread is usually bigger than the defect, and once you look, there’s a ticket you can write and a fix you can start.

There’s nothing new in it either, he adds. Three years or a hundred, it’s the same old stuff. Another year of complaining won’t teach you anything. An afternoon with the profiler might.
