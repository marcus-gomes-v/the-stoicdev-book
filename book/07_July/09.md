July 9th - Leadership Through Wisdom

“For I believe a good king is from the outset and by necessity a philosopher, and the philosopher is from the outset a kingly person.” 
—MUSONIUS RUFUS, LECTURES, 8.33.32-34

Leadership, whether in tech or on the battlefield, demands more than just industry knowledge or technical prowess; it requires philosophical insight. Herzl Halevi, an Israeli general, underscores the practicality of philosophy in leadership, flipping the common perception that philosophy is merely for contemplative reflection rather than actionable strategy. Plato’s vision of a utopia governed by a philosopher king isn’t far-fetched when applied to modern leadership roles—whether you're leading a startup, managing a team, or spearheading a project, the philosophical underpinnings of ethics, clear reasoning, and balanced decision-making are crucial. Marcus Aurelius, the quintessential philosopher king, exemplified this by using Stoic principles to govern an empire and navigate the complexities of rule. As leaders in technology, embracing philosophy isn’t just an academic exercise—it's a vital tool in our decision-making arsenal, ensuring we lead with wisdom and integrity in a field that continuously tests our values and vision.
