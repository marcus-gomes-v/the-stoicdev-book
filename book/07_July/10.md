## July 10th - Mastery in the Margins

***"Love the humble art you have learned, and take rest in it. Pass through the remainder of your days as one who whole-heartedly entrusts all possessions to the gods, making yourself neither a tyrant nor a slave to any person."***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 4.31

In the tech industry, where rapid innovation and market disruption are the norms, it's easy to overlook the value of mastering one's foundational skills. Much like renowned comedians who return to small clubs to refine their craft, tech professionals benefit from continually honing the basic yet critical skills that underpin their work. Whether it's coding, system design, project management, or any other "humble art," the dedication to excellence in these fundamental areas is what distinguishes true craftsmen from the merely competent.

Embracing this principle means finding joy and purpose in perfecting your craft, not just in achieving outward success or recognition. It involves a commitment to lifelong learning and improvement, ensuring that every task no matter how small or routine is performed to the best of your ability. This approach not only leads to personal fulfillment but also sets a standard of excellence that inspires others and elevates your entire field. So today, take a moment to reconnect with the core skills of your profession. Practice them, improve them, and most importantly, love them. This is the path to true mastery and contentment in your work.
