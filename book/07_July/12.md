## July 12th - Mastering Essential Principles

***“Neither in thy actions be sluggish nor in thy conversation without method, nor wandering in thy thoughts, nor let there be in thy soul inward contention nor external effusion, nor in life be so busy as to have no leisure.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 8.51

Read this one slowly. It’s a short checklist Marcus wrote for himself, and almost every line has a developer version.

Don’t be sluggish: the ticket you’ve pushed to next sprint four sprints in a row. Don’t talk without method: the standup update that wanders for three minutes and never says whether you’re blocked. Don’t let your thoughts wander: twelve tabs open, two of them about the actual bug. No inward contention and no spilling over: the reply you rewrite five times in your head, or the one you fire off angry.

The last line is the one most of us skip. Don’t be so busy that you have no leisure. A calendar with no gaps looks like dedication. Usually it means you’ve stopped thinking about the work and started only doing it.
