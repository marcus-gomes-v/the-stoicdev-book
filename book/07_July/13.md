## July 13th - The Subtle Strength of Leadership

***"One person, on doing well by others, immediately accounts the expected favor in return. Another is not so quick, but still considers the person a debtor and knows the favor. A third kind of person acts as if not conscious of the deed, rather like a vine producing a cluster of grapes without making further demands, like a horse after its race, or a dog after its walk, or a bee after making its honey. Such a person, having done a good deed, won’t go shouting from rooftops but simply moves on to the next deed just like the vine produces another bunch of grapes in the right season."***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 5.6

In the dynamic world of tech and leadership, the true essence of leadership often manifests quietly. It isn't about accolades or recognition but about the silent continuity of service and influence, much like nature's seamless processes. True leaders in tech are those who innovate, mentor, and contribute without a constant need for acknowledgment, understanding that the real impact of their actions might unfold unseen. Today, reflect on your leadership approach: Are you seeking immediate validation, or are you content to plant seeds whose fruits you might never personally harvest? Emulate the vine, which sustains growth season after season, by cultivating an environment where others can thrive long after your direct involvement fades.
