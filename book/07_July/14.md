## July 14th - Harnessing Power Wisely

***“Great power is always dangerous for the beginner. We ought, therefore, to bear such things according to our power—nay, in accordance with nature.”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 3.13.20

Give a new engineer write access to production in their first week and something will eventually break. They aren’t careless. They just don’t know yet what they don’t know. The same goes for the developer promoted to lead after one great project, or the one who just discovered metaprogramming and now uses it everywhere.

Epictetus is talking about training. A big capacity is risky when it arrives before the judgment to handle it. So carry what you can actually carry, and grow into the rest at the pace your nature allows, not the pace your impatience wants.

If you’re the beginner, ask for the smaller scope and do it well. If you’re the one handing out access, hand it out in steps. Nobody learns to use power well by getting all of it on day one.
