July 15th - Virtue as Its Own Reward

"When you’ve done well and another has benefited from it, why like a fool do you look for a third thing on top—credit for the good deed or a favor in return?" 
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 7.73

In the tech industry, where innovations and successes are often publicly celebrated and rewarded, it's essential to remember why we do what we do. The drive to innovate, assist colleagues, or develop groundbreaking technology shouldn't hinge on receiving accolades or expecting payback. True professionalism in any field, but especially in one as impactful as technology, requires actions driven by the right reasons—not for applause or personal gain. When you solve a problem, contribute to a project, or help a teammate, let the act itself be the reward. This approach not only fosters genuine progress and collaboration but also aligns with the stoic principle of doing good simply because it's the right thing to do, not because it might return a favor or enhance one's reputation.
