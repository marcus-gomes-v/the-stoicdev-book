## July 15th - Virtue as Its Own Reward

***“When thou hast done a good act and another has received it, why dost thou still look for a third thing besides these, as fools do, either to have the reputation of having done a good act or to obtain a return?”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 7.73

You spent Saturday helping someone on another team untangle their deployment pipeline. It works now, and they said thanks. On Monday their manager posts in the channel praising the fix, and your name isn’t in it.

Count what already happened. You did something good, and someone was helped. For Marcus that is the whole transaction, and the person who waits for a third item, the shout-out or the favor owed back, is a fool. It’s harsh, but he was writing to himself.

The third thing is what turns generosity into a trade, and trades go sour. You start helping only where someone will see. You start keeping a list. The help you gave on Saturday was complete on Saturday. Let Monday’s post be about whatever it’s about.
