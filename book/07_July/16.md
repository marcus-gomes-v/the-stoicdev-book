## July 16th - The Call to Align Actions with Values

***“About what am I now employing my own soul? On every occasion I must ask myself this question, and inquire, What have I now in this part of me which they call the ruling principle? and whose soul have I now,—that of a child, or of a young man, ... or of a tyrant, or of a domestic animal, or of a wild beast?”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 5.11

Try asking Marcus’s question at a random point in your workday. The ticket already says what you’re working on. He wants to know what your mind is being used for right now.

The answers can be embarrassing. Twenty minutes rewriting a Slack message so you look less wrong: that’s the young man, worried about how he comes across. Snapping at the junior who asked the same question twice: that’s the tyrant. Scrolling the incident channel for drama with no intention of helping: the domestic animal, happy as long as it’s fed.

Marcus didn’t save this for a yearly retreat. He asked it on every occasion, which is the only way it works. You catch the wild beast in the middle of the heated thread, or you don’t catch it at all.

Then comes the useful follow-up. What would the part of you that reasons rather be doing with the next hour?
