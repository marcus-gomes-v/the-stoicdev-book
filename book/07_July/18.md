## July 18th - Respect Boundaries, Cultivate Freedom

***“To my own free will the free will of my neighbor is just as indifferent as his poor breath and flesh. For though we are made especially for the sake of one another, still the ruling power of each of us has its own office, for otherwise my neighbor’s wickedness would be my harm, which God has not willed, in order that my unhappiness may not depend on another.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 8.56

A teammate merges without review and the release breaks. You share the codebase and the pager, and his choice is still his.

Marcus is precise about this. We exist for each other, and we should help each other. But each mind runs its own affairs. If your colleague’s carelessness could reach inside you and make you careless, or bitter, your character would belong to whoever sat next to you. He believed God had arranged things so that this can’t happen.

So deal with the broken release. Talk to the teammate, turn on branch protection, write it up in the retro. Just don’t hand over your judgment along with the incident. Your mood was never part of his commit.
