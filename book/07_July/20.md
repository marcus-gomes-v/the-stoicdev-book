July 20th - Anchored in Justice

"The unjust person acts against the gods. For insofar as the nature of the universe made rational creatures for the sake of each other, with an eye toward mutual benefit based on true value and never for harm, anyone breaking nature’s will obviously acts against the oldest of gods." 
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 9.1.1

In the competitive and often cutthroat tech industry, the drive to succeed can sometimes tempt professionals to compromise on justice. Marcus Aurelius reminds us that acting justly aligns us with the fundamental nature of humanity and the universe itself, which is geared toward mutual benefit and harmony.

Today, let this principle guide your decisions and interactions. Whether you're negotiating a deal, managing a team, or coding a new application, strive for fairness and integrity. Consider not only what is beneficial for you or your company but also how your actions impact others in your ecosystem. Upholding justice isn't just about following laws; it's about contributing to a professional environment where trust, respect, and mutual support are the norms, not the exceptions. This commitment to justice not only enhances your reputation but also builds a legacy of reliability and ethical leadership in tech.
