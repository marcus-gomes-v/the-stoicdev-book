## July 21st - Collaborative Purpose

***“When thou risest from sleep with reluctance, remember that it is according to thy constitution and according to human nature to perform social acts, but sleeping is common also to irrational animals. But that which is according to each individual’s nature is also more peculiarly its own, and more suitable to its nature, and indeed also more agreeable.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 8.12

Marcus gave himself this pep talk about getting out of bed more than once, and here the argument is different. Sleep is something every animal does. What belongs to you as a human being is acting together with other people. That part is properly yours, and he says it’s also the part that feels best.

It’s easy to forget on a remote morning. The bed is warm, the first meeting is a sync you didn’t want, and the code would be easier if you never had to explain it to anyone. But think about the days that actually stayed with you. The pairing session where the bug finally fell. The launch where the whole team watched the graphs together. The review where someone showed you a better way and you told them so.

Those days were social. You were doing what you’re built for, next to people doing the same. That’s worth getting up for, even on a day that starts with a sync you didn’t want.
