## July 23rd - Equanimity in Recognition and Rejection

***"Receive without pride, let go without attachment."***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 8.33

Navigating the peaks and valleys of professional recognition can be especially challenging in the tech world, where both accolades and criticisms come quickly and frequently. Whether it’s receiving a prestigious award or facing criticism for a project, the Stoic approach teaches us to handle both honors and slights with the same level of detachment.

Today, and every day in your career, strive to receive each piece of feedback without letting it define you. Celebrate your successes but remain humble, and accept criticism with grace, using it as a tool for personal and professional growth. This balanced approach not only keeps you grounded but also maintains your focus on what truly matters: your work and its contribution to the greater good. By embodying this stoic principle, you can inspire steadiness and resilience within your team and throughout your organization.
