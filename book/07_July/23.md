## July 23rd - Equanimity in Recognition and Rejection

***“Receive [wealth or prosperity] without arrogance; and be ready to let it go.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 8.33

The promotion comes through. Your name is in the quarterly update, the VP mentions your project in the all-hands, and for about a week you walk a little taller. Then the reorg. The project is folded into another team, your title stays the same but your scope halves, and the VP who praised you has left.

Marcus fits the whole lesson in a single line. Take good things without arrogance. Be ready to let them go. Few people had more good things to receive than a Roman emperor, or more reason to know how fast they disappear.

The two halves depend on each other. If the praise went to your head, losing it goes straight to your stomach. If you held the title loosely, the reorg is just a reorg. Enjoy the good quarter and thank the people who made it happen. Just don’t build your sense of who you are on top of it, because nothing in a company stays where you put it.
