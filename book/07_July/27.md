## July 27th - VIRTUE ABOVE ALL

***“Indeed, if you find anything in human life better than justice, truth, self-control, courage—in short, anything better than the sufficiency of your own mind, which keeps you acting according to the demands of true reason and accepting what fate gives you outside of your own power of choice—I tell you, if you can see anything better than this, turn to it heart and soul and take full advantage of this greater good you’ve found.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 3.6.1

In the relentless pursuit of career advancement, financial success, and recognition, it’s easy to overlook what truly holds value. We often chase after promotions, higher salaries, or the next big project, believing these will bring lasting fulfillment. However, these achievements, while rewarding, often come with their own set of challenges and stresses, and they rarely provide the lasting satisfaction we hope for.

What stands the test of time is virtue justice, truth, self-control, and courage. These are the cornerstones of a fulfilling life and a successful career. When we prioritize virtue, we align our actions with our values, leading to integrity and respect in our professional and personal lives. Virtue guides us to act with fairness, to speak honestly, to maintain discipline, and to face challenges with bravery.

In the tech industry, where innovation and ethics often intersect, embodying these virtues can set you apart as a leader and a role model. Let your decisions be driven by virtue rather than the transient allure of external rewards. This approach not only builds a solid foundation for your career but also contributes to a culture of excellence and ethical conduct
