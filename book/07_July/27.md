## July 27th - VIRTUE ABOVE ALL

***“If thou findest in human life anything better than justice, truth, temperance, fortitude, and, in a word, anything better than thy own mind’s self-satisfaction in the things which it enables thee to do according to right reason, and in the condition that is assigned to thee without thy own choice; if, I say, thou seest anything better than this, turn to it with all thy soul, and enjoy that which thou hast found to be the best.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 3.6

Marcus makes an open offer here. If you find something better than justice, truth, self-control and courage, go after it with everything you have. He isn’t being sarcastic. He’s confident you’ll come back empty-handed.

Try it with the things we usually chase. The staff title. The startup that gets acquired. Each looks like the answer for a while. Then you meet the people who have them. Some are happy and plenty aren’t, and the happy ones tend to be happy for reasons that have nothing to do with the title. They’re honest with their teams, and they keep their tempers on the bad days.

That’s the comparison Marcus wants you to make. Put the promotion on one side and your own integrity on the other, and ask which you’d keep if you could only keep one. Most of us know the answer. We just forget it during calibration season.

None of this means you should turn down the raise. Take it if it comes. Just don’t trade the good things for it, the honest estimate, the fair review, the bug you own up to, because those are the only things on the list that are fully yours.
