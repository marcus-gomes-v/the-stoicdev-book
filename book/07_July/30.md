## July 30th - THE VALUE OF SELF-CONTROL

***“Things do not touch the soul, for they are external and remain immovable; but our perturbations come only from the opinion which is within.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 4.3

The deploy fails. The client moves the deadline up a week. Your manager books a “quick chat” for Friday at five with no agenda. Any one of these can ruin an evening.

Marcus says they can’t, not on their own. Events stay outside. They don’t climb into your head. What climbs in is your opinion about them: this is a disaster, they’re going to fire me, I’ll never get this done. That opinion is the part you can work on.

It takes practice. When the next thing lands, notice the story you start telling and ask what you actually know. The deploy failed, so read the log. The deadline moved, so cut scope with your lead. The chat is a chat. Most of the time the facts are smaller than the story.
