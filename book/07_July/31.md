## July 31st - BALANCE YOUR WORK AND LIFE

***“It is a shameful ending, when a man’s breath deserts him in a court of justice, while, although well stricken in years, he is still striving to gain the sympathies of an ignorant audience for some obscure litigant.”***
—SENECA, ON THE BREVITY OF LIFE, 20.2

Seneca’s picture is specific: an old man collapsing in court, still arguing for a client he barely knows, still hoping a crowd that doesn’t care will take his side. To him it was a shameful way to go.

The modern version doesn’t need a courtroom. It’s the engineer answering pages from a hospital bed. It’s the founder who sold the company and still checks its dashboard every morning, or the senior dev with nothing to talk about on vacation except the backlog.

Most of us won’t go that far. We drift toward it a little at a time, one late night that turns into a habit. Work can be good and meaningful and still not be the whole of your life. Keep something that has nothing to do with it: people who don’t know what you do for a living, a weekend that stays a weekend. The work will still be there on Monday.
