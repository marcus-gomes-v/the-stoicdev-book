## July 31st - BALANCE YOUR WORK AND LIFE

***“How disgraceful is the lawyer whose dying breath passes while at court, at an advanced age, pleading for unknown litigants and still seeking the approval of ignorant spectators.”***
—SENECA, ON THE BREVITY OF LIFE, 20.2

In the tech industry, it’s easy to become consumed by the demands of your career, especially when you're passionate about your work. However, it's crucial to recognize the importance of balance. Consider the stories of those who refuse to step back, even as they age, leading to public struggles and personal humiliation. They serve as stark reminders that life is more than just a series of professional achievements.

You are not your job. Your role as a developer, manager, or entrepreneur is just one aspect of who you are. Focusing solely on work can lead to neglect of personal growth, relationships, and health.

Strive for a balanced life. Take time to nurture your interests, spend time with loved ones, and care for your well-being. Your career should support your life, not overshadow it.
