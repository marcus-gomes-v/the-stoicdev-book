August 1st - EMBRACE IMPERFECTION

“That cucumber is bitter, so toss it out! There are thorns on the path, then keep away! Enough said. Why ponder the existence of nuisance? Such thinking would make you a laughing-stock to the true student of Nature, just as a carpenter or cobbler would laugh if you pointed out the sawdust and chips on the floors of their shops. Yet while those shopkeepers have dustbins for disposal, Nature has no need of them.” 
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 8.50

In the tech field, we often strive for perfection, but the reality is that we work in an ever-changing, imperfect environment. Bugs, setbacks, and unexpected issues are part of the journey. Marcus Aurelius reminds us not to dwell on these nuisances. Just as a carpenter doesn't fret over sawdust, we shouldn't get bogged down by minor imperfections.

Rather than waiting for the perfect conditions, start now with what you have. Use every obstacle as an opportunity to improve and adapt. Focus on solutions, not problems. This mindset will not only help you navigate the daily challenges of your work but also enhance your ability to innovate and stay resilient. Embrace the imperfections, for they are an integral part of the path to success.
