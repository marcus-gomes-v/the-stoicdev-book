## August 1st - EMBRACE IMPERFECTION

***“A cucumber is bitter—Throw it away.—There are briers in the road—Turn aside from them.—This is enough. Do not add, And why were such things made in the world? For thou wilt be ridiculed by a man who is acquainted with nature, as thou wouldst be ridiculed by a carpenter and shoemaker if thou didst find fault because thou seest in their workshop shavings and cuttings from the things which they make. And yet they have places into which they can throw these shavings and cuttings, and the universal nature has no external space...”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 8.50

The flaky test fails again. Someone left a TODO in the payment module in 2019, and the staging database is three schema versions behind production. You could spend the morning asking why things are like this and who let it happen.

Or you could do what Marcus says. The test is bitter: quarantine it and open a ticket. There’s a brier in the road: step around it, and leave the module a little cleaner than you found it. That’s enough. Don’t add the speech about how nobody here cares about quality.

A carpenter isn’t offended by shavings on the workshop floor. They’re what work leaves behind. Odd workarounds and comments that apologize for the code below them are the sawdust of software that people actually use. Sweep up what’s in your way and get back to the bench.
