## August 2nd - ADAPT AND THRIVE

***“And as for looking after oneself and acquiring virtue, how could exile stand in the way? No one is kept by exile from learning what he ought to learn, or from putting it into practice.”***
—MUSONIUS RUFUS, LECTURES, 9

The reorg email lands on a Tuesday. Your team is dissolved, the new product you were excited about goes to another group, and you’re moved to internal tooling: the admin panel nobody demos, the cron jobs nobody understands. It feels like being sent away.

Nero banished Musonius to Gyaros, a bare rock of an island in the Aegean. This lecture is his answer to people who thought exile was the end of a life. Exile could take his city and his friends. It couldn’t stop him from learning, or from practicing what he had learned.

The unglamorous assignment works the same way. Nobody can keep you from reading those cron jobs until you understand them, from writing the docs that were never written, from becoming the one person who knows how the admin panel actually talks to the database. Those skills come with you to the next team, and the one after that.

You didn’t choose where you were sent. What you learn while you’re there is still yours.
