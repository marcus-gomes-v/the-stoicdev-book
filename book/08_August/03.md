## August 3rd - FIND PEACE WHERE YOU ARE

***“As things stand, you are not travelling; you are drifting, pushed along, trading one place for the next, when the thing you are after, living well, is there to be had in every place. Could anything be more chaotic than the Forum? Even there one can live quietly, if one has to.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 28.5-6

You tell yourself the problem is the open-plan office. The sales team next to you is on calls all day, and the kitchen doubles as a meeting room. If you could just work from home, you’d finally get some deep work done. Then you’re home, and the problem is the neighbor’s renovation, and after that it’s the new job, the new stack, the new city.

Seneca wrote this letter to a friend who kept traveling to cure his restlessness. His answer was that the restlessness packs its bags and comes along. What you’re looking for is available wherever you are, even in the Forum, the loudest place in Rome.

Headphones help. A door that closes helps. But at some point you have to sit down in the noise you’ve got and write the function. The developers who get real work done in chaotic places mostly stopped waiting for the perfect setup a long time ago.
