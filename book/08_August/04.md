## August 4th - SHIFT YOUR FOCUS

***“You must stop blaming God, and not blame any person. You must completely control your desire and shift your avoidance to what lies within your reasoned choice. You must no longer feel anger, resentment, envy, or regret.”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 3.22.13

In the tech world, we often face frustrating situations bugs that won't fix, clients that are hard to please, and unexpected downtime. It’s easy to place blame on others or circumstances. However, the true power lies in shifting our focus from what’s outside our control to what is within our reasoned choice.

Take inspiration from Nelson Mandela, who, despite being imprisoned for twenty-seven years, found ways to assert his will and maintain his dignity. He focused on what he could control his attitude, his actions, and his responses.

Today, when you encounter obstacles at work, remember that your energy is better spent on what you can control rather than on anger or blame. Focus on solutions, maintain your composure, and continue moving forward with determination and clarity.
