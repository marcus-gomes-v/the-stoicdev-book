## August 5th - THE POWER OF SILENCE

***“Silence is a lesson learned from the many sufferings of life.”***
—SENECA, THYESTES, 309

In the fast-paced world of technology, it's easy to feel the pressure to constantly share opinions or assert ideas. However, silence can be a powerful tool. Think back to a time when speaking impulsively led to regret. Often, we talk to fill the void or to seem knowledgeable, but this can lead to unnecessary mistakes.

As Robert Greene wisely said, "The more you say, the more likely you are to say something foolish." In tech, where precision and clarity are crucial, unnecessary talk can lead to confusion. Instead, practice the strength of silence. Listen more, observe, and choose your words carefully. Silence can build self-sufficiency and demonstrate confidence and control.

In your tech career, let your thoughtful actions and well-chosen words speak for you. Embrace the wisdom of silence as a path to greater clarity and strength.
