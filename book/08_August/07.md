August 7th - PRINCIPLES IN PRACTICE

“Wherever a person can live, there one can also live well; life is also in the demands of court, there too one can live well.” 
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 5.16

In the tech world, it’s easy to think that your environment dictates your behavior. High-pressure deadlines, office politics, and the race to innovate can seem like excuses to compromise on your principles. But Marcus Aurelius reminds us that you can live well anywhere, even in challenging environments.

Abraham Lincoln, often idolized for his integrity, was a politician—a profession many associate with compromise and corruption. Yet, he maintained his principles and led with compassion, fairness, and purpose. This is a powerful reminder that being principled and pragmatic are not mutually exclusive.

In your tech career, strive to be both innovative and ethical. Whether you're navigating the complexities of a startup or the bureaucracy of a large corporation, you can uphold your values. Lead by example, show that success doesn’t require sacrificing integrity, and create a work environment where ethical behavior is the norm.
