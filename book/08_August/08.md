## August 8th - START WITH WHERE THE WORLD IS

***“Do now what nature demands of you. Get right to it if that’s in your power. Don’t look around to see if people will know about it. Don’t await the perfection of Plato’s Republic, but be satisfied with even the smallest step forward and regard the outcome as a small thing.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 9.29.(4)

In the tech field, it's easy to get caught up in the pursuit of perfection waiting for the perfect moment, the ideal conditions, or the flawless plan. Yet, this mindset can lead to inaction and missed opportunities. The wisdom here is clear: start with where the world is, not where you wish it to be.

Saul Alinsky, a notable community organizer, emphasized beginning with reality as it stands, not as we desire. This practical approach doesn't diminish our aspirations; it merely grounds them in the attainable. For those in tech, this means iterating and improving continuously, rather than stalling for the perfect solution.

Today, take the smallest step forward. Implement that minor feature, fix that small bug, or write that bit of code. Each action, however small, contributes to progress. Don’t wait for ideal conditions; they rarely exist. Instead, start now and build incrementally. Remember, progress, no matter how modest, is better than stagnation.
