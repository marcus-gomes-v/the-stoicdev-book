August 10th - PERFECTION IS THE ENEMY OF ACTION

“We don’t abandon our pursuits because we despair of ever perfecting them.” 
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 1.2.37b

In the tech field, the drive for perfection can often paralyze progress. We delay releases, avoid tackling challenges, and procrastinate on projects because we fear they won't be flawless. This kind of all-or-nothing thinking, a common cognitive distortion, leads to frustration and stagnation.

Epictetus reminds us that the pursuit of progress should never be halted by the unattainable goal of perfection. In technology, continuous improvement and iterative development are key. Each version, each update, brings us closer to our goals, even if they are not perfect.

Today, focus on making progress rather than achieving perfection. Release that code update, tackle that challenge, and move forward with your projects. Accept that perfection is not the goal—progress is. By embracing this mindset, you'll find more satisfaction and success in your work and personal development.
