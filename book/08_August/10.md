## August 10th - PERFECTION IS THE ENEMY OF ACTION

***“Nor, in a word, is there any other field in which we give up the appropriate discipline merely from despair of attaining the highest.”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 1.2.37

You will probably never write a database engine like the people who built Postgres, or give the conference talk everyone quotes. That’s no reason to stop learning how query planners work, or to stop writing careful code in the small service you own.

Just before this line, Epictetus names two famous men. Milo was the great wrestler of legend, Croesus the king whose name meant wealth. Epictetus won’t be either of them, he says, and he still looks after his body and his property. Nobody gives up a discipline only because the very top is out of reach.

Developers do it all the time anyway. The side project stays private because it isn’t polished. The release slips another sprint because one edge case still bothers you. You don’t start learning a new language because you’ll never be an expert in it. Being a bit better than you were last month is a perfectly good reason to keep going. Ship the version that works.
