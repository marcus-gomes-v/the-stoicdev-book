## August 11th - NO TIME FOR THEORIES, JUST RESULTS

***“Once more the question came up among us: for gaining virtue, which accomplishes more, habit or theory? Here theory meant the teaching that shows correctly what must be done, and habit meant growing used to acting by that teaching. Musonius held that habit accomplishes more.”***
—MUSONIUS RUFUS, LECTURES, 5

Musonius argued this with a question. Picture two doctors. One talks about medicine brilliantly but has hardly treated anyone. The other speaks poorly but has treated patients for years. Which one do you want at your bedside? His students chose the second, and so would you.

Developers know both kinds. One colleague can explain every design pattern on a whiteboard. Another has been on call for five years and can spot a memory leak from the shape of a graph. During an outage, you know whose phone you’re calling.

Theory still matters, since the good doctor learned from it once. But reading about testing doesn’t make you someone who tests. Writing a test before every bug fix, for months, until skipping it feels strange, is what does that.
