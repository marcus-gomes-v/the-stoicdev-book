## August 12th - OWN YOUR ACTIONS, NOT JUST YOUR WORDS

***“Many words have been spoken by Plato, Zeno, Chrysippus, Posidonius, and by a whole host of equally excellent Stoics. I’ll tell you how people can prove their words to be their own—by putting into practice what they’ve been preaching.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 108.35; 38

In the tech world, ideas and theories are plentiful, but the real value comes from implementation. Much like the Stoics who borrowed and built upon each other's philosophies, tech professionals often work with shared knowledge frameworks, languages, and tools that have been refined over time.

But knowledge alone doesn’t set you apart. What does is how you apply it. Whether you’re following a coding best practice or adopting a project management methodology, what matters is how you execute these ideas in your work. It’s easy to quote industry leaders or follow the latest trends, but the true test is in the results you produce.

Today, focus on making these borrowed ideas your own through your actions. Don’t just talk about best practices demonstrate them in your work. Let your code, your leadership, and your problem-solving skills speak louder than any words. This is how you truly own the knowledge and prove its value.
