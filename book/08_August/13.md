## August 13th - TAKE CONTROL AND END YOUR STRUGGLES

***“Thou hast endured infinite troubles through not being contented with thy ruling faculty when it does the things which it is constituted by nature to do. But enough [of this].”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 9.26

Count the bad weeks that started with you ignoring your own judgment. You knew the migration needed a dry run, and you skipped it because the release train was leaving. You knew three days wasn’t enough, and you said yes because the room wanted a yes. You knew the reply was too sharp, and you sent it anyway.

In each case your reason did its job. It saw the problem. What went wrong is that you didn’t trust it, or you let fear and impatience talk over it. Marcus, writing to himself, sounds tired of the same pattern: endless trouble, all from not being content to let the ruling part of him do what it was made for. Then he cuts himself off. Enough.

The fix is small and a little annoying. Listen to the voice that already says “wait, run it on staging first,” and do what it tells you.
