## August 14th - PHILOSOPHY IS FOR LIVING, NOT FOR SHOW

***“Philosophy isn’t a parlor trick or made for show. It’s not concerned with words, but with facts. It’s not employed for some pleasure before the day is spent, or to relieve the uneasiness of our leisure. It shapes and builds up the soul, it gives order to life, guides action, shows what should and shouldn’t be done—it sits at the rudder steering our course as we vacillate in uncertainties. Without it, no one can live without fear or free from care. Countless things happen every hour that require advice, and such advice is to be sought out in philosophy.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 16.3

In the fast-paced world of technology, where every decision can feel like it’s made on the fly, it’s easy to overlook the deeper questions that should guide our actions. Are you making choices based on fleeting trends or enduring principles? Are your solutions focused on the immediate problem or on long-term stability?

Philosophy, as Seneca reminds us, is not just an intellectual exercise or a way to pass the time. It’s a toolkit for living a guide for making decisions that are sound, just, and aligned with a higher purpose. In tech, as in life, the challenges you face daily require more than quick fixes; they demand wisdom and foresight.

So, when you’re tempted to take shortcuts or to treat serious issues as a game of wits, remember: philosophy is your compass, not a distraction. It’s there to help you navigate complexities and uncertainties with clarity and purpose. Use it to steer your life and work in the right direction.
