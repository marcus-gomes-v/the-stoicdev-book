August 15th - THE SUPREME COURT OF YOUR MIND

“This can be swiftly taught in very few words: virtue is the only good; there is no certain good without virtue; and virtue resides in our nobler part, which is the rational one. And what can this virtue be? True and steadfast judgment. For from this will arise every mental impulse, and by it every appearance that spurs our impulses will be rendered clear.” 
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 71.32

In the tech industry, where rapid decisions are often made in the heat of the moment, it's easy to lose sight of the core principles that should guide our actions. Yet, just as a Supreme Court upholds the law, your mind must uphold virtue as the ultimate standard.

Virtue, according to Seneca, is the foundation of all good. It’s the rational part of you that must preside over every decision, ensuring that what drives you is not momentary impulse but steadfast judgment. This is what makes a person reliable and trustworthy—qualities that are essential not just in personal relationships, but in professional environments as well.

Think of your mind as a court where every thought, every decision, is judged by the highest standard of virtue. When you consistently act with integrity, courage, and wisdom, you build a reputation and a life that others admire and trust. In the fast-moving world of tech, this kind of consistency is not just an asset—it’s a necessity. Choose your actions wisely, and let virtue be the judge.
