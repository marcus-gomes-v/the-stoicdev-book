## August 16th - TURNING OBSTACLES INTO OPPORTUNITIES

***“As the nature of the universal has given to every rational being all the other powers that it has, so we have received from it this power also. For as the universal nature converts and fixes in its predestined place everything which stands in the way and opposes it, and makes such things a part of itself, so also the rational animal is able to make every hindrance its own material, and to use it for such purposes as it may have designed.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 8.35

A team is told it can’t use the cloud provider everyone else uses. The client is a hospital, the data can’t leave the building, and the budget is small. For a month the developers complain. Then one of them starts reading about running the whole stack on two servers in a basement, and a year later that is what the company is known for.

Marcus sees the same thing in nature, which takes whatever opposes it and makes it part of itself. He says we have that power too. A reasoning person can turn every hindrance into material for their own purpose.

You’ve probably watched it happen on a smaller scale. The developer stuck with the flaky CI pipeline nobody wanted ends up understanding the build better than anyone on the team. The outage that embarrassed everyone produces the alerting that saves the next release.

The obstacle rarely goes away. It becomes the material you work with, and sometimes it turns into the best thing you build.
