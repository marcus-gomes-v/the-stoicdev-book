August 17th - TAKE RESPONSIBILITY

“For nothing outside my reasoned choice can hinder or harm it—my reasoned choice alone can do this to itself. If we would lean this way whenever we fail, and would blame only ourselves and remember that nothing but opinion is the cause of a troubled mind and uneasiness, then by God, I swear we would be making progress.” 
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 3.19.2-3

In the tech industry, where collaboration is key and stakes are often high, it’s easy to fall into the trap of blaming others or external factors when things go wrong. A bug slips through, a teammate misunderstands your instructions, or a project misses its deadline—how often do we point outward instead of inward?

Epictetus reminds us that our reasoned choice—the way we think, react, and decide—holds the true power over our mindset and actions. Today, challenge yourself to take full responsibility for your reactions and choices. If something goes awry, reflect on how you contributed to the situation or how you can respond constructively, rather than blaming others or external events.

Start small. Take ownership of a single moment when you might otherwise assign blame. Over time, this practice strengthens your ability to lead with accountability, fostering growth in both yourself and those around you. Responsibility is the foundation of progress, both in work and in life.
