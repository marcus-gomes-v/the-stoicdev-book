## August 18th - CHOOSE YOUR BATTLES WISELY

***“A good person is invincible, for they don’t rush into contests in which they aren’t the strongest. If you want their property, take it—take also their staff, profession, and body. But you will never compel what they set out for, nor trap them in what they would avoid. For the only contest the good person enters is that of their own reasoned choice. How can such a person not be invincible?”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 3.6.5–7

In tech, as in life, rushing into challenges without thought often leads to unnecessary failure. Taking on tasks without preparation, saying yes to every demand, or fighting battles that don’t align with your strengths can waste time and energy. Epictetus reminds us that true strength lies in deliberate, reasoned choice not in reckless bravado.

Choosing your battles isn’t weakness; it’s strategy. It’s recognizing where your skills and values align with the challenge at hand and where your efforts will make the greatest impact. Discretion doesn’t mean avoiding hard work it means focusing on the right work.

Today, take a moment to assess the tasks or challenges ahead. Are you engaging where you can truly contribute and grow, or are you letting impulse guide you into unnecessary contests? By thinking carefully before acting, you make yourself invincible not because you never face difficulty, but because you only engage with purpose and reason.
