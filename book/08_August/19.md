## August 19th - SIMPLIFY TO FOCUS

***“It is said that if you would have peace of mind, busy yourself with little. But wouldn’t a better saying be do what you must and as required of a rational being created for public life? For this brings not only the peace of mind of doing few things, but the greater peace of doing them well. Since the vast majority of our words and actions are unnecessary, corralling them will create an abundance of leisure and tranquility. As a result, we shouldn’t forget at each moment to ask, is this one of the unnecessary things? But we must corral not only unnecessary actions but unnecessary thoughts, too, so needless acts don’t tag along after them.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 4.24

In the tech world, where demands can feel endless and distractions plentiful, it's easy to become overwhelmed by the sheer volume of tasks, meetings, and thoughts vying for attention. Marcus Aurelius reminds us that tranquility doesn’t come from doing less, but from focusing on what truly matters and doing it well.

Today, take a moment to identify what is unnecessary in your schedule, projects, or even your thoughts. Are there meetings that could be emails? Tasks driven by ego rather than purpose? Ideas you’re overthinking without action? By cutting these, you free up energy for the essential delivering quality work, solving meaningful problems, and cultivating a calmer, more productive mindset.

Simplify to focus. Tranquility and effectiveness thrive not in doing everything, but in doing the right things with clarity and intention.
