## August 20th - CHANGE WHERE IT MATTERS

***“Let everything within be different; let the face we show the public look like theirs.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 5.2

In this letter Seneca warns a young friend against dressing up as a philosopher: the rough cloak, the uncombed hair, the bed on the floor. People who do that, he says, want to be noticed more than they want to improve.

Developers have their own version. The custom keyboard, the sigh when someone opens a GUI for git. None of it is wrong. It just has nothing to do with whether the code is any good, and it can make the people around you feel judged before you’ve said a word.

Seneca’s alternative is to look ordinary and be different inside. Use the same tools as your team without complaining. Then be the one who reads the whole diff, and who stays patient with the new hire asking the same question for the second time. Nobody will notice your setup. They will notice that the things you touch tend to work.
