## August 21st - DON’T SUFFER TWICE

***“A mind that frets about the future is in a sorry state, unhappy before there is anything to be unhappy about, anxious that the things it enjoys should last right to the end. It will never find rest, and in waiting for what is to come it will lose the present things it could have enjoyed.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 98.5-6

Layoff rumors start in late autumn, the way they do at a lot of companies. Nobody knows anything. You start checking the org chart, reading the CEO’s posts for hidden meanings, refreshing the anonymous forum at midnight. Three months later nothing has happened, and you’ve spent those three months only half at work.

Seneca has a phrase for this: being miserable before the misery. And the worry is usually about losing what you like, the team you finally fit into, the manager who has your back. The strange cost is that you stop enjoying those things while you still have them.

If the bad news comes, you’ll deal with it then, and you’ll deal with it better rested. Until it does, the team is still here and the project is still yours. Go to the planning meeting and actually be in it.
