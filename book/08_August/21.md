## August 21st - DON’T SUFFER TWICE

***“It’s ruinous for the soul to be anxious about the future and miserable in advance of misery, engulfed by anxiety that the things it desires might remain its own until the very end. For such a soul will never be at rest—by longing for things to come it will lose the ability to enjoy present things.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 98.5b-6a

In the tech world, where uncertainty is constant whether it’s awaiting a product launch, debugging a critical issue, or preparing for a presentation it’s easy to let anxiety about the future rob you of the present. But as Seneca reminds us, worrying about what hasn’t happened yet is not only unproductive but also voluntary misery.

Instead of obsessing over worst-case scenarios, focus on what you can do now. If bad news comes, you’ll face it when it arrives but don’t let it take away the value of today. Use your energy to work on solutions, learn something new, or make progress on meaningful tasks. Worrying won’t change the future, but working with focus and purpose will prepare you for it.

Let the future unfold as it will. Be too engaged with the present to waste time on needless worry.
