August 22nd - FOCUS ON WHAT MATTERS

“It is essential for you to remember that the attention you give to any action should be in due proportion to its worth, for then you won’t tire and give up, if you aren’t busying yourself with lesser things beyond what should be allowed.” 
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 4.32b

In the tech world, where countless notifications, meetings, and tasks demand your attention, it’s easy to get bogged down by the small stuff. Marcus Aurelius and Richard Carlson offer the same advice: prioritize wisely and don’t let minor matters consume your energy or time.

Not everything on your to-do list carries equal weight. That endless email chain, the minor bug, or the office politics—these things often feel urgent but are rarely important. By overinvesting in them, you drain time and energy from what truly matters: meaningful work, your well-being, and the relationships that sustain you.

Today, assess your tasks and commitments. Which ones align with your long-term goals? Which are distractions? Focus on what truly deserves your energy, and let the rest fade into the background. By doing so, you not only protect your productivity but also preserve the balance that makes life fulfilling.
