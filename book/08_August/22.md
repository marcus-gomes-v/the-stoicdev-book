## August 22nd - FOCUS ON WHAT MATTERS

***“It is necessary to remember that the attention given to everything has its proper value and proportion. For thus thou wilt not be dissatisfied, if thou appliest thyself to smaller matters no further than is fit.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 4.32

Every developer has lost an afternoon to something small. A forty-comment thread about whether to allow trailing commas. A code review where a typo and a data-loss bug got exactly the same amount of energy.

Marcus writes this right after picturing whole generations who wore themselves out over idle things and then disappeared. His fix is a sense of proportion. Each thing deserves a certain amount of your attention and no more. Give the small things only what they’re worth, and you won’t end the week drained and oddly dissatisfied without knowing why.

It helps to size attention the way you size tickets. The trailing comma is a one: state your preference once, then let it go. The data-loss bug is a thirteen. That’s where the long thread, the careful review and the late night belong, if anything does. Most of what fills a week is ones and twos, and it can be treated that way.
