## August 24th - WISDOM KNOWS NO BOUNDARIES

***“I shall never be ashamed to quote a good saying because it comes from a bad author.”***
—SENECA, ON TRANQUILITY OF MIND, 11.8

The bad author Seneca has in mind is Publilius, who wrote mimes, the low comedy of the Roman stage. Serious people weren’t supposed to quote him. Seneca did anyway, because now and then Publilius wrote a line too noble even for tragedy.

Developers are snobs in their own ways. We dismiss an idea because it came from a language we look down on, a framework we’ve sworn off, or a support agent who “isn’t technical.” Meanwhile the support agent has read three hundred tickets about the bug we can’t reproduce.

Where an idea comes from tells you something, but it doesn’t tell you whether it’s right. The junior developer’s question in review can be the one that finds the race condition. A pattern from a stack you dislike can solve your problem cleanly. Take the good line, credit the person it came from, and leave your pride out of it.
