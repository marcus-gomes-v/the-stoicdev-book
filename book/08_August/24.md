## August 24th - WISDOM KNOWS NO BOUNDARIES

***“I’ll never be ashamed to quote a bad writer with a good saying.”***
—SENECA, ON TRANQUILITY OF MIND, 11.8
In the tech field, where innovation thrives on collaboration and shared ideas, the source of wisdom should matter less than its value. Seneca often quoted Epicurus, despite their philosophical differences, because what mattered was the quality of the thought, not its origin.

Today, embrace this mindset. Whether it’s a junior developer’s suggestion, a methodology from a competing philosophy, or advice from a field outside of tech, focus on the merit of the idea. Does it improve your work? Does it enhance your perspective? If so, it’s worth adopting, regardless of its source.

By shedding biases and focusing on merit, you open yourself to a wealth of insights and solutions that might otherwise be overlooked. Let wisdom guide you, no matter where it comes from.
