## August 25th - HONOR THE OLD, INNOVATE THE NEW
***“Won’t you be walking in your predecessors’ footsteps? I surely will use the older path, but if I find a shorter and smoother way, I’ll blaze a trail there. The ones who pioneered these paths aren’t our masters, but our guides. Truth stands open to everyone, it hasn’t been monopolized.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 33.11

In the tech industry, the foundations we build on algorithms, programming languages, methodologies are the legacies of pioneers who came before us. Their work is invaluable, but as Seneca reminds us, they are guides, not masters. Progress requires respecting what works while staying open to new, better ways.

Maybe it’s adopting a new framework that streamlines your workflow, challenging a long-standing process to find a more efficient one, or integrating emerging technologies. Clinging to tradition for its own sake stifles innovation, while thoughtful experimentation drives progress.

Today, honor the past by learning from it, but don’t be afraid to question or improve on it. True growth happens when we balance respect for established wisdom with the courage to embrace new possibilities.
