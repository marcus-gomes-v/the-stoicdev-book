## August 26th - TURNING LOSS INTO A NEW PATH

***“I was wrecked before I ever set foot on the ship... In the meantime this journey has taught me how many things we own that we do not need, and how easily we could choose to set down things that we never miss when necessity takes them away.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 87.1

Seneca never says what the shipwreck was. He only tells us that he’s on the road with his friend Maximus, a few servants, one carriage, a mattress on the ground and two cloaks, one to lie on and one to cover himself with. He says the two days so far have been very happy ones.

Most developers have lived a smaller version of this. Your laptop dies the week before a release, and you spend a few days on a loaner with nothing installed. No custom aliases, no carefully tuned editor. Somehow you ship anyway, and you notice how little of that setup you actually reached for.

Bigger losses work the same way, only more painfully. A layoff takes away the title and the stock. It also shows you which things you needed and which you were carrying out of habit. You can’t choose the shipwreck. You can choose to look at what’s left and see how much of it is enough.
