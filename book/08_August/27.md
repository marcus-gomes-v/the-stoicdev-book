## August 27th - FIND HUMOR IN CHALLENGES

***“Heraclitus would shed tears whenever he went out in public—Democritus laughed. One saw the whole as a parade of miseries, the other of follies. And so, we should take a lighter view of things and bear them with an easy spirit, for it is more human to laugh at life than to lament it.”***
—SENECA, ON TRANQUILITY OF MIND, 15.2

In the high-pressure world of tech, frustrations abound bugs that won’t resolve, team miscommunications, or tools that don’t work as expected. It’s tempting to let these challenges weigh you down, but Seneca reminds us that we always have a choice: laugh or cry.

Choosing laughter doesn’t mean dismissing the seriousness of your work; it means approaching problems with a lighter spirit. Humor can break the tension, foster creativity, and improve collaboration. When you laugh at life’s absurdities, you gain the clarity to focus on solutions instead of dwelling on the setbacks.

Today, as challenges arise, practice seeing the humor in them. A misstep in a presentation or an unexpected error in the system can be met with a smile instead of stress. Laughter lightens the load and makes the journey a bit more enjoyable for you and your team.
