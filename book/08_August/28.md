August 28th - LUXURY WITHOUT DEPENDENCE

“The founder of the universe, who assigned to us the laws of life, provided that we should live well, but not in luxury. Everything needed for our well-being is right before us, whereas what luxury requires is gathered by many miseries and anxieties. Let us use this gift of nature and count it among the greatest things.”
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 119.15b

In the modern tech world, where success often brings material wealth, Seneca’s reflection on luxury versus necessity remains deeply relevant. Wealth isn’t inherently bad, but being enslaved by it is. Many high earners find themselves trapped in endless cycles of work, funding lifestyles they don’t truly need, while sacrificing their peace of mind.

Seneca’s approach offers a pragmatic balance. You can achieve financial success without becoming dependent on excess. Wealth can be a tool, not a master—something you use to support meaningful pursuits, not a constant source of anxiety.

Today, reflect on what truly brings you happiness and fulfillment. Are your pursuits aligned with what matters, or are you chasing luxuries that add unnecessary burdens? Live well, but with intention, and let your wealth, however modest or abundant, serve your deeper values rather than define them
