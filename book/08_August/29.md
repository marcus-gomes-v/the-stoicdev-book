## August 29th - TRUE WEALTH IS DESIRING LESS

***“No one can have everything they want. What anyone can do is stop wanting what they lack, and make glad use of whatever comes to hand.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 123.3

Seneca wrote this after arriving late at night at his country house, worn out from the road, to find that the baker had no bread. He decided to wait. Hunger, he said, would make even poor bread taste soft and fine.

Developers are rarely short of bread, but we are very good at wanting. The new laptop, the team with the more interesting stack. There is always a tool you’d be twice as productive with, if only procurement would approve it. Some of that wanting is healthy. It’s part of what pushes you to grow.

The trouble starts when the wanting gets louder than the work. The machine you have compiles the code. The team you’re on has problems worth solving. The free tier of the monitoring tool will tell you a lot if you take an afternoon to set it up properly. Make good use of what’s in front of you first, and then see how much of the wanting is left.
