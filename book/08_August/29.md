## August 29th - TRUE WEALTH IS DESIRING LESS

***“No person has the power to have everything they want, but it is in their power not to want what they don’t have, and to cheerfully put to good use what they do have.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 123.3

In the tech industry, ambition often drives innovation and success, but it can also create an insatiable desire for more more recognition, more tools, more projects. Yet, even the most successful professionals find that there are limits to what money, status, or influence can buy. True satisfaction comes not from having everything but from wanting less.

As John D. Rockefeller wisely noted, wealth is not measured by income but by the alignment of desires with what one already has. This is a shortcut to contentment that bypasses the endless chase for more.

Today, pause and reflect: Are you chasing something you don’t truly need? Instead of striving to accumulate, focus on appreciating and maximizing what you already have your skills, tools, and opportunities. By wanting less, you gain the freedom and peace of mind to focus on what truly matters.


.August 30th - ALIGN YOUR EFFORTS WITH PURPOSE

***“Anything that must yet be done, virtue can do with courage and promptness. For anyone would call it a sign of foolishness for one to undertake a task with a lazy and begrudging spirit, or to push the body in one direction and the mind in another, to be torn apart by wildly divergent impulses.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 31.b-32

When you feel yourself dragging your feet on a task, ask yourself: Why am I doing this? Is it a necessity, or is it misaligned with your values and priorities? If it’s truly essential, focus on realigning your mind with the task at hand. Address your reluctance whether it’s driven by fear, frustration, or simple fatigue and channel your energy into purposeful action.

In tech, where focus and precision are key, doing something begrudgingly often leads to mistakes and wasted effort. Don’t be the person who says yes to a task but approaches it with half-hearted effort. As Steve Jobs wisely said, “Quality is much better than quantity.” 

Today, tackle your work with clarity and intention. If the task matters, commit fully. By aligning your actions and mindset, you’ll not only produce better results but also find greater satisfaction in the process.
