## August 30th - ALIGN YOUR EFFORTS WITH PURPOSE

***“Whatever has to be done, the wise man will do boldly and without delay. For this, one might say, is the mark of folly: to do what you do sluggishly and resentfully, to drive the body one way and the mind another, to be pulled apart between impulses that run in opposite directions.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 74.31-32

You said yes to the migration in sprint planning. Now it’s Thursday, and you’ve opened the ticket four times, read the first paragraph, and gone back to chat. Your hands are on the keyboard. Your head is still in Monday’s argument about whether this migration was needed at all.

Seneca calls that folly: the body pushed one way, the mind another. It’s also where a lot of careless bugs come from. Code you write while quietly resenting it gets the minimum attention, and it shows in review.

There are two honest ways out. If the work really shouldn’t be done, go back and say so, with reasons, before you sink another week into it. If it should, or if the decision is made and you’ve accepted it, then do it properly and do it now. Halfway between those two is the worst place to be.
