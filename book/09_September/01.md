## September 1st - A STRONG SOUL OUTLASTS FORTUNE

***“For the mind is stronger than any fortune. It steers its own affairs one way or the other, and it is the author of its own happy or wretched life.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 98.2

Two developers get the same news on the same Tuesday: the product they spent a year building is being shut down. One spends the next month bitter, refreshing job boards and telling anyone who will listen that leadership never understood the work. The other is disappointed too, for a week or so. Then she writes up what the team learned, cleans up the parts worth keeping, and starts asking which project needs help.

Same event. Two very different months.

Seneca’s claim sounds too strong at first. He doesn’t mean you can stop the reorg. Fortune hands you the material, good or bad, and the mind decides what gets built from it. Whether this becomes the year you were wronged or the year you learned something is settled by what you do next, and that part was never in the memo.
