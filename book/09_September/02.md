## September 2nd - PHILOSOPHY AS REHAB FOR THE SOUL

***“Men, the philosopher’s lecture-hall is a hospital—you shouldn’t walk out of it feeling pleasure, but pain, for you aren’t well when you enter it.”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 3.23.30

Just like physical therapy focuses on rebuilding strength where injury or neglect has caused weakness, philosophy exists to address the weaknesses in our minds and character. It’s not designed to comfort you it’s designed to challenge you, to push you into discomfort that spurs growth and resilience.

In the tech industry, where constant adaptation and problem-solving are part of the job, Stoicism works like this mental rehab. It forces you to confront your impatience during delays, your frustration with teammates, or your fear of failure. It’s not always pleasant, but it’s necessary to build the mental toughness and emotional resilience needed to thrive.

Today, embrace the moments of discomfort and reflection. Let them strengthen the parts of you that need growth. Like rehab, the process might sting, but the results endurance, clarity, and fortitude are well worth it.
