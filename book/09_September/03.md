September 3rd - PREPARE LIKE IT MATTERS

“We must undergo a hard winter training and not rush into things for which we haven’t prepared.” 
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 1.2.32

Success in tech, like in any field, doesn’t come from halfhearted effort or sporadic dedication. Epictetus’s "hard winter training" reminds us that consistent preparation is essential. Just as soldiers train rigorously in peacetime to be ready for war, and athletes like LeBron James dedicate their off-seasons to improvement, we must constantly sharpen our skills and build resilience for the challenges life throws at us.

In tech, this means mastering foundational skills, staying updated with emerging trends, and tackling projects with deliberate focus. Don’t wait for a crisis to force your growth—use every "off-season" moment to train yourself for what’s next. When challenges arise, your preparation will ensure you meet them with strength, clarity, and competence. Life doesn’t take breaks, so neither should your commitment to self-improvement. Be ready for whatever comes.
