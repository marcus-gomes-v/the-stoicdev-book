## September 3rd - PREPARE LIKE IT MATTERS

***“Yet a bull does not become a bull all at once, any more than a man becomes noble, but a man must undergo a winter training, he must prepare himself and must not plunge recklessly into what is inappropriate for him.”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 1.2.32

Roman armies usually stopped fighting in winter and went into quarters. The ones that kept drilling through the cold months were the ones that meant it. That’s the picture behind Epictetus’s phrase, and his bull makes the same point: nobody becomes formidable all at once.

The engineer who calmly runs an incident call didn’t get there in a day either. They spent a lot of unremarkable evenings reading postmortems, learning how the database actually handles locks, writing small tools nobody asked for. When the big moment came, it looked like talent. Mostly it was winter training.

The second half of his sentence matters just as much. Don’t plunge into what you aren’t ready for. Volunteering to lead the migration because it sounds impressive, before you’ve read the code it touches, isn’t courage. Do the quiet work in the quiet months, while nobody is watching and nothing is on fire.
