## September 4th - STRENGTH IS FORGED IN ADVERSITY

***“I judge you unfortunate because you have never lived through misfortune. You have passed through life without an opponent—no one can ever know what you are capable of, not even you.”***
—SENECA, ON PROVIDENCE, 4.3

In the tech industry, setbacks are inevitable failed launches, bugs in critical systems, or moments when your skills are pushed to their limits. While frustrating, these challenges reveal your true capacity. Without them, you might never know the full extent of your resilience or creativity.

Think about your most difficult projects or moments of uncertainty. Chances are, they taught you far more than the easy wins ever did. They forced you to innovate, to collaborate under pressure, and to discover reserves of patience and determination you didn’t know you had.
Today, when faced with adversity, remind yourself: this is an opportunity to grow stronger. Misfortune isn’t just a test it’s a lesson and a chance to prove to yourself what you’re truly capable of. Embrace it as part of the journey.
