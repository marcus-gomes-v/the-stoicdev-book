## September 4th - STRENGTH IS FORGED IN ADVERSITY

***“I think you unhappy because you never have been unhappy: you have passed through your life without meeting an antagonist: no one will know your powers, not even you yourself.”***
—SENECA, ON PROVIDENCE, 4.3

Some careers look perfect from the outside. Good school, good first job, every project shipped on time, promotions right on schedule. Seneca would feel a little sorry for that person, and he wouldn’t be joking.

His point is that you can’t know what you’re made of until something pushes back. The developer who has never been paged at 3 a.m. for a system they barely understand doesn’t yet know whether they stay calm or freeze. The lead who has never had to tell a team their project was cancelled doesn’t know if they can do it with honesty and care.

So when the hard project lands on your desk, or the launch goes badly, try seeing it his way. Here, finally, is an opponent. However it turns out, you’ll come away knowing something about yourself that the easy years could never have told you.
