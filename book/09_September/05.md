## September 5th - MASTER WHAT IS YOURS TO CONTROL

***“Remember, therefore, that if what is naturally slavish you think to be free, and what is not your own to be your own, you will be hampered, will grieve, will be in turmoil, and will blame both gods and men; while if you think only what is your own to be your own, and what is not your own to be, as it really is, not your own, then no one will ever be able to exert compulsion upon you, no one will hinder you, you will blame no one, will find fault with no one, will do absolutely nothing against your will, you will have no personal enemy, no one will harm you, for neither is there any harm that can touch you.”***
—EPICTETUS, ENCHIRIDION, 1.3

Look at how much of a bad week goes into things that were never yours. Whether the VP likes the roadmap. Whether the other team ships their API on time. Whether the recruiter calls back. You can influence some of that a little, but you don’t own it, and treating it as yours is how you end up, in Epictetus’s words, hampered, grieving and blaming everyone.

What is yours is a short list and a very solid one: your judgments, your choices, what you want and what you avoid. It sounds narrow until you actually live by it.

You can’t make the other team ship. You can write the adapter so your code survives their delay, tell your lead early, and stop rehearsing the argument in the shower. Nobody can block that part of the job. And once you stop claiming what isn’t yours, there’s a lot less left to be angry about, and a lot less anyone can take.
