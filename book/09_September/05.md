September 5th - MASTER WHAT IS YOURS TO CONTROL

“Remember, then, if you deem what is by nature slavish to be free, and what is not your own to be yours, you will be shackled and miserable, blaming both gods and other people. But if you deem as your own only what is yours, and what belongs to others as truly not yours, then no one will ever be able to coerce or to stop you, you will find no one to blame or accuse, you will do nothing against your will, you will have no enemy, no one will harm you, because no harm can affect you.” 
—EPICTETUS, ENCHIRIDION, 1.3

Captain James Stockdale’s harrowing years as a prisoner of war revealed the core of Stoic philosophy: focus on what you can control. In the face of brutal captivity, he understood that while his captors could control his body, his mind, his choices, and his will remained his own. By letting go of false hopes and unrealistic expectations, he channeled his energy into enduring and resisting in ways that were within his power.

In tech, where shifting priorities, unexpected bugs, or external pressures are the norm, adopting Stockdale’s mindset is invaluable. Don’t waste energy on what lies beyond your influence—like market fluctuations or unrealistic client demands. Instead, focus on what is yours to control: your effort, your problem-solving approach, and your ability to stay composed.

Today, remind yourself of what’s within your sphere of control. Master those elements, and let the rest unfold as it will. That’s how you remain steady, resilient, and unshaken.
