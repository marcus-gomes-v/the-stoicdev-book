## September 6th - FREEDOM BEYOND CHAINS

***“You can bind up my leg, but not even Zeus has the power to break my freedom of choice.”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 1.1.23

Even in the harshest conditions, Epictetus, James Stockdale, and John McCain demonstrated a truth that cannot be taken away: the freedom to choose their principles and actions. Despite physical chains and unimaginable suffering, they refused to let their captors dictate their character or values.

In the tech world, while the stakes may not be as severe, challenges and pressures can feel constraining. Deadlines, difficult clients, or organizational politics might make you feel powerless. But no external factor can take away your ability to choose how you respond, the values you hold, or the effort you bring to the table.

Today, remind yourself that your freedom lies in your decisions. No matter the external pressure, you control your attitude, your integrity, and your choices. That’s a freedom no one can take from you unless you allow it. Hold onto it tightly.
