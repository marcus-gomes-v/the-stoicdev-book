## September 6th - FREEDOM BEYOND CHAINS

***“My leg you will fetter, but my moral purpose not even Zeus himself has power to overcome.”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 1.1.23

Epictetus knew what a chain was. He grew up as someone else’s property and walked with a lame leg for most of his life. When he says the leg can be fettered but not the will, he isn’t speaking loosely.

Our version is far milder, and it still feels like a cage some days. The architecture was decided before you joined. The deadline came down from sales. The company picks your tools, your hours, sometimes even the words you may use with customers.

All of that can be true, and none of it reaches the part he cared about. You still decide whether to give the honest estimate or the comfortable one. You still decide whether to cut a corner you’d be ashamed of, and how to treat the junior developer who is just as stuck as you are. A lot can be taken from you at work. Those choices can’t, unless you hand them over.
