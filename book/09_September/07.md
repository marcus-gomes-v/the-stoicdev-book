## September 7th - YOUR POWER TO CHOOSE

***“Consider who you are. To begin with, a Man; that is, one who has no quality more sovereign than moral choice, but keeps everything else subordinate to it, and this moral choice itself free from slavery and subjection.”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 2.10.1

Job titles do a lot of work in our heads. Senior, staff, principal, lead. We introduce ourselves with them and measure ourselves against them, and when one disappears in a reorg it can feel like losing a piece of who we are.

Epictetus starts earlier than that. Before you’re a backend engineer or a manager, you’re a human being, and what makes you one is your power to choose. Everything else is meant to sit underneath it: your skills, your reputation, your salary, the logo on your badge.

That order is useful on a hard day. The promotion went to someone else. It hurts, and it’s worth talking about honestly. But it didn’t touch the thing at the top. You still decide how you treat the person who got it, whether you keep doing careful work, whether you ask for clear feedback or sulk. Nobody else holds that, and nobody else can make it a slave.
