September 7th - YOUR POWER TO CHOOSE

“Consider who you are. Above all, a human being, carrying no greater power than your own reasoned choice, which oversees all other things, and is free from any other master.” 
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 2.10.1

Viktor Frankl, enduring the horrors of concentration camps, discovered that even when everything else is stripped away, one unshakable freedom remains: the ability to choose your response and assign meaning to your circumstances. This hidden power, rooted in reason and choice, is the foundation of resilience.

In the tech industry, where challenges can range from high-pressure deadlines to navigating company politics, this same power is yours to wield. You can’t control every external factor—system failures, unexpected client demands, or team dynamics—but you can control how you respond. Will you complain, or will you adapt? Will you see challenges as setbacks, or as opportunities to grow?

Today, take stock of the choices available to you, no matter how constrained the situation may feel. Use your reason to guide your actions and seek the positives where others only see problems. Your power lies not in controlling everything, but in mastering your responses to everything.
