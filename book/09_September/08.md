## September 8th - STAY STEADY, COME WHAT MAY

***“No one is crushed by Fortune, unless they are first deceived by her . . . those who aren’t pompous in good times, don’t have their bubbles burst with change. Against either circumstance, the stable person keeps their rational soul invincible, for it’s precisely in the good times they prove their strength against adversity.”***
—SENECA, ON CONSOLATION TO HELVIA, 5.4b, 5b-6

In the tech industry, where fortunes can change rapidly startups rise and fall, markets fluctuate, and projects fail or succeed overnight it’s easy to get swept up in the highs and crushed by the lows. Seneca reminds us that the key to stability is detachment from the whims of fortune, whether favorable or unfavorable.

Seneca himself experienced both exile and political power, and it was his commitment to Stoic philosophy that kept him grounded through each turn of fate. The same lesson applies to your career. Success should be met with humility and restraint, not overconfidence. Failure should be met with patience and reflection, not despair.

Today, as you navigate the inevitable ups and downs, practice maintaining a steady course. Celebrate victories without becoming dependent on them. Face setbacks without letting them define you. True strength lies in a rational soul that stays invincible, regardless of fortune's twists.
