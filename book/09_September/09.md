September 9th - FEAR MAKES EVERYTHING WORSE

“But there is no reason to live and no limit to our miseries if we let our fears predominate.” 
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 13.12b

In the tech world, challenges and uncertainties—whether a failing project, a missed deadline, or a sudden shift in the market—can spark fear. But as FDR wisely pointed out, it’s often the fear itself, not the situation, that paralyzes us and worsens the problem. Panic clouds judgment, stifles creativity, and prevents forward movement.

Seneca and the Stoics remind us that fear amplifies misery. A tough situation can be handled with rational effort, but fear makes it feel insurmountable. Whether you’re leading a team, debugging a system, or facing professional uncertainty, resist the temptation to let fear take control. Instead, focus on what you can do and act decisively.

Today, remind yourself that fear is an unhelpful companion. By rejecting panic and embracing calm, you clear the path to advance—even in the most difficult situations. Courage, after all, is not the absence of fear but the decision to move forward despite it.
