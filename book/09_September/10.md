## September 10th - PRACTICE FOR TOUGHER DAYS

***“Here’s a lesson to test your mind’s mettle: take part of a week in which you have only the most meager and cheap food, dress scantly in shabby clothes, and ask yourself if this is really the worst that you feared. It is when times are good that you should gird yourself for tougher times ahead, for when Fortune is kind the soul can build defenses against her ravages. So it is that soldiers practice maneuvers in peacetime, erecting bunkers with no enemies in sight and exhausting themselves under no attack so that when it comes they won’t grow tired.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 18.5-6

In the tech world, it’s easy to feel secure when things are running smoothly when your systems are stable, your projects are progressing, and opportunities abound. But as Seneca advises, it’s in these sunny moments that you should prepare for the storms. Stability is not guaranteed, and challenges often strike unexpectedly.

What if your server crashes tomorrow? What if you lose a key team member? What if the economy takes a sudden downturn? Preparing now, in times of peace, strengthens your ability to weather these storms. Run disaster simulations. Practice operating with minimal resources. Learn skills that would help you navigate a career pivot.

Today, take a step to practice for the tougher days ahead. By exposing yourself to controlled adversity, you’ll find that even your worst fears are manageable and you’ll face future challenges with resilience and calm.
