## September 11th - PREPARE FOR LESS

***“Let us get used to dining out without the crowds, to being a slave to fewer slaves, to getting clothes only for their real purpose, and to living in more modest quarters.”***
—SENECA, ON TRANQUILITY OF MIND, 9.3b

In tech and in life, success often brings excess bigger teams, more tools, higher salaries, or greater recognition. But as history and Seneca remind us, these luxuries can be fleeting. Systems crash, markets shift, or opportunities dry up. If you haven’t prepared for the possibility of less, these changes can feel devastating.

One way to safeguard your mental resilience is to simplify now. Experiment with minimalism: use fewer tools, streamline your workflows, or take on projects with smaller teams. Live within your means financially and emotionally, so you’re not dependent on abundance to thrive.

Today, consider what “less” might look like in your life and work. By practicing simplicity and self-reliance, you build a foundation of resilience. When challenges come, as they inevitably will, you’ll be prepared not just to survive but to adapt and move forward.
