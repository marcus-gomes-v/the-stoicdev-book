## September 11th - PREPARE FOR LESS

***“Let us then teach ourselves to be able to dine without all Rome to look on, to be the slaves of fewer slaves, to get clothes which fulfil their original purpose, and to live in a smaller house.”***
—SENECA, ON TRANQUILITY OF MIND, 9.3

There’s a stage in many tech careers where life quietly expands to fit the salary. A bigger apartment, a car payment, subscriptions you forgot about, a three-monitor setup you can no longer work without. None of it is wrong. It just becomes weight you don’t notice until something shifts: a layoff, a startup that runs out of money, a year when you’d like to take a lower-paid job you actually care about.

Seneca’s phrase about being “the slaves of fewer slaves” is about that weight. Everything you own asks something of you in return.

Practicing with less doesn’t mean living like a monk. It can be as small as building a side project on a boring stack, or keeping your fixed costs low enough that one bad quarter doesn’t decide your next move. The point is to stay free to choose, whatever the market does.
