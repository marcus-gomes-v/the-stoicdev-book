## September 12th - STAY GROUNDED

***“Nothing, he declared, was more unbecoming than arrogance, especially in the young.”***
—DIOGENES LAERTIUS, LIVES OF THE EMINENT PHILOSOPHERS, 7.1.22

Diogenes Laertius tells a story just before this line. A rich, handsome young man insisted on joining Zeno’s classes, so Zeno sat him on the dustiest bench, then moved him next to the beggars, until the young man gave up and left. Zeno had little patience for people who arrived already sure of their own importance.

Every team has met some version of him. Sometimes it’s the new grad who rewrites a working module in their favorite framework without asking. Sometimes it’s someone ten years in who stopped listening around year five. Arrogance hurts at any age, but it does the most damage early, because it shuts out exactly the people who could teach you.

Being good at something young is a gift. Keep asking questions in the channel anyway. Read the review comments twice before you argue with them, and let the people who’ve been here longer finish their sentences.
