## September 13th - DEFEND YOUR INNER CITADEL

***“No, it is events that give rise to fear—when another has power over them or can prevent them, that person becomes able to inspire fear. How is the fortress destroyed? Not by iron or fire, but by judgments... here is where we must begin, and it is from this front that we must seize the fortress and throw out the tyrants.”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 4.1.85-86; 87a

The Stoics believed in the concept of the Inner Citadel a fortress within that protects the soul from external harm. While external events may challenge or inconvenience us, they cannot breach this inner stronghold unless we let them in through fear, doubt, or faulty judgment.

In the tech world, where setbacks failed projects, demanding clients, or shifting priorities are inevitable, it’s easy to let external pressures undermine your confidence. But your Inner Citadel is where resilience resides. It’s the calm, reasoned part of you that decides how to respond, regardless of the circumstances.

Today, when challenges arise, visualize your Inner Citadel standing strong. Guard it by rejecting fear and emotional overreaction. Let your judgments guide your actions, not the whims of external events. By protecting your inner fortress, you ensure that no setback can shake your resolve or define your worth.
