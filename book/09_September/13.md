## September 13th - DEFEND YOUR INNER CITADEL

***“Nay, it is things that inspire man with fear; and when one person is able to secure them for another, or to take them away, then he becomes capable of inspiring fear. How, then, is a citadel destroyed? Not by iron, nor by fire, but by judgements... But here is where we must begin, and it is from this side that we must seize the acropolis and cast out the tyrants...”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 4.1.85-87

Think about who scares you at work. It’s probably not the person. It’s what they can give or take away: the promotion, the visa sponsorship, the good project, the bonus. Epictetus puts it bluntly. One human being has nothing frightening about him to another, any more than one horse frightens another horse. It’s the things we want that make someone powerful over us.

So the tyrant isn’t really the director in the corner office. It’s the belief in your own head that you can’t be all right without what they control. That’s the citadel he means, and it falls to judgments, not to force.

That’s also where you take it back. You can still want the promotion and work hard for it. But once you stop believing your worth depends on it, the person who controls it becomes a colleague again, someone you can disagree with in a meeting.
