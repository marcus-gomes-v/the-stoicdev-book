## September 14th - PRAY FOR STRENGTH, NOT ESCAPE

***“Try praying differently, and see what happens: Instead of asking for ‘a way to sleep with her,’ try asking for ‘a way to stop desiring to sleep with her.’ Instead of ‘a way to get rid of him,’ try asking for ‘a way to not crave his demise.’ Instead of ‘a way to not lose my child,’ try asking for ‘a way to lose my fear of it.’”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 9.40.(6)

In the fast-moving world of tech, we often "pray" for quick solutions a bug fix, a faster resolution, or an easy path forward. Yet Marcus Aurelius challenges us to reframe our desires. Instead of asking for the problem to disappear, ask for the strength to navigate it. Instead of wishing for the chaos to subside, seek clarity amidst it.

Life, like work, rarely grants easy answers or guarantees. What it does grant is the ability to cultivate fortitude, patience, and focus. Today, when you find yourself hoping for a smooth road, pause and shift your perspective. Ask for the resilience to endure challenges, the wisdom to prioritize, and the strength to handle setbacks. You might find that everything you need is already within you.
