## September 14th - PRAY FOR STRENGTH, NOT ESCAPE

***“Begin, then, to pray for such things, and thou wilt see. One man prays thus: How shall I be able to lie with that woman? Do thou pray thus: How shall I not desire to lie with her? Another prays thus: How shall I be released from this? Pray thou: How shall I not desire to be released? Another thus: How shall I not lose my little son? Thou thus: How shall I not be afraid to lose him? In fine, turn thy prayers this way, and see what comes.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 9.40

Most of what we wish for at work is for the problem to go away. Let the flaky test pass this once. Let the client drop the feature request. Let the difficult colleague transfer to another team.

Marcus suggests turning the request around. Not “how do I get rid of this person,” but “how do I stop needing them gone.” Not “how do I make sure the launch doesn’t fail,” but “how do I stop being so afraid that it will.” The first kind of wish depends on the world cooperating. The second depends on you, which is why it has a chance of being granted.

It sounds like a small change in grammar. Try it the next time you catch yourself hoping a problem will just disappear, and see, as he says, what comes.
