September 16th - GREATNESS IS EARNED, NOT GIVEN

“Success comes to the lowly and to the poorly talented, but the special characteristic of a great person is to triumph over the disasters and panics of human life.” 
—SENECA, ON PROVIDENCE, 4.1

In the tech industry, where rapid advancements and market swings dominate, it’s easy to admire those who seem to coast through success effortlessly. Maybe it’s the entrepreneur who lands funding despite a flawed pitch or the colleague who always seems to be in the right place at the right time. But luck, while helpful, doesn’t define greatness.

True greatness is found in perseverance. It’s in the developer who learns from failed deployments, the leader who steadies the team through chaos, or the entrepreneur who rebuilds after failure. Triumphing through challenges shows not only resilience but also mastery over fear, doubt, and external circumstances.

Today, focus less on what comes easily and more on what you can overcome. Greatness isn’t found in luck but in your ability to endure, adapt, and succeed despite difficulties. That’s the kind of success that truly lasts.
