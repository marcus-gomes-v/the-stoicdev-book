## September 17th - RISE ABOVE HATRED

***“Suppose any man shall despise me. Let him look to that himself. But I will look to this, that I be not discovered doing or saying anything deserving of contempt. Shall any man hate me? Let him look to it. But I will be mild and benevolent towards every man, and ready to show even him his mistake, not reproachfully, nor yet as making a display of my endurance, but nobly and honestly...”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 11.13

Some people will dislike you at work, and sometimes you won’t know why. A peer who leaves comments on every one of your pull requests with a little extra edge. Someone from another team who brings up your old outage in every planning meeting.

Marcus splits this cleanly. Their contempt is their business. Your business is to make sure nothing in your own conduct deserves it. That’s a much shorter list of things to worry about.

The second half is harder. He wants you to stay kind to the person, and even be ready to show them where they’re wrong, but not to score a point or to show everyone how patient you are. Just honestly. A calm reply on the PR that explains your reasoning, with no sarcasm and no audience in mind. Then back to work.
