September 17th - RISE ABOVE HATRED

“What if someone despises me? Let them see to it. But I will see to it that I won’t be found doing or saying anything contemptible. What if someone hates me? Let them see to that. But I will see to it that I’m kind and good-natured to all, and prepared to show even the hater where they went wrong. Not in a critical way, or to show off my patience, but genuinely and usefully.” 
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 11.13

In the tech world, opinions and criticism are plentiful—whether it’s feedback on your work, team dynamics, or public reactions to a product. Sometimes, those opinions edge into negativity or even outright disdain. Marcus Aurelius reminds us that other people’s hatred says more about them than it does about us.

Your job isn’t to control others’ feelings; it’s to remain kind, composed, and focused on your values. If their criticism is valid, accept it humbly and learn. If it’s not, let it roll off your back. Wasting energy on haters only diverts your focus from the meaningful work you’re here to do.

Today, if you encounter hostility, respond with patience and grace. Show by your actions—not your reactions—that you’re above pettiness. Great work and character speak louder than words. Keep doing both.
