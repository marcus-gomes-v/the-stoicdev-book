## September 18th - FACING PAIN WITH GRACE

***“Whenever you suffer pain, keep in mind that it’s nothing to be ashamed of and that it can’t degrade your guiding intelligence, nor keep it from acting rationally and for the common good. And in most cases you should be helped by the saying of Epicurus, that pain is never unbearable or unending, so you can remember these limits and not add to them in your imagination. Remember too that many common annoyances are pain in disguise, such as sleepiness, fever and loss of appetite. When they start to get you down, tell yourself you are giving in to pain.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 7.64

Pain is inevitable in life and work. Whether it’s physical, emotional, or professional setbacks, how we respond to pain reveals our character. Marcus Aurelius reminds us that pain, no matter how severe, doesn’t control our ability to think rationally or act with kindness. Churchill exemplified this when, after nearly being killed in an accident, he showed compassion to the man who hit him instead of succumbing to anger or self-pity.

In tech, pain can manifest as burnout, rejection, or failed projects. These moments test your endurance and mindset. Today, when you encounter discomfort or frustration, remind yourself that no pain is endless or insurmountable. Resist the urge to catastrophize and instead focus on enduring with grace and rationality. Pain is temporary, but your ability to face it with strength and poise will leave a lasting impact.
