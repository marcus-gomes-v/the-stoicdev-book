## September 19th - STRENGTH THROUGH ADAPTABILITY

***“Remember that to change your mind and to follow someone’s correction are consistent with a free will. For the action is yours alone—to fulfill its purpose in keeping with your impulse and judgment, and yes, with your intelligence.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 8.16

In tech, where tools, trends, and solutions evolve rapidly, stubbornly clinging to a single idea or method can quickly become a liability. Marcus Aurelius reminds us that true strength lies not in rigidity but in adaptability being open to correction and capable of adjusting your course as new information arises.

Flexibility of the will allows you to respond effectively to change, whether it’s a shift in project requirements, feedback from colleagues, or a discovery that renders your initial assumptions invalid. It’s not weakness to pivot; it’s wisdom. It shows that your decisions are driven by reason and purpose, not ego or blind determination.

Today, practice the strength of adaptability. Be willing to reassess, recalibrate, and embrace better approaches when they emerge. This combination of flexibility and intelligence is what makes you not only resilient but also truly effective.
