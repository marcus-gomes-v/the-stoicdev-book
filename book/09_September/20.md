## September 20th - LIFE IS A WRESTLING MATCH

***“The art of living is more like wrestling than dancing, because an artful life requires being prepared to meet and withstand sudden and unexpected attacks.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 7.61

In the tech world, where challenges can come from unexpected bugs, shifting requirements, or fierce competition, life often feels more like wrestling than dancing. Wrestling demands strength, resilience, and adaptability it’s not about flowing gracefully with the music but about bracing for the unexpected and staying grounded.

Life throws curveballs, just like a wrestler faces sudden moves from their opponent. Whether it’s a critical system failure at the worst time or a competitive product launch that threatens your market position, success depends on your ability to respond with grit and agility. Philosophy, like wrestling training, prepares you to handle these moments, strengthening your will and sharpening your judgment.

Today, embrace life’s challenges like a wrestler entering the ring. Train your mind to remain steady under pressure, respond skillfully to adversity, and leverage every moment to grow stronger. Life isn’t about avoiding challenges it’s about meeting them head-on.
