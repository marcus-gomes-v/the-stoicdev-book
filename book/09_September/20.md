## September 20th - LIFE IS A WRESTLING MATCH

***“The art of life is more like the wrestler’s art than the dancer’s, in respect of this, that it should stand ready and firm to meet onsets which are sudden and unexpected.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 7.61

A dancer knows the choreography. The music is fixed, the steps are rehearsed, and if everything goes to plan the performance looks effortless. A wrestler never gets that. The other person is working hard to do exactly what you didn’t plan for.

Most sprint plans are written like choreography. Ticket one on Monday, ticket two on Wednesday, demo on Friday. Then a customer finds a data bug on Tuesday, a dependency ships a breaking change, and someone on the team gets sick. It’s tempting to treat each of these as an interruption of the real work. Marcus would say they are the real work.

A wrestler’s readiness looks relaxed from outside: a low stance, weight centered, so a sudden push doesn’t knock you over. For us that means slack in the plan, changes that are easy to roll back, and the habit of asking, when something hits, what the next good move is rather than who ruined the week.
