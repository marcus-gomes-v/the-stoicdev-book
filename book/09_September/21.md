September 21st - FIND YOUR WAY BACK TO CALM

“When forced, as it seems, by circumstances into utter confusion, get a hold of yourself quickly. Don’t be locked out of the rhythm any longer than necessary. You’ll be able to keep the beat if you are constantly returning to it.” 
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 6.11

In the tech industry, where unexpected bugs, outages, or last-minute changes are the norm, chaos can feel inevitable. Plans collapse, assumptions fail, and your carefully laid strategy might unravel in moments. The key isn’t to avoid disruption—that’s impossible—but to regain your composure quickly and refocus.

Marcus Aurelius reminds us that the rhythm of life is like the rhythm of a team or a system under pressure. Mistakes or interruptions happen, but how swiftly you recover and return to a state of balance defines your success.

Today, when faced with unexpected setbacks, don’t let frustration or confusion dominate. Pause, take a deep breath, and realign. Find your rhythm again and move forward. Resilience isn’t about avoiding disruption; it’s about how effectively you regain control after it.
