## September 22nd - EMBRACE THE CHALLENGE

***“It is difficulties that show what men are. Consequently, when a difficulty befalls, remember that God, like a physical trainer, has matched you with a rugged young man. What for? some one says. So that you may become an Olympic victor; but that cannot be done without sweat. To my way of thinking no one has got a finer difficulty than the one which you have got, if only you are willing to make use of it as an athlete makes use of a young man to wrestle with.”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 1.24.1-2

Picture the worst thing on your plate right now. The legacy billing service no one understands. The stakeholder who changes the requirements every week. The interview loop where you keep failing the system design round.

Epictetus would call that your sparring partner. A good trainer doesn’t match a fighter with someone easy. He picks someone younger and rougher, because the point of practice is to get stronger, and nobody gets stronger against no resistance.

What stands out in his phrasing is the confidence. Nobody, he says, has a finer difficulty than the one you’ve got. It isn’t pleasant, but it’s yours, and it’s shaped exactly like the thing you need to learn. That billing service will teach you more about money, edge cases and careful migrations than any greenfield project. The only question is whether you’re willing to wrestle with it.
