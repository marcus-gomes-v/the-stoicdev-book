September 22nd - EMBRACE THE CHALLENGE

“Difficulties show a person’s character. So when a challenge confronts you, remember that God is matching you with a younger sparring partner, as would a physical trainer. Why? Becoming an Olympian takes sweat! I think no one has a better challenge than yours, if only you would use it like an athlete would that younger sparring partner.” 
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 1.24.1-2

Challenges in the tech industry are like a sparring match—they test your skills, endurance, and mindset. Whether it’s debugging a complex issue, adapting to a new technology, or navigating difficult team dynamics, these obstacles reveal your character and potential for growth.

The Stoics saw these trials not as setbacks but as opportunities to improve, much like athletes sharpening their skills against stronger opponents. It’s not about seeking the easy path or avoiding difficulty—it’s about leaning into it. The greatest developers, leaders, and innovators don’t shy away from challenges; they embrace them as the training ground for excellence.

Today, view each difficulty as a chance to grow stronger and more capable. Instead of frustration, choose determination. Each obstacle is a step on the path to mastery, and the effort you put in now will yield the victories of tomorrow.
