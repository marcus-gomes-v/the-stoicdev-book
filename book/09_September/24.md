## September 24th - PREPARE FOR THE UNEXPECTED

***“Being unexpected adds to the weight of a disaster, and being a surprise has never failed to increase a person’s pain. For that reason, nothing should ever be unexpected by us. Our minds should be sent out in advance to all things and we shouldn’t just consider the normal course of things, but what could actually happen. For is there anything in life that Fortune won’t knock off its high horse if it pleases her?”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 91.3a–4

In the tech industry, unexpected failures system outages, data breaches, or market disruptions can amplify stress and chaos if we aren’t prepared for them. Seneca reminds us that the weight of a disaster is often heavier when it catches us off guard. The solution? Anticipate reversals of fortune, not as pessimism, but as preparation.

When designing systems, building teams, or planning projects, think beyond the ideal scenarios. Ask yourself: What’s the worst that could happen? How would I respond? Just as Seneca reflected on fate’s reversals, tech professionals can simulate failure scenarios, create contingencies, and mentally prepare for adversity.

Today, take a moment to anticipate challenges that could arise in your work or life. Not to worry, but to prepare. When adversity strikes, your foresight and readiness will transform surprises into manageable situations. Fortune may be fickle, but your resilience doesn’t have to be.
