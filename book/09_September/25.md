September 25th - BREAK FREE FROM DEPENDENCE

“Show me someone who isn’t a slave! One is a slave to lust, another to greed, another to power, and all are slaves to fear. I could name a former Consul who is a slave to a little old woman, a millionaire who is the slave of the cleaning woman. . . . No servitude is more abject than the self-imposed.” 
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 47.17

In tech and modern life, dependencies are everywhere—on tools, routines, habits, or external validation. The latest device, the approval of a colleague, or even a morning coffee can quietly exert control over us. The more we rely on these things, the more vulnerable we become to losing them.

As Seneca and Epictetus warn, true freedom lies in limiting our attachments to what is outside our control. What would happen if the server went down, your favorite tool became unavailable, or you didn’t get the recognition you expected? Would you adapt, or would your dependency leave you paralyzed?

Today, test your dependencies. Skip a comfort you rely on—try starting the day without your usual coffee, step away from a distracting habit, or simplify your workflow. These small acts of discipline strengthen your independence and make you more resilient to life’s disruptions. True freedom begins when you no longer let external dependencies dictate your well-being.
