## September 25th - BREAK FREE FROM DEPENDENCE

***“Point to one man who is not a slave. One is a slave to lust, another to greed, another to ambition, and all of them to hope and to fear. I can name a former consul who waits on an old woman, a rich man who waits on a servant girl, young men of the noblest families who are owned by pantomime dancers. No slavery is more disgraceful than the one we choose.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 47.17

Letter 47 is Seneca’s argument that slaves are human beings and should be treated that way, which was not the usual view among rich Romans. In the middle of it he turns the question on his readers. You look down on a man for being owned. What owns you?

It’s an uncomfortable question for anyone with a phone. The urge to check the dashboard every ten minutes. The need for your manager’s approval before every decision. A salary you’ve built your life around so tightly that you’d stay in a job you hate rather than risk it. Each one starts as a convenience and slowly becomes a boss.

His last line is the sharp one: no slavery is more disgraceful than the one we choose, because we could also choose to stop. Pick one of those small masters and test it. Turn the notifications off for a week and see who’s actually in charge.
