September 26th - MAKE LEISURE MEANINGFUL

“Leisure without study is death—a tomb for the living person.” 
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 82.4

In tech, where constant hustle is often glorified, taking time off feels like a rare luxury. But Seneca reminds us that leisure isn’t about idleness; it’s an opportunity for growth, reflection, and learning. A break from work should recharge your mind and enrich your perspective, not simply serve as an escape.

Vacation, retirement, or even a quiet weekend shouldn’t mean switching off entirely—it should be a chance to dive into books, explore creative ideas, or reflect on your goals. Enjoy the relaxation, but also use it to gain insight into yourself, your work, and the world around you.

Today, if you’re stepping away from your desk or planning time off, ask yourself: How can this leisure time enrich me? Whether it’s reading something thought-provoking, journaling, or observing the world with fresh eyes, make your downtime a source of inspiration. Leisure becomes meaningful when it helps you grow.
