## September 26th - MAKE LEISURE MEANINGFUL

***“Leisure without learning is death, the burial of a man still alive.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 82.3

Seneca says this right after admitting the opposite is just as bad. Being whirled around by endless obligations is no life, he writes, and lying idle among comforts isn’t one either. Both are a kind of death.

Developers know both versions. There’s the sprint that never ends, where you haven’t read anything that wasn’t a ticket in months. And there’s the vacation, or the long weekend, or the gap between jobs, that dissolves into scrolling and leaves you more tired than when it started.

What he wants from free time is something that feeds the mind. That doesn’t have to mean self-improvement with a spreadsheet. Read a book that has nothing to do with software. Learn how a mechanical watch works. Write the essay you keep thinking about. Take the walk without a podcast so a thought can finish. Rest is good. Rest that leaves you more awake is better.
