## September 27th - PROSPERITY REVEALS CHARACTER

***“Peace itself will supply you with fears. Once the mind has been badly shaken it will not trust even what is safe, and when it has made a habit of heedless fright it becomes unfit even to look after its own protection. For it does not sidestep danger; it flees. And we lie more open to danger once we have turned away.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 104.10

You’d think the anxiety would go away once things got better. The project shipped, the funding came through, the review was glowing. And yet a mind that has learned to panic keeps finding new things to panic about. Now there’s more to lose. Now every message from the CEO might be the one where it all falls apart.

Seneca saw that good times don’t cure fear. A mind that has made a habit of fright can’t even protect itself, because running away isn’t the same as avoiding danger. A team in that state treats every alert as an emergency, rewrites things that work, and makes its worst calls in its calmest months.

More success won’t fix it. What helps is facing the fear while things are quiet: look at the risk, decide what you would actually do, and then put it down. Turning your back on it only leaves you more exposed.
