## September 27th - PROSPERITY REVEALS CHARACTER

***“For even peace itself will supply more reason for worry. Not even safe circumstances will bring you confidence once your mind has been shocked—once it gets in the habit of blind panic, it can’t provide for its own safety. For it doesn’t really avoid danger, it just runs away. Yet we are exposed to greater danger with our backs turned.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 104.10b

In tech, where success often brings recognition, resources, and responsibility, prosperity can reveal the true nature of your character. If you’re prone to panic, doubt, or insecurity, no amount of success will bring you peace it might even amplify your worries as you find yourself with more to lose.

Seneca reminds us that real stability comes from within. Instead of hoping for fortune, work on building resilience. Develop habits of calm and thoughtful decision-making, regardless of the circumstances. Good fortune isn’t inherently good if it only magnifies your fears; it becomes meaningful when paired with the strength to handle it gracefully.

Today, focus on cultivating inner stability. Treat each success or challenge as an opportunity to strengthen your character. True prosperity isn’t found in external gains but in the confidence and resilience to thrive, no matter what life brings.
