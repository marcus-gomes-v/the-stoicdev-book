## September 28th - THE POWER OF YOUR RESPONSE

***“How appropriate that the gods put under our control only the most powerful ability that governs all the rest—the ability to make the right use of external appearances—and that they didn’t put anything else under our control. Was this simply because they weren’t willing to give us more? I think if it had been possible they would have given us more, but it was impossible.”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 1.1.7-8

In tech, as in life, the variables you don’t control often feel overwhelming project outcomes, client decisions, or even team dynamics. Epictetus reminds us, however, that the greatest power lies in what you do control: your response.

Instead of fretting over what’s out of your hands, focus on what you can govern your perspective, your effort, and your attitude. The ability to choose how you interpret and act on external events is the ultimate trump card. A delayed project can be a chance to refine, criticism can be a tool for growth, and setbacks can be transformed into opportunities.

Today, remind yourself of this power. Whatever comes your way, decide how you’ll frame it and respond. That decision is yours alone, and it’s more than enough to thrive.
