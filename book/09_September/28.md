## September 28th - THE POWER OF YOUR RESPONSE

***“As was fitting, therefore, the gods have put under our control only the most excellent faculty of all and that which dominates the rest, namely, the power to make correct use of external impressions, but all the others they have not put under our control. Was it indeed because they would not? I for one think that had they been able they would have entrusted us with the others also; but they were quite unable to do that.”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 1.1.7-8

“External impressions” sounds abstract, but you deal with them all day. A one-line review comment: “Why would you do it this way?” A calendar invite from your skip-level titled “Quick sync.” A red build on main. Each one arrives with a story already attached, and the story is usually not kind.

Epictetus says the one thing truly given to us is the power to use those impressions well: to look at the story, test it, and decide whether to accept it. Maybe the reviewer is curious, not contemptuous. Maybe the sync is about something good.

He even admits the gods might have given us more if they could. We’re stuck with bodies, deadlines and other people’s choices. But the faculty we did get rules all the rest, and on most days it’s enough.
