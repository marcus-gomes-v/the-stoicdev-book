September 29th - WHAT YOU NEED IS LESS THAN YOU THINK

“Nothing can satisfy greed, but even a small measure satisfies nature. So it is that the poverty of an exile brings no misfortune, for no place of exile is so barren as not to produce ample support for a person.” 
—SENECA, ON CONSOLATION TO HELVIA, 10.11b

Think back to the first time you lived on your own—your modest paycheck, your tiny apartment, your budget meals. It wasn’t luxurious, but it worked. You survived, maybe even thrived, with far less than you have now. And yet, over time, success often shifts our perception of what’s necessary. What once felt like plenty now seems insufficient, leaving us constantly chasing more.

Seneca’s wisdom reminds us that our actual needs are small. No matter how much we’ve gained, we should never forget how little we truly need to live well. In tech, where the pursuit of growth, recognition, and resources can feel endless, this perspective is grounding.

Today, reflect on your true needs. Would you still be OK if everything were scaled back? Recognizing this can reduce fear, alleviate stress, and help you focus on what truly matters—your work, your relationships, and your purpose. Let go of unnecessary wants and rediscover the strength of simplicity.
