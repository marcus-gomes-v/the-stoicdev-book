## September 29th - WHAT YOU NEED IS LESS THAN YOU THINK

***“Nothing is sufficient for covetous desire, but Nature can be satisfied even with scant measure. The poverty of an exile, therefore, causes no inconvenience, for no place of exile is so barren as not to produce what is abundantly sufficient to support a man.”***
—SENECA, ON CONSOLATION TO HELVIA, 10.11

Think back to your first developer job. Maybe a shared flat, a secondhand laptop, lunch from the same cheap place every day. It wasn’t glamorous, but you were learning fast and you were fine. Probably more than fine.

Since then the numbers have gone up, and so has your idea of enough. The senior salary felt huge for about six months. Now it’s the baseline, and the next level is the one that will finally make you comfortable. Seneca is blunt about this: nothing is enough for that kind of wanting. The line moves every time you reach it.

He wrote this from exile on Corsica, to comfort his mother, and he meant it literally. Even that bare island gave him what a person needs. You don’t have to give anything up to take the point. Just know where your real floor is. Once you know how little you need, a layoff or a down round can still hurt, but it can’t frighten you the way it used to.
