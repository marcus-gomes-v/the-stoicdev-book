## September 30th - YOUR MIND IS UNTOUCHABLE

***“If you lay violent hands on me, you’ll have my body, but my mind will remain with Stilpo.”***
—ZENO, QUOTED IN DIOGENES LAERTIUS, LIVES OF THE EMINENT PHILOSOPHERS, 7.1.24

Zeno reminds us that while the external world may take control of our circumstances or bodies, it cannot breach the sanctuary of our mind unless we allow it. This inner fortress, fortified by philosophy, keeps us untouchable, no matter how harsh the external conditions.

In the tech industry, challenges often feel personal failed launches, criticism from stakeholders, or unexpected setbacks. But these external events don’t define you. Your power lies in how you respond. Like Rubin “Hurricane” Carter, who refused to let prison confine his mind, you can remain free, even when circumstances feel restrictive or unfair.

Today, when adversity strikes, remember that your mind is yours alone. While the world may impact your external circumstances, it has no claim over your thoughts, your perspective, or your choices. Stay grounded in this power, and you’ll remain unshaken.
