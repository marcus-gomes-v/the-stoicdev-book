## September 30th - YOUR MIND IS UNTOUCHABLE

***“The right way to seize a philosopher, Crates, is by the ears: persuade me then and drag me off by them; but, if you use violence, my body will be with you, but my mind with Stilpo.”***
—ZENO, QUOTED IN DIOGENES LAERTIUS, LIVES OF THE EMINENT PHILOSOPHERS, 7.1.24

Crates had been Zeno’s first teacher, and he didn’t like watching his student drift off to another school. So he grabbed Zeno by the cloak to drag him away from Stilpo. Zeno’s reply is a very polite refusal. Take a philosopher by the ears, he says. Convince me and I’ll follow. Use force and you’ll have my body, while my mind stays where it was.

Most of us have been on both sides of that cloak. A manager mandates a framework without explaining why, and the team adopts it on paper and quietly works around it. Or you push a change through on seniority and then wonder why nobody owns it.

It cuts both ways. Your mind can be moved by reasons, never by pressure, so nobody can force you to believe what you don’t. And if you want people’s minds and not just their compliance, you have to go through their ears.
