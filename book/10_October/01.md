October 1st - LET YOUR VIRTUE LIGHT THE WAY
“Does the light of a lamp shine and keep its glow until its fuel is spent? Why shouldn’t your truth, justice, and self-control shine until you are extinguished?” —MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 12.15
In the tech industry, your work and leadership can be a beacon—of integrity, fairness, and calm under pressure. Like a lamp fueled by its oil, your virtues of truth, justice, and self-control can illuminate the path for others while sustaining your purpose.
It doesn’t matter where you are in your journey—just starting out, hitting your stride, or nearing the end of a long career. What matters is that you consistently embody these virtues. Your example can inspire colleagues, drive collaboration, and create a positive impact in your team and beyond.
Today, let your actions be guided by the light of your virtues. Speak truth, act justly, and maintain self-control, no matter the circumstances. Shine brightly for as long as you’re here, making your corner of the world better for others.
