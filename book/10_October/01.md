## October 1st - LET YOUR VIRTUE LIGHT THE WAY

***“Does the light of the lamp shine without losing its splendor until it is extinguished? and shall the truth which is in thee and justice and temperance be extinguished [before thy death]?”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 12.15

A lamp doesn’t dim halfway through the night because it’s tired of burning. It gives the same light until the oil runs out.

People dim all the time. You see it in the engineer serving out a notice period: the handover doc gets thinner, code reviews get a thumbs-up and nothing else. You see it in the veteran who stopped asking for tests years ago because nobody listened. And you see it in yourself on a slow Thursday, when the honest status update feels like more trouble than the vague one.

Marcus asks why your truthfulness, your fairness and your self-control should go out before you do. It makes no difference whether you shipped your first feature last month or you’re a few years from retiring. The standard isn’t lower at the start or at the end.

Write the handover as if you were the one inheriting it. Give the real estimate. Keep the light on for as long as you’re in the room.
