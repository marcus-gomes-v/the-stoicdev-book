October 2nd - INVEST IN VIRTUE

“But the wise person can lose nothing. Such a person has everything stored up for themselves, leaving nothing to Fortune, their own goods are held firm, bound in virtue, which requires nothing from chance, and therefore can’t be either increased or diminished.” —SENECA, ON THE FIRMNESS OF THE WISE, 5.4

In the tech industry, where careers can rise and fall with market trends, new tools, or the whims of stakeholders, it’s easy to feel like security depends on external achievements or 
financial success. But Seneca reminds us that the most resilient and enduring asset isn’t money, reputation, or accolades—it’s virtue.

When challenges arise—a project fails, a position is lost, or circumstances shift—your technical skills, integrity, and inner strength are what truly carry you through. These are assets Fortune can’t touch. Seneca’s life is a testament to this: even when stripped of wealth and status, he relied on his virtue to face his final moments with dignity.

Today, prioritize building the kind of wealth that no external force can take away. Focus on becoming a person of integrity, wisdom, and resilience. In moments of trial, these are the assets that will sustain you.
