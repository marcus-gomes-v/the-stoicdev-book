## October 2nd - INVEST IN VIRTUE

***“But the wise man can lose nothing. He has invested everything in himself, has entrusted nothing to fortune, has his property in safety, and is content with virtue, which does not need casual accessories, and therefore can neither be increased or diminished.”***
—SENECA, ON THE FIRMNESS OF THE WISE, 5.4

The layoff email arrives at 9:04 on a Tuesday. By 9:30 your laptop is locked and your Slack account is gone. The title, the stock that hadn’t vested, the team you spent three years building: it belonged to someone else the whole time, and now they’ve taken it back.

Seneca would say you just found out what was never yours. What is yours walks out the door with you. The judgment you built from a hundred incidents. The habit of telling the truth in planning meetings. The patience you learned mentoring people who were slower than you. Nobody can revoke your access to that.

He knew both sides of this. He was one of the richest men in Rome, and he lost his place at court and finally his life on Nero’s orders. The accounts of his death describe a man who stayed calm to the end, because what he valued most had never been in Nero’s hands.

Money and titles are fine to have. Just don’t keep all your savings there.
