## October 3rd - RECOGNIZING OUR INTERCONNECTEDNESS

***“Meditate often on the interconnectedness and mutual interdependence of all things in the universe. For in a sense, all things are mutually woven together and therefore have an affinity for each other—for one thing follows after another according to their tension of movement, their sympathetic stirrings, and the unity of all substance.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 6.38

In tech, collaboration is the backbone of innovation. Whether you’re developing software, deploying systems, or brainstorming solutions, success depends on recognizing the interconnected contributions of your team and even the broader ecosystem. Marcus Aurelius reminds us that everything is woven together each person, each action, and even each mistake plays a role in the larger whole.

It’s easy to forget this interconnectedness when deadlines loom or frustrations mount. But today, pause and consider: how is your work connected to others? How does your effort flow into a bigger purpose? How does the success of your team or organization benefit from every individual’s role?

By appreciating this mutual interdependence, you foster collaboration, empathy, and humility. Remember, you’re not an isolated contributor you’re part of a larger network where every action has ripple effects. Use this perspective to strengthen your connections and make your work more impactful.
