## October 4th - THE HIVE AND THE BEE

***“That which is not good for the swarm, neither is it good for the bee.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 6.54

Every company has one: the only developer who understands the payments module. They like it that way. Every incident goes through them, and their manager thinks they’re irreplaceable.

Look at it from the swarm’s side. A team with a bus factor of one can’t take real vacations and doesn’t sleep well on release nights. Sooner or later that weakness lands on the person in the middle too. They get paged at their sister’s wedding. They never get to join the interesting new project, because who would cover payments?

Marcus kept it to one line: what isn’t good for the swarm isn’t good for the bee. Hoarding knowledge, taking credit for a fix the whole team found, picking the tool that looks good on your CV over the one the team can maintain. Each feels like a win for you. Each is a slow loss for everyone, and you are part of everyone.

Write the runbook. Pair someone in on the scary part of the code.
