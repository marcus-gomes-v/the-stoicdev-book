October 5th - CHOOSE YOUR WORDS WISELY
“Better to trip with the feet than with the tongue.” —ZENO, QUOTED IN DIOGENES LAERTIUS, LIVES OF THE EMINENT PHILOSOPHERS, 7.1.26

In the fast-paced world of tech, where communication happens quickly—via emails, chats, or meetings—it’s easy to speak impulsively. But Zeno’s wisdom reminds us that words, once spoken, cannot be taken back. A hurtful comment or thoughtless remark can linger far longer than a misstep in a project or a line of broken code.

Today, pause before you speak. When emotions run high or frustrations arise, take a moment to ask: Will this contribute positively to the conversation or the relationship? Words have immense power—they can motivate or demoralize, clarify or confuse. Use them thoughtfully.

Remember, mistakes in action can often be corrected, but the damage caused by careless words is harder to undo. Let your speech reflect wisdom, kindness, and restraint.
