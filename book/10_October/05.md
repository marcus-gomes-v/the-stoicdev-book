## October 5th - CHOOSE YOUR WORDS WISELY

***“Better to trip with the feet than with the tongue.”***
—ZENO, QUOTED IN DIOGENES LAERTIUS, LIVES OF THE EMINENT PHILOSOPHERS, 7.1.26

Most mistakes in code can be undone. A bad migration gets rolled back. A broken build is fixed in ten minutes. Even a long outage ends with a postmortem and a list of action items.

Words are harder to roll back. The sarcastic comment on a junior’s pull request. The “who wrote this?” in a public channel. The joke about another team in a meeting where someone from that team was quietly listening. People forget the outage. They remember how you spoke to them during it.

Zeno taught in a public colonnade in Athens, in front of anyone who happened to walk by, so he knew what a careless sentence costs. Before you hit enter on the reply you wrote while angry, read it once more. If you wouldn’t say it with that person sitting next to you, change it or delete it. A twisted ankle heals faster.
