## October 6th - CELEBRATE THE SUCCESS OF OTHERS

***“It is natural to hold our friends dear and to be as glad of their progress as if it were our own. If we fail to do this, even virtue will not last in us, because it grows strong by exercising our capacity to feel.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 109.15

The promotion list goes out on a Friday afternoon. Your name isn’t on it. The name of the person who joined a year after you is. The congratulations start rolling into the channel, and your cursor hovers over the emoji button for a long time.

The sting is normal. What you do next is up to you. You can send a flat “congrats” and spend the weekend building the case for why they didn’t deserve it. Or you can mean it. Ask them what they learned on the project that got them there. Someone you work with every day just got better at the job, and your team is stronger for it.

Seneca’s point goes further than being a good sport. Being glad for a friend works like a muscle. Stop using it and the rest of your character goes soft along with it. Envy barely touches the other person’s day, but it slowly makes you smaller.
