October 6th - CELEBRATE THE SUCCESS OF OTHERS

“It’s in keeping with Nature to show our friends affection and to celebrate their advancement, as if it were our very own. For if we don’t do this, virtue, which is strengthened only by exercising our perceptions, will no longer endure in us.” —SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 109.15

In the competitive world of tech, watching others succeed—especially when you feel stuck or overlooked—can sting. Our instincts may tell us that someone else’s gain diminishes our chances. But Seneca challenges us to rise above this limited view. Virtue, after all, grows stronger when we actively practice empathy and selflessness.

Cheering for others’ successes not only strengthens relationships but also creates a culture of trust and collaboration. By supporting your colleagues and celebrating their wins, you contribute to an environment where everyone can thrive—including yourself.

Today, practice putting jealousy aside. When a teammate excels or a competitor achieves something remarkable, celebrate it as if it were your own success. This mindset fosters not only virtue but also a stronger, more collaborative community. In the long run, everyone benefits.
