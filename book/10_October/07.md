October 7th - BE GOOD FOR YOUR OWN SAKE
“The person who does wrong, does wrong to themselves. The unjust person is unjust to themselves—making themselves evil.” —MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 9.4
In the fast-paced and high-stakes world of tech, the temptation to cut corners, take credit where it’s not due, or let ethics slide in pursuit of success can be real. But Marcus Aurelius reminds us of a critical truth: when you do wrong, you harm yourself most of all. Your integrity, your peace of mind, and your sense of self all suffer.
Think back to a time when you compromised your values. Did it feel good in the end? Likely not. The anxiety of getting caught, the hollow feeling of undeserved rewards, or the realization that you’ve betrayed your own principles—these are heavy burdens to carry.
Today, when faced with ethical choices, remember that doing the right thing isn’t just for others—it’s for you. Avoid the guilt and regret that come from wrongdoing. Act with integrity, not just because it’s virtuous, but because it’s the path to inner peace and self-respect.
