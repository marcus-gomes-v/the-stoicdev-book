October 8th - THE PLEASURE OF VIRTUE
“Yes, getting your wish would have been so nice. But isn’t that exactly why pleasure trips us up? Instead, see if these things might be even nicer—a great soul, freedom, honesty, kindness, saintliness. For there is nothing so pleasing as wisdom itself, when you consider how sure-footed and effortless the works of understanding and knowledge are.” —MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 5.9
Pleasure, whether it’s a fleeting success at work, a moment of praise, or the dopamine hit of solving a tough problem, feels good in the moment. But as Marcus Aurelius reminds us, these fleeting pleasures pale in comparison to the enduring satisfaction that comes from living virtuously.
In the tech world, where the rush of shipping a feature or closing a deal is short-lived, it’s easy to get caught in a cycle of chasing temporary highs. Instead, focus on cultivating virtues like honesty, kindness, and wisdom. These are not only more sustainable but also more fulfilling in the long run.
Today, when you’re tempted to seek pleasure in momentary rewards, shift your focus to building character. Choose actions that align with wisdom and integrity. The sense of fulfillment these bring will outlast any fleeting victory.
