## October 9th - LIVE BY YOUR STANDARDS

***“And so are matters judged and weighed, if we have the standards ready with which to test them; and the task of philosophy is this—to examine and to establish the standards; but to go ahead and use them after they have become known is the task of the good and excellent man.”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 2.11.23-25

Most teams have their standards written down somewhere. A definition of done. A rule that every change comes with a test. A line in the onboarding doc saying nobody pushes straight to main. They were argued over in a long meeting, approved and pinned in a channel.

Then it’s Thursday, the demo is on Friday, and the standards quietly go missing. Just this once. The test can come in a follow-up ticket that nobody will ever pick up.

Epictetus splits the work in two. Philosophy examines the standards and settles them. Using them once you know them is the job of a good person. Most of us are much better at the first part. We can debate a review checklist for an hour. Applying it at six in the evening to our own pull request, when no one would notice if we skipped it, is the hard part.

Pick one standard you already believe in and hold to it this week, especially on the day it gets in your way.
