October 9th - LIVE BY YOUR STANDARDS
“When the standards have been set, things are tested and weighed. And the work of philosophy is just this, to examine and uphold the standards, but the work of a truly good person is in using those standards when they know them.” —EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 2.11.23-25
In the tech world, where decisions must often be made quickly, it’s easy to default to convenience over principle. Yet, Epictetus reminds us that true strength lies in setting your standards and unwaveringly applying them to every situation—big or small. Whether you’re writing code, managing a team, or handling conflict, ask yourself: Is this consistent with my values and the person I want to be?
Living by standards isn’t just about lofty goals. It’s about the everyday decisions—the small actions that reflect who you are. Choosing clarity over shortcuts in your work. Responding with patience in a stressful meeting. Treating colleagues with kindness, even when deadlines loom.
Today, set a standard for yourself and hold to it. Don’t just aspire to do good in general—choose to do good in every moment that matters. Each action, no matter how small, is a chance to embody the values you’ve committed to.
