October 10th - GUIDE YOUR ACTIONS WITH VIRTUE
“Leave the past behind, let the grand design take care of the future, and instead only rightly guide the present to reverence and justice. Reverence so that you’ll love what you’ve been allotted, for nature brought you both to each other. Justice so that you’ll speak the truth freely and without evasion, and so that you’ll act only as the law and value of things require.” —MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 12.1
In the world of tech, where the past can feel irrelevant and the future is constantly being shaped, Marcus Aurelius reminds us of a crucial truth: the present is where our actions matter most. Reverence and justice should guide those actions. Reverence means appreciating your current opportunities and challenges as part of a larger whole. Justice means doing what is right—speaking truth, treating others fairly, and acting with integrity.
Epictetus’s advice to "persist and resist" pairs perfectly with these virtues. Persist in appreciating the present moment and fulfilling your duties with care. Resist the temptation to cut corners, evade responsibility, or act selfishly.
Today, focus on what’s right in front of you. Approach your work with reverence for its importance and act with justice toward those you collaborate with. Virtue isn’t an abstract idea; it’s the compass for every decision you make.
