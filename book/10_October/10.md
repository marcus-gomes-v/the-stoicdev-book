## October 10th - GUIDE YOUR ACTIONS WITH VIRTUE

***“All those things at which thou wishest to arrive by a circuitous road thou canst have now, if thou dost not refuse them to thyself. And this means, if thou wilt take no notice of all the past, and trust the future to providence, and direct the present only conformably to piety and justice. Conformably to piety that thou mayest be content with the lot which is assigned to thee, for nature designed it for thee and thee for it. Conformably to justice, that thou mayst always speak the truth freely and without disguise, and do the things which are agreeable to law and according to the worth of each.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 12.1

Half of a retro is spent on the past and the other half on a future nobody controls. We replay the migration that went wrong. We worry about the reorg everyone is whispering about. Meanwhile the work in front of us, the one thing we can actually touch, sits and waits.

Marcus narrows the present down to two things. The first he calls piety: being content with the lot you were given. The legacy service, the team you didn’t pick, the stack you would never have chosen. It’s yours now, and it is the only place you can do good work today.

The second is justice. Say the true thing plainly, without the careful fog of a status update written to protect yourself. Treat people according to what they’re worth, not according to their title or how loud they are in meetings.

Leave the postmortem to the past and the roadmap to the future. Do this one ticket honestly.
