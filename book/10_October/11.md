## October 11th - LET HONESTY SPEAK FOR ITSELF

***“How unsound and insincere is he who says, I have determined to deal with thee in a fair way!—What art thou doing, man? There is no occasion to give this notice. It will soon show itself by acts. The voice ought to be plainly written on the forehead. Such as a man’s character is, he immediately shows it in his eyes, just as he who is beloved forthwith reads everything in the eyes of lovers. The man who is honest and good ought to be exactly like a man who smells strong, so that the bystander as soon as he comes near him must smell whether he choose or not.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 11.15

Listen for “to be honest” in your next meeting. It usually comes right before something that had been held back, and it quietly suggests that whatever came before it was something less.

Marcus was blunter about it. Anyone who announces that they’re about to deal with you fairly has already made you suspicious. Honesty that needs a preface isn’t doing its job. The real thing shows in how you act, the way a strong smell reaches everyone in the room whether they like it or not.

On a team it looks like the engineer whose estimates you don’t pad in your head. The lead who says “I got this wrong” in the incident review before anyone has to point it out. The reviewer whose “looks good” means they actually read the code. Nobody had to tell you these people are straight with you. You worked it out from a hundred small moments.

That kind of trust takes years to build. You can start by dropping the preface and letting the work speak.
