## October 11th - LET HONESTY SPEAK FOR ITSELF

***“How rotten and fraudulent when people say they intend to ‘give it to you straight.’ What are you up to, dear friend? It shouldn’t need your announcement, but be readily seen, as if written on your forehead, heard in the ring of your voice, a flash in your eyes—just as the beloved sees it all in the lover’s glance. In short, the straightforward and good person should be like a smelly goat—you know when they are in the room with you.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 11.15

In tech, where trust is the foundation of effective collaboration, honesty shouldn’t be something you need to declare it should be evident in your actions, your decisions, and your words. If you constantly have to preface statements with, “To be honest,” or “Let me be straightforward,” it raises the question: why isn’t honesty your default?

True integrity means living and working in a way where people can rely on you without second-guessing your words or intentions. It means communicating transparently, owning up to mistakes, and making decisions based on fairness and truth. This consistency builds trust, credibility, and a reputation that speaks louder than any announcement of honesty ever could.

Today, practice honesty so consistently that it doesn’t need a preface. Let your actions and words align so clearly with truth that people never doubt your integrity. When honesty is your default, it becomes the foundation of both personal character and professional success.
