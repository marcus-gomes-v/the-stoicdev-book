## October 12th - LEAD WITH LOVE

***“Hecato says: ‘I will show you a love charm with no medicine, no herbs and no witch’s incantation. If you want to be loved, love.’”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 9.6

Some people spend their whole career trying to be liked at work. They agree too fast in design reviews, laugh at the right moments and avoid every hard conversation. It rarely works. People can tell when they’re being managed.

The recipe Seneca borrowed from Hecato has one ingredient. If you want to be loved, love. Care about the people you work with first, and stop keeping score of whether it comes back.

In practice it is small and specific. The new hire is nervous about her first on-call shift, so you stay on the call with her. A colleague spent weeks on a long design doc, so you read all of it and leave real comments instead of a thumbs-up. A teammate has gone quiet in standups, so you ask him in private how he’s doing and then you actually listen.

None of this is a technique, which is exactly why it works. Most of the time the warmth comes back to you. When it doesn’t, you still spent the day as someone worth knowing.
