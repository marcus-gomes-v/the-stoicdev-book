October 12th - LEAD WITH LOVE
“Hecato says, ‘I can teach you a love potion made without any drugs, herbs, or special spell—if you would be loved, love.’” —SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 9.6
In the tech industry, where competition and deadlines can strain relationships, it’s easy to let frustration or indifference take hold. But Seneca and Barbara Jordan remind us of a timeless truth: love and empathy have the power to transform any situation. Whether it’s collaborating with a difficult teammate, handling client pushback, or mentoring a junior colleague, approaching with kindness and understanding fosters trust and connection.
Love isn’t just for personal relationships—it’s a powerful tool in professional settings. When you act with empathy, genuinely appreciate others, or listen with patience, you create an environment where people feel valued. And often, that positivity comes back to you.
Today, practice leading with love. Show appreciation for a colleague, approach disagreements with understanding, and look for opportunities to connect with those around you. Love, as Seneca reminds us, is its own reward—and it’s the foundation of lasting relationships and meaningful work.
