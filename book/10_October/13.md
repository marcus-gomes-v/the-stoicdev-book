October 13th - RISE ABOVE REVENGE
“The best way to avenge yourself is to not be like that.” —MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 6.6
“How much better to heal than seek revenge from injury. Vengeance wastes a lot of time and exposes you to many more injuries than the first that sparked it. Anger always outlasts hurt. Best to take the opposite course. Would anyone think it normal to return a kick to a mule or a bite to a dog?” —SENECA, ON ANGER, 3.27.2
In the tech industry, where competition and credit can be contentious, it’s easy to feel wronged by someone else’s rudeness, dishonesty, or self-interest. The instinct to respond in kind—by being curt, sabotaging, or withholding—is tempting but ultimately counterproductive.
Marcus Aurelius and Seneca remind us that the best revenge is to remain above such behavior. If someone cuts corners or takes credit for your work, meet them with integrity. If someone treats you poorly, respond with kindness. By refusing to sink to their level, you prove that their actions have no power over you—and you set a higher standard for yourself and others.
Today, focus on being the example. When faced with poor behavior, choose to act with dignity and principle. In doing so, you show that true strength lies not in retaliation but in rising above pettiness and living well.
