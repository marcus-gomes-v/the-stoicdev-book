## October 13th - RISE ABOVE REVENGE

***“The best way of avenging thyself is not to become like [the wrong-doer].”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 6.6

***“How far better is it to heal an injury than to avenge it? Revenge takes up much time, and throws itself in the way of many injuries while it is smarting under one. We all retain our anger longer than we feel our hurt: how far better it were to take the opposite course and not meet one mischief by another. Would any one think himself to be in his perfect mind if he were to return kicks to a mule or bites to a dog?”***
—SENECA, ON ANGER, 3.27.2

Someone on another team presented your design to the VP as their own and got the headcount you asked for. Or a reviewer left a condescending comment on your pull request where the whole team could see it. The reply writes itself. You could be curt in the next meeting, slow-walk their next request, leave an equally sharp comment on their code.

Marcus says the best revenge is not to become like the person who wronged you. Seneca adds the practical side. Revenge eats time, it invites more injuries than the one it started with, and the anger outlasts the hurt. You end up spending a whole quarter on a feud over a slight that would have faded in a week.

He also asks whether you would kick a mule back. Some people behave badly because that’s simply how they behave. Say clearly what happened if it matters, fix what you can, and keep your own standards exactly where they were.
