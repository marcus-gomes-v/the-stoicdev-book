## October 14th - TURN ANGER INTO ACTION

***“Art thou angry with him whose armpits stink? art thou angry with him whose mouth smells foul? What good will this anger do thee? He has such a mouth, he has such armpits: it is necessary that such an emanation must come from such things: but the man has reason, it will be said, and he is able, if he takes pains, to discover wherein he offends; I wish thee well of thy discovery. Well then, and thou hast reason: by thy rational faculty stir up his rational faculty; show him his error, admonish him. For if he listens, thou wilt cure him, and there is no need of anger.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 5.28

One teammate leaves debug logs scattered through every pull request. Another talks over people in planning until the quiet ones stop trying. You’ve noticed. You’ve complained about it at lunch and rolled your eyes on camera. You have never once told them.

Marcus has little patience for that kind of anger. People do what people like that do, the way a certain mouth gives off a certain smell. Yes, they could notice it themselves. But you noticed, and you have a mind too. Use it to reach theirs. Tell them plainly and kindly, in private.

The conversation is usually less dramatic than the resentment was. “Can I mention something? In planning you sometimes jump in before Ana finishes, and I don’t think she gets back in.” Often they had no idea. If they listen, the problem is solved. If they don’t, you’ve still traded silent irritation for an honest try.
