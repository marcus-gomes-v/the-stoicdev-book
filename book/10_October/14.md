October 14th - TURN ANGER INTO ACTION
“Are you angry when someone’s armpits stink or when their breath is bad? What would be the point? Having such a mouth and such armpits, there’s going to be a smell emanating. You say, they must have sense, can’t they tell how they are offending others? Well, you have sense too, congratulations! So, use your natural reason to awaken theirs, show them, call it out. If the person will listen, you will have cured them without useless anger. No drama nor unseemly show required.” —MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 5.28
In a tech environment, where collaboration is essential, minor annoyances can escalate if left unchecked. A colleague interrupts meetings, a teammate doesn’t follow best practices, or someone’s approach feels disruptive. It’s easy to stew silently, letting frustration build, but Marcus Aurelius offers a better alternative: address the issue calmly and constructively.
Instead of assuming ill intent or waiting for people to magically change, engage them with empathy. Politely point out the problem and offer solutions. A simple, respectful conversation can often lead to better outcomes than passive anger or resentment ever could.
Today, when faced with irritation, pause before reacting. Shift your focus from frustration to action. Approach others with the intent to help, not judge. In doing so, you create opportunities for growth—for them, and for yourself.
