## October 15th - ASSUME THE BEST IN OTHERS

***“Everything turns on your assumptions about it, and that’s on you. You can pluck out the hasty judgment at will, and like steering a ship around the point, you will find calm seas, fair weather and a safe port.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 12.22

In a team environment, especially in tech where tensions can rise under deadlines or complex problems, it’s easy to misinterpret actions or intentions. Did your colleague miss the meeting because they don’t care, or because their schedule conflicted? Did the developer skip documentation out of laziness, or because they were overwhelmed? Your assumptions shape your reactions and your relationships.

Marcus Aurelius reminds us that we have control over our judgments. If you assume malice or carelessness, you create conflict and frustration. If you assume good intentions, you invite understanding and resolution. A simple pause to question your assumption can change everything.

Today, give your team, friends, or family the benefit of the doubt. Assume the best in others until proven otherwise. Not only will this strengthen your relationships, but you’ll also find calmer seas in your own mind and work environment.
