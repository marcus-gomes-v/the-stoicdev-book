## October 15th - ASSUME THE BEST IN OTHERS

***“Consider that everything is opinion, and opinion is in thy power. Take away then, when thou choosest, thy opinion, and like a mariner who has doubled the promontory, thou wilt find calm, everything stable, and a waveless bay.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 12.22

Your colleague missed the design review without a word. By lunch you have a story: they don’t care about this project, they think it’s beneath them, they’re checked out. By the afternoon you’re drafting a pointed message.

Then you find out their kid was sent home sick from school.

Most of the friction on a team comes from stories like that one. The developer who skipped the docs was lazy, or maybe they were covering two people’s work. The manager who moved your deadline is clueless, or maybe they just lost a fight with their own boss and protected you from worse. You rarely know. But you always pick a version, and the version you pick decides how the next conversation goes.

Marcus says the opinion is yours, and you can drop it whenever you choose. Do that and it feels like a boat rounding the headland into a bay with no waves. Hold the story loosely, and ask a real question before you send the message.
