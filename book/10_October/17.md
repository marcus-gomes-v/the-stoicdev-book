October 17th - THE POWER OF KINDNESS
“A benefit should be kept like a buried treasure, only to be dug up in necessity. . . . Nature bids us to do well by all. . . . Wherever there is a human being, we have an opportunity for kindness.” —SENECA, ON THE HAPPY LIFE, 24.2-3
Every interaction today is an opportunity to practice kindness. Whether it’s a colleague, a client, or a stranger, each person you meet offers a chance to act with understanding and generosity. Seneca reminds us that kindness benefits not only the recipient but also the giver, creating a shared moment of humanity.
In the tech world, where deadlines and pressure often take center stage, small acts of kindness—acknowledging someone’s efforts, offering help, or listening patiently—can transform relationships and foster collaboration. These gestures may not always be reciprocated, but that’s not the point. Kindness is about who you are, not what you get in return.
Today, choose kindness. Approach each encounter with empathy and a willingness to uplift others. By focusing on what you can give, rather than what you might receive, you’ll create a ripple effect that strengthens not only those around you but your own character as well.
