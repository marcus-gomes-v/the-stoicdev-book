## October 18th - BE THE FRIEND YOU EXPECT FROM OTHERS

***“Nothing is more disgraceful than a wolfish friendship [false friendship]. Avoid this most of all. The good and simple and benevolent show all these things in the eyes, and there is no mistaking.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 11.15

You can usually spot the false friend at work. Warm in the one-on-one, then strangely silent when your proposal gets torn apart in the bigger meeting. Always around when they need a reviewer, never when you do. The fable Marcus has in mind is the wolf making friends with the sheep, and most of us have met a wolf or two.

The harder question is whether you’ve been one. Think about the colleague you praised in private and didn’t back up in public, because the VP was in the room. The friend from your old team you only message when you’re job hunting. None of it felt like betrayal at the time. It felt like being careful.

Marcus says the good, simple and kind person shows it in their eyes and there is no mistaking it. People can see whether you’re on their side. So be the same person in the hallway and in the meeting, and show up when it costs you something. That is the only kind of friendship worth asking from anyone else.
