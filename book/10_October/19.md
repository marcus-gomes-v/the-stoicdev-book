October 19th - REPLACE BAD HABITS WITH BETTER ONES
“Since habit is such a powerful influence, and we’re used to pursuing our impulses to gain and avoid outside our own choice, we should set a contrary habit against that, and where appearances are really slippery, use the counterforce of our training.” —EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 3.12.6
Bad habits, whether in work or life, often take hold not because we intend them but because we let them persist unchecked. Epictetus reminds us that the most effective way to counter these impulses is not to fight them directly but to replace them with better habits.
In tech, this could mean replacing the habit of procrastinating with the practice of tackling the smallest part of a task first. It could mean transforming negativity during team discussions into a habit of constructive feedback. Just as you’d redirect a dog’s focus, redirect your energy toward building positive patterns.
Today, when you notice an unhelpful habit emerge, take a step back and choose its opposite. Don’t just fight the bad—build the good. Over time, the better habits will take root, driving out the ones that no longer serve you. Training yourself in this way isn’t just about breaking patterns; it’s about creating a stronger, more disciplined version of yourself.
