October 21st - BE A HERO IN THE PRESENT
“Such behavior! People don’t want to praise their contemporaries whose lives they actually share, but hold great expectations for the praise of future generations—people they haven’t met or ever will! This is akin to being upset that past generations didn’t praise you.” —MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 6.18
It’s tempting to dream of legacy—leaving behind a name that lasts through time, like Alexander the Great or other historical figures. But as Marcus Aurelius points out, what’s the value in being admired by people you’ll never meet, long after you’re gone? You won’t know it. You won’t feel it. And often, such pursuits come at the cost of your humanity in the present.
In tech, this might look like obsessing over accolades or fame—striving to build something revolutionary not because it’s meaningful, but because you hope it will immortalize your name. But true greatness lies in what you do here and now: treating others with respect, solving problems with integrity, and contributing to the present moment in meaningful ways.
Today, shift your focus from the distant future to the present. Be someone worthy of admiration right now, not because it might echo through history, but because it’s the right thing to do. The legacy that truly matters is the impact you have on the lives of those around you today.
