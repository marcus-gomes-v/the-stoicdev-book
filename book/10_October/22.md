## October 22nd - IMPROVE WHO YOU ARE, NOT JUST WHAT YOU CAN DO

***“So someone’s good at taking down an opponent, but that doesn’t make them more community-minded, or modest, or well-prepared for any circumstance, or more tolerant of the faults of others.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 7.52

In tech, the drive for improvement is constant learning new tools, mastering complex systems, and refining technical skills. But Marcus Aurelius warns against focusing solely on competence at the expense of character. You might become the best coder, manager, or strategist, but what does that mean if you neglect kindness, patience, or humility along the way?

True self-improvement goes beyond skill-building. It’s about being better for others not just delivering on projects but showing empathy to your teammates, making time for those who need guidance, and contributing to a collaborative culture. Winning at work while failing as a human being is not a victory.

Today, as you work on getting better at something, pause and ask yourself: Is this also making me a better person? Strive for growth that’s holistic improving not only your abilities but also your values and connections with others. After all, who you are matters more than what you can do.
