## October 23rd - EMBRACE THE QUALITIES YOU CONTROL

***“Thou sayest, Men cannot admire the sharpness of thy wits.—Be it so: but there are many other things of which thou canst not say, I am not formed from them by nature. Show those qualities then which are altogether in thy power, sincerity, gravity, endurance of labor, aversion to pleasure, contentment with thy portion and with few things, benevolence, frankness, no love of superfluity, freedom from trifling, magnanimity.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 5.5

Every team has the brilliant one. They read a paper over the weekend and come back with a new caching strategy. They solve in an afternoon what took you a week. Sitting next to that person, it’s easy to decide you’re just not built for this.

Marcus doesn’t argue. Maybe people won’t admire your quick mind. Fine. Now look at the qualities you can’t blame on nature: sincerity, seriousness, stamina for hard work, being content with little, kindness, frankness, not wasting time on trifles. None of them needs talent. Each is a decision you make on an ordinary Tuesday.

On a real team they often matter more than raw speed. The person who tells the truth about a slipped deadline, sticks with an ugly bug until it’s actually fixed and doesn’t gossip about the other squad is the one everyone wants on the next project. You can’t choose how sharp you are. You can choose all of that.
