## October 23rd - EMBRACE THE QUALITIES YOU CONTROL

***“People aren’t in awe of your sharp mind? So be it. But you have many other qualities you can’t claim to have been deprived of at birth. Display then those qualities in your own power: honesty, dignity, endurance, chastity, contentment, frugality, kindness, freedom, persistence, avoiding gossip, and magnanimity.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 5.5

In the tech world, it’s easy to feel overshadowed by those with natural talent, sharp intellect, or an impressive resume. But as Marcus Aurelius points out, your worth isn’t determined by attributes you were born with it’s shaped by the virtues you choose to cultivate.

You can’t control your starting point, but you can control how you act and grow. Honesty, kindness, persistence, and humility aren’t limited by circumstances or innate abilities. They’re choices you can make every day, in every interaction. And these qualities, built through effort, often leave a deeper impression than any natural gift.

Today, focus on displaying the virtues that are fully within your control. Be honest in your work, kind to your teammates, persistent in your challenges, and free from negativity. These are the qualities that define true greatness qualities anyone can cultivate, including you.
