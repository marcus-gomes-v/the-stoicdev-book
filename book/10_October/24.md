October 24th - GOODNESS STARTS WITHIN
“Dig deep within yourself, for there is a fountain of goodness ever ready to flow if you will keep digging.” —MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 7.59
In life and in work, it’s easy to look outward for sources of goodness—waiting for someone to praise us, for a project to succeed, or for circumstances to align perfectly. But Marcus Aurelius reminds us that goodness isn’t something we find externally. It begins within us, flowing through our thoughts, choices, and actions.
Whether it’s in a stressful meeting or a moment of quiet focus, you have the power to create goodness. A kind word to a struggling colleague, extra effort on a project, or patience in the face of frustration—these are all ways to tap into the fountain of goodness within you.
Today, instead of waiting for goodness to come your way, be the one who brings it. Dig deep and let your actions reflect the best parts of yourself. The more you draw from that fountain, the more it flows, not just for you, but for everyone around you.
