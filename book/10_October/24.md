## October 24th - GOODNESS STARTS WITHIN

***“Look within. Within is the fountain of good, and it will ever bubble up, if thou wilt ever dig.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 7.59

A lot of us wait for the good stuff to arrive from outside. A kind word in the review. A launch that goes well. A manager who finally notices. When none of it comes, the day feels empty and we feel entitled to be a little worse in return: curt in the thread, careless in the code, absent in the meeting.

Marcus points the other way. The source isn’t out there. It’s inside you, and it keeps flowing as long as you keep digging.

Digging is a plain, unglamorous thing. It’s answering the confused question in the channel properly, even though it’s the third time this week. It’s writing the commit message for the person who will read it in two years. It’s staying patient with the stakeholder who changed the requirements again. None of these depend on anyone else being nice to you first.

The strange part is that the more you draw from it, the more there seems to be. People who give a lot at work rarely run dry. People who wait usually do.
