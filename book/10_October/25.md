October 25th - MASTERING THE ART OF LIVING

“What, then, makes a person free from hindrance and self-determining? For wealth doesn’t, neither does high-office, state or kingdom—rather, something else must be found . . . in the case of living, it is the knowledge of how to live.” —EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 4.1.62-64

Life’s most essential tasks are simple yet profound: to be a good person and to pursue what you love. Everything else—chasing wealth, status, or external validation—is secondary. Epictetus reminds us that freedom and fulfillment come not from what we have but from understanding how to live well.

In the tech world, it’s easy to get consumed by distractions—pursuing shiny new tools, reacting to outside pressures, or chasing fleeting goals. But the key is to stay focused on what matters: aligning your actions with your values and pouring your energy into work that excites and fulfills you. Every line of code, every interaction with a teammate, every choice is a chance to live with integrity and purpose.

Today, ask yourself: What is the best use of my time and skills? How can I approach this moment with kindness and focus? The art of living is learned one decision at a time—start practicing now.
