October 26th - THE THREE PILLARS OF LIVING WELL
“The best and the greatest number of authors have asserted that philosophy consists of three parts: the moral, the natural, and the rational. The first puts the soul in order. The second thoroughly examines the natural order of things. The third inquires into the proper meaning of words, and their arrangements and proofs which keep falsehoods from creeping in to displace truth.” —SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 89.9
Philosophy, as Seneca outlines, is not just an abstract exercise but a practical guide for living. Its three pillars—moral, natural, and rational—work together to shape a life of integrity, understanding, and clarity. Morality governs your actions and relationships. Nature reminds you of your place in the larger order of things. Reason ensures that your thoughts are clear, logical, and aligned with truth.
In the tech world, these principles are just as applicable. Moral wisdom helps you act ethically, whether leading a team or building a product. Understanding nature fosters humility and a sense of connection to the bigger picture. Rational thinking helps you discern truth, cut through noise, and avoid pitfalls like bias or faulty assumptions.
Today, let these three parts guide your decisions. Whether solving a problem, navigating a challenge, or planning your next move, align your actions with morality, your perspective with nature, and your thoughts with reason. This is how you live a good life—right here, right now.
