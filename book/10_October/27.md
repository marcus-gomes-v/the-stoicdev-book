October 27th - THE CONSEQUENCES OF OUR EXAMPLES

“Crimes often return to their teacher.” —SENECA, THYESTES, 311

The people we influence—whether colleagues, clients, or mentees—learn from our actions as much as our words. Seneca’s relationship with Nero is a stark reminder of this truth. Even if Seneca tried to be a moral compass, his association with Nero left him complicit in the emperor’s atrocities, and ultimately, it cost him his life.

In the tech industry, where leadership and collaboration shape outcomes, your example matters immensely. If you cut corners, encourage unethical shortcuts, or act without integrity, those who learn from you may replicate and amplify those behaviors—potentially against you or others. Conversely, leading with honesty and principle fosters trust, loyalty, and a culture that values the right things.

Today, reflect on the example you set. Are you teaching others to act with integrity and care, or are you sowing seeds that could one day harm you—or the environment you’ve worked hard to build? Remember, the impact of your actions ripples outward and often finds its way back. Choose wisely.
