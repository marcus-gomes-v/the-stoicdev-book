## October 28th - BUILT FOR CONNECTION

***“You’ll more quickly find an earthly thing kept from the earth than you will a person cut off from other human beings.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 9.9.3

In the tech world, where remote work and siloed tasks are common, it’s easy to forget how deeply connected we are to others. Marcus Aurelius reminds us that mutual interdependence is not just a social norm it’s part of what makes us human. Just as gravity pulls objects to the earth, our nature draws us toward collaboration, connection, and care for one another.

Philosophy and adversity can sometimes lead us to isolate ourselves, focusing inward or on personal struggles. But no amount of knowledge or self-sufficiency can replace the support and strength we gain from others. Helping a teammate debug a problem, sharing knowledge, or simply listening when someone is struggling these are not distractions. They are part of living fully and fulfilling our role as social beings.

Today, make time for connection. Ask how you can help. Be open to receiving help. Remember, we were made to work together, grow together, and support one another. It’s not just good for others it’s essential for you, too.
