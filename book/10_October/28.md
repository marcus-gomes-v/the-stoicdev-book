## October 28th - BUILT FOR CONNECTION

***“Sooner, then, will one find anything earthy which comes in contact with no earthy thing, than a man altogether separated from other men.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 9.9

Remote work made it possible to go a whole week without hearing a colleague’s voice. Headphones on, tickets in, pull requests out. Some weeks that feels like peace. After a few months it can start to feel like something is missing, even if you couldn’t say what.

Marcus’s image is physical. Earth falls toward earth, and you would sooner find a clod of soil touching no other soil than a person cut off from everyone else. In the same passage he admits that people try. Their nature catches them anyway.

Developers are good at trying. We tell ourselves we work best alone, that asking for help slows everyone down, that a hard stretch is something to get through quietly. Then the bug that ate two days falls apart in ten minutes once you explain it to someone. The bad month gets lighter the moment you tell one teammate the truth about it.

Turn the camera on now and then. Ask the question in the channel instead of struggling in private. Offer to pair on something that isn’t yours. You weren’t built to run as an isolated process, and neither was anyone on your team.
