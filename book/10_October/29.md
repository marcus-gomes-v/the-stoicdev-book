## October 29th - CHARACTER OVER CREDENTIALS

***“We each give ourselves our character; our work is handed out by chance. Let some dine with you because they are worthy, and others so that they may become worthy.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 47.15

Seneca wrote this in a letter about slaves, at a time when most Romans wouldn’t share a table with the people who served them. His point was that a job is handed out by chance. Character is the one thing each person makes for themselves.

Tech has its own seating chart. The engineers from famous schools and big-name companies get the interesting projects. The bootcamp grad, the support engineer who taught herself to code, the contractor on the night shift are kept at a polite distance from the design decisions.

When you’re hiring, or choosing who joins a project, look past the résumé to the person. Look for the one who owns their mistakes and keeps learning when nobody is checking. And take the second half of Seneca’s advice seriously. Invite some people in because they’ve earned it, and some so that they can. A seat in the architecture review is often how someone becomes the person who deserves it.
