## October 30th - PRIORITIZE WISDOM

***“Are you not ashamed to reserve only the leavings of your life for yourself, and appoint for the enjoyment of your own right mind only that time which you cannot devote to any business?”***
—SENECA, ON THE BREVITY OF LIFE, 3.5

Look at your calendar for next week. Standups, planning, two incident reviews, a one-on-one, a block called “focus time” that someone will book over by Tuesday. Now find the hour set aside for thinking. Not for a ticket. For thinking about how you work, what you’re learning, and whether the direction you’re heading in is one you actually chose.

For most of us it isn’t there. We plan to read that book on the train, to reflect on the year during the holidays, to sort out the career question once this release is out. Seneca saw the same habit in Rome: people promising themselves leisure at fifty, and giving their own minds only the scraps that business didn’t want.

He thought that was something to be ashamed of. The quality of your decisions, at work and outside it, comes out of that neglected hour. Put it on the calendar first, before the meetings arrive, and defend it the way you would defend a code freeze.
