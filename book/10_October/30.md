## October 30th - PRIORITIZE WISDOM

***“Aren’t you ashamed to reserve for yourself only the remnants of your life and to dedicate to wisdom only that time can’t be directed to business?”***
—SENECA, ON THE BREVITY OF LIFE, 3.5b

In our fast-paced, tech-driven world, it's easy to fill our schedules with meetings, tasks, and projects while leaving little time for reflection or personal growth. Seneca challenges us to rethink this approach. If philosophy or self-improvement is essential to living a good life, why do we relegate it to the scraps of our day, squeezed in when everything else is done?

Like Alexander the Great carving up conquered territories, you should take command of your time and allocate a lion’s share to what truly matters. Wisdom and reflection aren’t luxuries they’re the foundation for making better decisions, building stronger relationships, and living with purpose.

Today, make time for thinking, learning, and self-improvement. Block it out on your calendar as you would a meeting or a deadline. Don’t let philosophy accept your leftovers; give it the priority it deserves. Everything else in your life will benefit as a result.
