## October 31st - RETURN TO YOUR NATURE

***“The human being is born with an inclination toward virtue.”***
—MUSONIUS RUFUS, LECTURES, 2.7.1-2

Unlike the idea of original sin, Stoicism teaches that humans are naturally inclined toward goodness and virtue. Our survival as a species depended on cooperation, empathy, and fairness qualities deeply rooted in who we are. If we stray from this, it’s not because we are inherently flawed, but because we’ve allowed the wrong influences and ideas to shape us.

In the tech world, where competition and ego can sometimes overshadow collaboration, this reminder is vital: you were born with the capacity for fairness, honesty, and kindness. These virtues are not foreign concepts they’re part of your core. Philosophy, as Musonius and Seneca emphasize, is a tool to peel back the layers of cynicism or bad habits and bring you closer to your true nature.

Today, reconnect with that nature. Approach your work and relationships with an openness to fairness and virtue. Let philosophy guide you back to the person you were always meant to be: someone who builds, supports, and thrives through goodness.
