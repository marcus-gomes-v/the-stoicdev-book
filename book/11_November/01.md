## November 1st - EMBRACE WHAT HAPPENS

***“Do not seek to have everything that happens happen as you wish, but wish for everything to happen as it actually does happen, and your life will be serene.”***
—EPICTETUS, ENCHIRIDION, 8

***“From everything that happens in the universe it is easy for a man to find occasion to praise providence, if he has within himself these two qualities: the faculty of taking a comprehensive view of what has happened in each individual instance, and the sense of gratitude. Otherwise, one man will not see the usefulness of what has happened, and another, even if he does see it, will not be grateful therefor.”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 1.6.1-2

The release was supposed to go out Thursday. On Wednesday night a dependency ships a breaking change, the build goes red, and the plan for the week is gone. You can spend the evening wishing it hadn’t happened. It did. The only question left is what you do with Thursday.

Epictetus asks for more than putting up with it. He asks you to want things to happen the way they actually happen, since that is the only version of the week on offer.

The second passage goes a step further. Look closely at what happened and you can usually find a use in it: the version pin you should have had all along, the integration test nobody wrote, a line in the release checklist about upstream changes. Seeing that takes attention. Being glad of it takes gratitude, and most of us have the first without the second. We find the lesson and still resent it.

Try to have both. The week goes better once you stop arguing with it.
