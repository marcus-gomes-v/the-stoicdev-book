November 2nd - ALIGN YOUR WILL WITH FATE
“But I haven’t at any time been hindered in my will, nor forced against it. And how is this possible? I have bound up my choice to act with the will of God. God wills that I be sick, such is my will. He wills that I should choose something, so do I. He wills that I reach for something, or something be given to me—I wish for the same. What God doesn’t will, I do not wish for.” —EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 4.1.89
No matter how meticulously we prepare or how skilled we are, the final outcome is often beyond our control. General Eisenhower understood this on the eve of D-Day. He had done everything in his power to ensure success, yet he accepted that fate would ultimately decide. Epictetus teaches us to bind our wishes to what happens, embracing the outcome as if we had willed it ourselves.
In the tech world, where even the best plans can be derailed by unforeseen bugs, client demands, or market shifts, this mindset is crucial. Do your best, prepare thoroughly, but accept that some outcomes are beyond your influence. What matters is not the result, but how you respond to it.
Today, align your efforts with preparation and your attitude with acceptance. Work diligently, but let go of the need to control everything. When you match your will to what happens, you find peace, even in uncertainty.
