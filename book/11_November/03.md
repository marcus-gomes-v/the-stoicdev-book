November 3rd - ACCEPT WHAT NATURE PRESCRIBES
“Just as we commonly hear people say the doctor prescribed someone particular riding exercises, or ice baths, or walking without shoes, we should in the same way say that nature prescribed someone to be diseased, or disabled, or to suffer any kind of impairment. In the case of the doctor, prescribed means something ordered to help aid someone’s healing. But in the case of nature, it means that what happens to each of us is ordered to help aid our destiny.” —MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 5.8
When a doctor prescribes a treatment, even if it’s unpleasant or inconvenient, we usually comply without question because we trust it’s for our benefit. Marcus Aurelius challenges us to apply the same mindset to life itself. What if every difficulty, setback, or unexpected event was prescribed by nature to shape and strengthen us, like a remedy for the soul?
In tech, challenges—like debugging a relentless issue or dealing with a difficult client—can feel like disruptions to your plans. But what if these “prescriptions” are exactly what you need to grow? Just as medicine heals the body, adversity strengthens the mind and character.
Today, approach life’s difficulties as if they were a custom treatment plan for your personal growth. Trust that these prescriptions, though uncomfortable, are part of nature’s design to aid your progress. Resistance only delays the healing; acceptance accelerates it.
