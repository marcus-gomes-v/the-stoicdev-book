## November 4th - EMBRACE CHANGE WITHOUT JUDGMENT

***“There is no evil in things changing, just as there is no good in persisting in a new state.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 4.42

In tech, change is constant new tools, shifting requirements, evolving markets. While some see these changes as opportunities and others as threats, Marcus Aurelius reminds us that change itself is neutral. It’s not inherently good or bad; it simply is. Our perception and response determine its impact.

When a process gets overhauled or a project pivots unexpectedly, resist the urge to label it as a setback or a win. Instead, focus on adapting and making the most of the situation. Fighting against change only drains energy that could be spent on progress.

Today, when change arises, pause before forming an opinion. Look at it objectively and ask: What can I do with this? By ceasing to fight or fear change, you create space for growth, resilience, and opportunity. Remember, the power lies not in controlling change but in how you adapt to it.
