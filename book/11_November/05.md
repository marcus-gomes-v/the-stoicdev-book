November 5th - LET GO OF CONTROL

“This is the very thing which makes up the virtue of the happy person and a well-flowing life—when the affairs of life are in every way tuned to the harmony between the individual divine spirit and the will of the director of the universe.” —CHRYSIPPUS, QUOTED IN DIOGENES LAERTIUS, LIVES OF THE EMINENT PHILOSOPHERS, 7.1.88

In tech, as in life, the desire for control can be overwhelming—control over outcomes, timelines, or how others behave. But as Chrysippus and twelve-step programs remind us, letting go of the illusion of control brings freedom and peace. The key isn’t believing in a divine plan but acknowledging that not everything is within your power and that trying to micromanage the universe only breeds frustration.

When systems crash or projects veer off course, instead of resisting reality, align yourself with it. Accept the limits of your influence and focus on what you can control—your effort, your mindset, and your ability to adapt.

Today, release the need to control everything. Trust the flow of events, and find harmony by working with reality, not against it. You’ll discover that life flows much more smoothly when you stop trying to be the director of the universe.
