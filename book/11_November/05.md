## November 5th - LET GO OF CONTROL

***“And this very thing constitutes the virtue of the happy man and the smooth current of life, when all actions promote the harmony of the spirit dwelling in the individual man with the will of him who orders the universe.”***
—CHRYSIPPUS, QUOTED IN DIOGENES LAERTIUS, LIVES OF THE EMINENT PHILOSOPHERS, 7.1.88

Some tech leads review every pull request personally, rewrite the estimates other people gave, and check the dashboards at midnight in case something moved. It looks like responsibility. Mostly it is fear, and it wears everyone out, starting with the lead.

Chrysippus, who is said to have written more than seven hundred books, described the good life as a smooth current. You get there when what you do is in tune with the order of things instead of fighting it. You can call that order God, or nature, or just the way any system behaves once enough people are involved. Much of what happens on a project was never going to obey you.

Decide where your hands belong: your code, your reviews, how clearly you write and speak. Let the rest run. Keep watching it closely enough to step in when it actually needs you, and no more often than that.
