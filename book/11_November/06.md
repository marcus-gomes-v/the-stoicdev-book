November 6th - LIFE SPINS ON A WHIM

“If the breaking day sees someone proud, the ending day sees them brought low. No one should put too much trust in triumph, no one should give up hope of trials improving. Clotho mixes one with the other and stops Fortune from resting, spinning every fate around. No one has had so much divine favor that they could guarantee themselves tomorrow. God keeps our lives hurtling on, spinning in a whirlwind.” —SENECA, THYESTES, 613

In the tech world, fortunes can change rapidly—a startup secures funding one day and faces layoffs the next, or a bug turns into a breakthrough. Seneca’s reminder is clear: life’s twists and turns are beyond our control, and the thread of fate is spun by forces outside of us.

Instead of clinging to triumph or despairing in hardship, embrace the uncertainty. Success is fleeting, as are setbacks. What matters is how you respond. Do you stay grounded and humble in the good times? Do you remain hopeful and persistent in the tough ones?

Today, remember that life’s unpredictability is constant. Focus not on controlling Fortune, but on meeting each twist and turn with resilience, gratitude, and grace. The thread may spin, but your character remains steady.
