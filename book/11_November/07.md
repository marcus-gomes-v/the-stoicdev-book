## November 7th - TRUE POWER COMES FROM WITHIN

***“In what did he trust? Not in reputation, or money, or office, but in his own might, that means, his judgements about the things which are under our control, and those which are not under our control. For these are the only things that make men free, that make men unhampered, that lift up the neck of those who have become abject, that make them look with level eyes into the faces of the rich, and the faces of tyrants.”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 3.26.34-35

Epictetus is talking about Odysseus, thrown ashore naked after a shipwreck and walking up to a group of young women to ask for help. No ship, no crew, no clothes. What did he trust? Not his reputation, his money or his rank, but his own judgment about what was his to handle and what wasn’t.

Your shipwreck will be quieter. The company gets acquired and your team is dissolved. The title you worked toward vanishes in a reorg. You’re in an interview loop for the first time in eight years, and nobody across the table has heard of the system you’re proudest of.

What you still have is how you think. The judgment that tells you which problem to fix first. The calm you keep in a room full of panicking people. Those never belonged to the old company. They come with you, and they let you look anyone in the eye, the hiring manager and the CEO included.
