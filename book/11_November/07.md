## November 7th - TRUE POWER COMES FROM WITHIN

***“Don’t trust in your reputation, money, or position, but in the strength that is yours—namely, your judgments about the things that you control and don’t control. For this alone is what makes us free and unfettered, that picks us up by the neck from the depths and lifts us eye to eye with the rich and powerful.”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 3.26.34-35

True power isn’t about the size of your team, the projects you’ve delivered, or the accolades you’ve received. It’s about mastering yourself your desires, fears, and impulses. Diogenes the Cynic, with nothing but his self-mastery, was more powerful than Alexander the Great because he had conquered the need for conquest itself.

In tech, where achievements and status often dominate the culture, it’s easy to tie your worth to external measures. But as Epictetus reminds us, real strength lies in understanding what you can and cannot control. Fame, fortune, and titles are fleeting, but self-mastery is enduring.

Today, focus inward. Are you ruling over yourself? Are you free from the pull of ego or the need for external validation? True power doesn’t come from what you possess or control it comes from who you are. Start building that strength, and you’ll find yourself truly unstoppable.
