November 8th - PLAY YOUR ROLE WELL
“Remember that you are an actor in a play, playing a character according to the will of the playwright—if a short play, then it’s short; if long, long. If he wishes you to play the beggar, play even that role well, just as you would if it were a cripple, a honcho, or an everyday person. For this is your duty, to perform well the character assigned you. That selection belongs to another.” —EPICTETUS, ENCHIRIDION, 17
Life doesn’t always hand us the roles we want. Marcus Aurelius didn’t ask to be emperor, and Epictetus didn’t choose to be a slave, yet both accepted their circumstances and acted with excellence within them. Whether your current role in tech is at the forefront or behind the scenes, it’s your duty to play it well—because what you do matters, no matter the scale.
Some are born into privilege, while others must navigate adversity. Some projects feel perfectly aligned with your aspirations, while others seem like burdens. But what if, instead of resisting, you embraced your role? Use it as a stepping stone, a lesson, or a way to hone your craft.
Today, focus on excelling in the role you’ve been given, whatever it may be. Whether a supporting act or a starring role, give it your best. Accept your place with gratitude and determination, knowing that mastery of any role prepares you for the next stage—whatever it might be.
