## November 8th - PLAY YOUR ROLE WELL

***“Remember that you are an actor in a play, the character of which is determined by the Playwright: if He wishes the play to be short, it is short; if long, it is long; if He wishes you to play the part of a beggar, remember to act even this rôle adroitly; and so if your rôle be that of a cripple, an official, or a layman. For this is your business, to play admirably the rôle assigned you; but the selection of that rôle is Another’s.”***
—EPICTETUS, ENCHIRIDION, 17

Not every role on a team is one you would have picked. Someone has to own the billing integration nobody understands. Someone has to stay on the maintenance team while the new product gets the budget and the conference talks. Sometimes that someone is you, and the decision was made in a meeting you weren’t in.

Epictetus knew about parts handed out by someone else. He was born a slave and was lame for much of his life. His advice is blunt: you don’t choose the part, and you don’t decide how long the play runs. Your business is to play it well.

Playing it well looks like the maintenance engineer who writes the clearest incident reports in the company. Or the developer stuck with the billing code who finally documents how it works, so the next person doesn’t suffer the way they did. Nobody gives a talk about that work. It says more about you than any project you got to choose.
