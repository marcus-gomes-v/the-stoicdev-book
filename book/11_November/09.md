## November 9th - EVERYTHING FLOWS, NOTHING STAYS

***“The universe is transformation: life is opinion.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 4.3

Run git blame on a five-year-old project and look for a line from the first commit. Often there isn’t one. Every file has been rewritten, renamed, split or deleted, and most of the people who wrote the originals have moved on. Everyone still calls it the same product.

Marcus says it in one line: the universe is transformation, life is opinion. The first half describes the world, your codebase included. Nothing holds still. The second half is about you. Whether the change feels like loss or progress depends mostly on the story you tell about it.

The developer who joined when the stack was new tends to tell a sad story. The one who joined last month doesn’t know there was an old stack. Same repository, two opinions.

You’ve changed just as much. The code you wrote three years ago probably makes you wince, and that’s a good sign. The process works on people too, if you let it keep working.
