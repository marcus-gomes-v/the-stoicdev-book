## November 9th - EVERYTHING FLOWS, NOTHING STAYS

***“The universe is change. Life is opinion.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 4.3.4b

In tech, where progress and iteration are constant, Marcus Aurelius’s observation feels especially relevant. The tools we use evolve, systems are replaced, and what seems cutting-edge today will eventually become obsolete. Even your team and your own role are subject to the relentless flow of change.

Like the ship of Theseus or a rebuilt Shinto shrine, the essence of something isn’t fixed it’s shaped by continuous adaptation. Your work, your relationships, and even your sense of self are all part of this ongoing transformation. Resisting change only creates tension; embracing it opens the door to growth.

Today, reflect on how change has shaped you and your environment. Instead of clinging to what was or fearing what’s next, lean into the fluid nature of life. Recognize that transformation doesn’t erase value it creates it. You’re not who you were yesterday, and that’s a good thing. Keep moving, keep growing, and stay open to what’s to come.
