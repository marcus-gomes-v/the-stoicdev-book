November 10th - THE ETERNAL CYCLE

“Think by way of example on the times of Vespasian, and you’ll see all these things: marrying, raising children, falling ill, dying, wars, holiday feasts, commerce, farming, flattering, pretending, suspecting, scheming, praying that others die, grumbling over one’s lot, falling in love, amassing fortunes, lusting after office and power. Now that life of theirs is dead and gone... the times of Trajan, again the same...” —MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 4.32

In tech, where innovation moves fast and trends are constantly shifting, it’s easy to feel like everything is new and unprecedented. But Marcus Aurelius reminds us that, at its core, human life and its patterns are timeless. The tools may change, but ambition, love, frustration, and joy remain the same across generations.

This perspective is grounding. It shows that the challenges you face today are not unique—they’ve been experienced in some form by countless others before you. Similarly, the victories and breakthroughs that feel so fresh are part of a long continuum of human progress.

Today, let this timelessness bring you clarity. Step back from the chaos and remind yourself that what feels urgent and novel is part of a larger, enduring cycle. Approach your work and life with humility and perspective—knowing that, while you are a small part of history, your actions still contribute to its endless rhythm.
