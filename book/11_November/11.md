## November 11th - REDEFINE YOUR CHALLENGES

***“When you are distressed by an external thing, it’s not the thing itself that troubles you, but only your judgment of it. And you can wipe this out at a moment’s notice.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 8.47

Franklin Delano Roosevelt could have let polio define him as a victim. Instead, he redefined the challenge, proving that his physical limitations did not limit his ambition or ability to lead. His greatness came not from avoiding adversity but from facing it with resilience and perspective.

In tech, challenges are inevitable be it a failed launch, a sudden change in priorities, or a setback in your career. But as Marcus Aurelius reminds us, the event itself isn’t what causes distress; it’s how we perceive and react to it. A failure can be a lesson, a delay can create space for improvement, and a detour might lead to innovation.

Today, when faced with a challenge, pause and examine your judgment of it. Could this setback be reframed as an opportunity? Could this difficulty strengthen you in ways you don’t yet see? Acceptance doesn’t mean passivity it means choosing how to respond with clarity and purpose.
