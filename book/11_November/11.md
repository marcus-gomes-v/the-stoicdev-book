## November 11th - REDEFINE YOUR CHALLENGES

***“If thou art pained by any external thing, it is not this thing that disturbs thee, but thy own judgment about it. And it is in thy power to wipe out this judgment now.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 8.47

In a short one-on-one, your manager tells you the promotion went to someone else this cycle. You walk back to your desk and the story starts writing itself. They never liked you. Your work doesn’t count. You should have left a year ago.

Marcus would ask you to separate two things. There is the event: a committee picked another name. Then there is everything you added on that walk back. The first came from outside. The second came from you, and what you built you can take apart, now, without a week of stewing first.

That doesn’t mean pretending you’re fine. Ask for the feedback in writing and read it honestly. Maybe there’s a real gap, maybe the process is political, maybe it was simply close. Each of those leads somewhere different, and none of them needs the story about nobody valuing you.

The disappointment is real. The rest of the weight you can put down.
