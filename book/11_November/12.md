## November 12th - OWN YOUR CHOICES

***“But if we judge only those things which are in our power to be good or bad, there remains no reason either for finding fault with God or standing in a hostile attitude to man.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 6.41

The postmortem starts and you can feel the room looking for somewhere to put the blame. The vendor’s SDK. The product manager who pushed the date. The intern who merged without a second review. All of it is partly true, which is what makes it so tempting.

Marcus saw where that hostility comes from. If the outcome is what you count as good or bad, then whoever stands between you and the outcome becomes an enemy. Count only your own choices as good or bad and there’s nobody left to be angry at. The SDK did what SDKs do. The date was the date.

What’s left is the question you can actually answer: what did I decide, and what would I decide differently? Maybe you saw the risk in planning and didn’t push hard enough. Maybe you approved that review with half your attention.

Say that part out loud in the postmortem. People stop defending themselves and start fixing the system.
