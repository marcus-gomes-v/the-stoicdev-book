November 12th - OWN YOUR CHOICES

“If we judge as good and evil only the things in the power of our own choice, then there is no room left for blaming gods or being hostile to others.” —MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 6.41

Harry Truman’s famous mantra, “The buck stops here,” serves as a powerful reminder of personal responsibility. As president, he couldn’t shift blame or dodge accountability. Similarly, in our own lives, the chain of responsibility ends with us. We can’t control everything, but we can always control how we respond to events.

In tech, where team dynamics and external pressures often complicate outcomes, it’s tempting to assign blame—to the market, to a colleague, or to a deadline. But as Marcus Aurelius teaches, the strong don’t waste time blaming others or external forces. They focus on what they can control: their actions, their attitudes, and their choices.

Today, embrace your role as the leader of your own life. Don’t pass the buck. Accept responsibility for your responses and decisions, knowing that this is where true strength lies. When you focus on what’s in your power, you free yourself from excuses—and that’s where growth begins.
