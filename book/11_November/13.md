November 13th - SILENCE YOUR COMPLAINTS
“Don’t allow yourself to be heard any longer griping about public life, not even with your own ears!” —MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 8.9
Complaining might feel cathartic in the moment, but it rarely solves problems or eases your burdens. Marcus Aurelius, despite his immense responsibilities, reminds us to avoid grumbling about the challenges we face. Complaining only amplifies negativity, both for ourselves and for those around us.
In the tech world, where high-pressure environments and shifting demands are common, complaining about the workload, deadlines, or clients can quickly spiral into a toxic habit. Instead of focusing on frustrations, channel that energy into action. Solve the problem, improve the process, or simply accept the situation and move forward with composure.
Today, adopt Benjamin Disraeli’s motto: “Never complain, never explain.” Silence the inner voice of discontent and focus on what you can do instead. By keeping a positive and proactive mindset, you’ll find that your burdens feel lighter and your impact greater.
