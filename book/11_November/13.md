## November 13th - SILENCE YOUR COMPLAINTS

***“Let no man any longer hear thee finding fault with the court life or with thy own.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 8.9

Every company has a channel, or a lunch table, where people go to complain. The roadmap is wrong, leadership is clueless, the build is slow. It feels like relief while it lasts. An hour later nothing has changed, except that you’re a little angrier and so is everyone who listened.

Marcus lived most of his life at the center of the imperial court, and he wrote himself this note: let no one hear you finding fault with court life any more, or with your own. He had more to complain about than most of us. He still decided it wasn’t worth the breath.

Raising a problem is different. It goes to someone who can act on it, with a proposal attached. A complaint goes in a circle and comes back to you.

So the next time the build is slow, skip the rant. Open a ticket with the numbers, or spend an afternoon on the cache. If you can do neither, let it go quietly.
