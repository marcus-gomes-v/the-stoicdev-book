## November 14th - YOUR REACTION DEFINES THE OUTCOME

***“He was carried off to prison. What happened? He was carried off to prison. But the observation: ‘He has fared ill,’ is an addition that each man makes on his own responsibility.”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 3.8.5-6

The deploy failed and had to be rolled back. What happened? The deploy failed and was rolled back. That’s the whole fact.

Then come the additions. This is a disaster. I’m going to look incompetent. The team will never trust my changes again. None of that is in the logs. You wrote it yourself, in the thirty seconds after the alert fired.

Epictetus drills his students to answer events the way they’d answer a trick question in logic class, plainly and without commentary. His son is dead. His ship is lost. He was carried off to prison. Calling any of it a misfortune is something each of us adds on our own responsibility.

This isn’t pretending the rollback was nice. It cost time, and a customer may have noticed. But once you stop piling weight on it, you can see what’s needed: a clear note in the incident channel, a look at why the canary didn’t catch it, a fix. That’s work you can do.
