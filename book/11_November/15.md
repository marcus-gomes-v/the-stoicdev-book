## November 15th - EMBRACE THE FLOW OF CHANGE

***“Often think of the rapidity with which things pass by and disappear, both the things which are and the things which are produced. For substance is like a river in a continual flow, and the activities of things are in constant change, and the causes work in infinite varieties; and there is hardly anything which stands still.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 5.23

Open the dependency file of a project you wrote two years ago. Half the libraries are deprecated. One has been abandoned by its maintainer. The framework is three major versions behind, and the tutorials that taught you how to use it have gone dark.

Marcus tells himself to think often about how fast things pass. He means it as a correction more than a gloomy exercise. We keep acting as if the current setup is the permanent one, and then we’re surprised every time it moves.

A river is a fair picture of a codebase. You can stand on the bank complaining that the water is never the same, or you can learn to read the current. Pin what needs pinning. Write code that’s easy to delete. Don’t build your identity around a tool that will be legacy in five years.

And don’t take it personally when the thing you mastered stops mattering. That’s what things do. The habit of learning the next one is what you keep.
