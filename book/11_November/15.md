## November 15th - EMBRACE THE FLOW OF CHANGE

***“Meditate often on the swiftness with which all that exists and is coming into being is swept by us and carried away. For substance is like a river’s unending flow, its activities continually changing and causes infinitely shifting so that almost nothing at all stands still.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 5.23

Heraclitus famously said, “No man steps in the same river twice.” The river flows, ever-changing, and so do we. Marcus Aurelius builds on this, reminding us that life is a constant current one of transformation, motion, and impermanence.

In tech, change is not just inevitable; it’s essential. Tools evolve, methodologies shift, and markets demand adaptation. Resisting change leads to frustration, but embracing it allows you to grow. A failing project can redirect to an unexpected success. A new technology can inspire innovation. The flow of change brings both challenges and opportunities.

Today, remind yourself that nothing stays still not your problems, not your successes, not even you. Instead of clinging to the way things were or fearing what’s to come, step into the current with grace. Adapt, learn, and flow with life’s changes they are what keep us moving forward.
