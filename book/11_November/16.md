## November 16th - LIVE BEYOND HOPE AND FEAR

***“Hecato says, ‘cease to hope and you will cease to fear.’ . . . The primary cause of both these ills is that instead of adapting ourselves to present circumstances we send out thoughts too far ahead.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 5.7b-8

Hope and fear might seem like opposites one optimistic, the other pessimistic but to the Stoics, they are two sides of the same coin. Both pull us out of the present and project us into an uncertain future. Hope desires what we want to happen; fear dreads what we don’t. Both distract us from where we have the most power: this moment, right now.

In tech, hope can look like dreaming of a flawless launch or a big promotion, while fear is the dread of a catastrophic failure or rejection. Both take your focus away from what truly matters: the work at hand. Instead of hoping for a perfect outcome or fearing a bad one, channel that energy into acting with clarity and effort in the present.

Today, practice letting go of both hope and fear. Embrace the now, not with worry or longing but with acceptance and action. By focusing on what you control in this moment, you free yourself from the emotional tug-of-war and align with reality as it unfolds.
