November 18th - ALIGN YOUR MIND WITH STOIC PRINCIPLES

“Our rational nature moves freely forward in its impressions when it: 1) accepts nothing false or uncertain; 2) directs its impulses only to acts for the common good; 3) limits its desires and aversions only to what’s in its own power; 4) embraces everything nature assigns it.” —MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 8.7

Even Marcus Aurelius needed reminders of Stoic habits to keep his thoughts and actions aligned with his philosophy. Whether it was a challenging day or simply a need to refocus, these principles acted as a guide for maintaining a steady mind and a virtuous life.

In the fast-moving tech world, where uncertainty, competition, and shifting goals are the norm, these habits are equally valuable:

Accept only what is true: Question assumptions and base decisions on facts, not speculation.

Work for the common good: Contribute to the team and the community, not just personal gain.

Focus on what’s in your control: Let go of what you can’t influence and direct energy toward what you can.

Embrace what comes your way: Treat setbacks as opportunities and successes as gifts, not entitlements.

Today, take a moment to check in with yourself. Are you embodying these habits? Let them guide your actions and decisions, helping you navigate challenges with clarity and purpose.
