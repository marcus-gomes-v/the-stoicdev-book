## November 18th - ALIGN YOUR MIND WITH STOIC PRINCIPLES

***“A rational nature goes on its way well when in its thoughts it assents to nothing false or uncertain, and when it directs its movements to social acts only, and when it confines its desires and aversions to the things which are in its power, and when it is satisfied with everything that is assigned to it by the common nature.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 8.7

This passage reads almost like a checklist, and Marcus may well have used it as one. A rational mind is on the right path, he says, when it assents to nothing false or uncertain, aims its actions at the common good, wants and avoids only what lies in its own power, and accepts whatever nature assigns it.

Run a hard day at work through those four. The incident report blames the database, so check whether that’s known or just the first plausible guess. You’re about to argue for the design that makes your own team look good, so ask whether it’s better for the people who use the product. The launch slipped and you’re furious, though the date was never yours to set. And the reorg nobody asked for is here either way, which leaves only the question of what you’ll do with it.

None of this needs ceremony. Marcus wrote these notes to himself, some of them in army camps on the Danube frontier, between everything else he had to do. A few honest minutes at the end of the day are enough to notice where you drifted.
