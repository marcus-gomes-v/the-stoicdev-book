November 19th - TRUST THE PATH
“For any challenge we should hold three thoughts at our command:
 ‘Lead on God and Destiny,
 To that Goal fixed for me long ago.
 I will follow and not stumble; even if my will
 is weak I will soldier on.’” —CLEANTHES
“Whoever embraces necessity counts as wise, skilled in divine matters.” —EURIPIDES
“If it pleases the gods, so be it. They may well kill me, but they can’t hurt me.” —PLATO’S CRITO AND APOLOGY
—EPICTETUS, ENCHIRIDION, 53
Life unfolds in ways that often seem random, unfair, or overwhelming. These three maxims, compiled by Epictetus, remind us to trust in the larger forces at play—whether you call them fate, destiny, or simply the natural order of the universe. They ask us to let go of resistance and embrace what happens as part of a plan, even if we don’t fully understand it.
In tech, where unexpected failures or pivots can feel like setbacks, this mindset is invaluable. Instead of resisting the changes or lamenting the difficulties, see them as necessary steps in a bigger process. A failed project could teach you resilience; an unexpected outcome might lead to innovation.
Today, practice acceptance by imagining that everything happening is meant to guide you. Whether it’s success or struggle, see it as a step on the path designed for your growth. Let this perspective free you from frustration and help you embrace whatever comes with grace and determination.
