November 20th - THIS MOMENT IS ETERNAL
“If you’ve seen the present, you’ve seen all things, from time immemorial into all of eternity. For everything that happens is related and the same.” —MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 6.37
Life may feel chaotic and unpredictable, but Marcus Aurelius reminds us that the patterns we face today have played out countless times before. Birth and death, successes and failures, joy and sorrow—these are all recurring threads in the fabric of existence. Understanding this can ground us in the present and remind us not to be overwhelmed by the moment.
In tech, where rapid innovation and constant change dominate, it’s easy to feel like we’re navigating uncharted waters. But look closely: the challenges you face—tight deadlines, market shifts, difficult decisions—are not new. They’ve been solved before, in different forms, by countless others.
Today, find comfort in the eternal rhythm of life. Instead of being swept up in the urgency of the moment, take a step back. Recognize that what feels overwhelming is part of a greater flow, as constant and enduring as time itself. Face it calmly, knowing that you are not alone in this experience—it’s the same story, repeated anew.
