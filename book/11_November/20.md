## November 20th - THIS MOMENT IS ETERNAL

***“He who has seen present things has seen all, both everything which has taken place from all eternity and everything which will be for time without end; for all things are of one kin and of one form.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 6.37

Look closely at your current crisis. A deadline that can’t move. A stakeholder who rewrites the requirements every week. Two senior engineers who won’t speak to each other. It feels like the worst version of this job anyone has ever had.

Marcus would disagree. Whoever has seen the present, he says, has seen everything, past and future, because all things are of one kin and one form. He meant human life in general, and it holds up remarkably well for software. The tools change every few years. The situations don’t. Someone fought the immovable deadline on a mainframe in 1975, and someone will fight it in a language that doesn’t exist yet.

That has a practical side. If the situation is old, so are the answers. People have written down how to negotiate scope, how to mediate between two stubborn colleagues, how to turn down a requirement without starting a war. Go and find what they learned.

You aren’t the first person in this spot, and that alone makes it lighter.
