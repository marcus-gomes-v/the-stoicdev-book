## November 21st - A MOMENT OF WISDOM LASTS FOREVER

***“A good isn’t increased by the addition of time, but if one is wise for even a moment, they will be no less happy than the person who exercises virtue for all time and happily passes their life in it.” —CHRYSIPPUS QUOTED BY PLUTARCH IN MORALIA: “AGAINST THE STOICS ON COMMON CONCEPTIONS,” 1062***

Wisdom and happiness are not measured by their duration. Like winning an Olympic medal, a single moment of virtue or clarity is timeless. Once experienced, it leaves an indelible mark, a touchstone you can carry with you forever, no matter how brief the encounter.

In tech, we often focus on sustained success ongoing progress, relentless improvement, and constant achievement. But a single breakthrough, a fleeting moment of insight, or even a brief period of collaboration can define your journey. It doesn’t need to last forever to matter forever.

Today, embrace the moments when happiness or wisdom appears. Don’t grasp at them or fear their passing. Recognize that even a single instant of clarity or joy is enough to illuminate your path. Once felt, it becomes a part of you, shaping your perspective and actions for all time.
