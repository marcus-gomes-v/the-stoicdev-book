November 23rd - FREEDOM FROM ATTACHMENTS
“In short, you must remember this—that if you hold anything dear outside of your own reasoned choice, you will have destroyed your capacity for choice.” —EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 4.4.23
Attachments—to people, possessions, status, or even ideas—bind us to things we cannot control. As Epictetus and Anthony de Mello both observed, attachments are the root of unhappiness because they create fear, resistance, and suffering when those things inevitably change or are lost.
In tech, attachments might look like clinging to a specific tool, process, or role. When systems evolve or circumstances shift, holding on to what “used to work” only makes the transition harder. True freedom comes from focusing on what’s within your control—your reasoned choices, your adaptability, and your willingness to embrace change.
Today, take stock of your attachments. Are you overly tied to a particular outcome, a way of working, or a source of recognition? Loosen that grip. By letting go of what’s outside your control, you regain your freedom and strengthen your ability to respond effectively to whatever comes next.
