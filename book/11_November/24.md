## November 24th - PREPARE TO LET GO

***“Whenever you experience the pangs of losing something, don’t treat it like a part of yourself but as a breakable glass, so when it falls you will remember that and won’t be troubled. So too, whenever you kiss your child, sibling, or friend, don’t layer on top of the experience all the things you might wish, but hold them back and stop them, just as those who ride behind triumphant generals remind them they are mortal. In the same way, remind yourself that your precious one isn’t one of your possessions, but something given for now, not forever...”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 3.24.84-86a

Nothing in life is permanent not the projects you work on, the possessions you acquire, or even the relationships you cherish. Epictetus’s wisdom urges us to practice detachment, not out of apathy, but to prepare ourselves for the inevitable. Everything we hold dear is only ours for a time, and pretending otherwise makes loss more painful when it comes.

In tech, this could mean recognizing that a product you poured years into may one day be replaced, or a teammate you value may move on. It’s natural to feel attachment, but training yourself to acknowledge impermanence helps you face these changes with grace.

Today, practice viewing the people and things you care about as fragile gifts, not permanent possessions. Enjoy them fully, but remind yourself they are not truly yours. This perspective doesn’t diminish love or joy; it deepens your appreciation and strengthens your resilience when things inevitably change.
