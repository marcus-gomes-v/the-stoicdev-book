## November 25th - BLESSINGS IN DISGUISE

***“As for me, I would choose being sick over living in luxury, for being sick only harms the body, whereas luxury destroys both the body and the soul, causing weakness and incapacity in the body, and lack of control and cowardice in the soul. What’s more, luxury breeds injustice because it also breeds greediness.”***
—MUSONIUS RUFUS, LECTURES, 20.95.14-17

What seems like a curse illness, failure, or hardship often turns out to be the catalyst for growth, resilience, and clarity. Meanwhile, what appears to be a blessing wealth, luxury, or sudden success can often bring unintended consequences like complacency, greed, or a fractured sense of self.

In tech, winning big (a major funding round, an acquisition, or a viral success) might feel like the ultimate goal, but it can also breed challenges like mismanagement, burnout, or hubris. On the other hand, setbacks a failed product launch or a missed opportunity often spark innovation and sharpen focus.

Today, reflect on how unexpected struggles or disappointments have shaped you for the better. And when things seem too good to be true, stay vigilant. True fortune isn’t what happens to you it’s how you choose to respond to it. Funny how life works out when you see it that way.
