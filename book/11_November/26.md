## November 26th - WE ARE ALL THE SAME IN THE END

***“Many grains of frankincense on the same altar: one falls before, another falls after; but it makes no difference.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 4.15

Marcus compares us to grains of incense thrown on the same altar. One falls into the fire a little sooner, another a little later. It makes no difference.

At work we are very good at measuring the gaps between the grains. Who made staff engineer at thirty. Who has the bigger team or the better equity. Someone from your old cohort is now a director, you find out on a Sunday night while scrolling, and the evening is gone.

Marcus was the most powerful man in his world, and he still wrote this to himself. If the emperor thought the distance between him and everyone else came to nothing, the distance between you and a former classmate is smaller still.

Ambition isn’t the problem. The comparison is. The incense burns either way, so spend the time before it does on your own work and on the people sitting next to you.
