## November 26th - WE ARE ALL THE SAME IN THE END

***“We are like many pellets of incense falling on the same altar. Some collapse sooner, others later, but it makes no difference.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 4.15

The differences we use to measure ourselves against others wealth, age, accomplishments are ultimately trivial. Marcus Aurelius reminds us that, in the grand scheme of things, these distinctions dissolve. We’re all part of the same cycle of life, and we’ll all return to the same earth.

In tech, it’s easy to get caught up in comparisons: Who has the better role? Who shipped the more impressive product? Who’s climbing the career ladder faster? But these comparisons are distractions. They pull you away from your own work, your own growth, and your own journey.

Today, let go of comparisons. Recognize that the differences between you and others are minor and fleeting. Focus instead on the work in front of you, the contributions you can make, and the relationships you can nurture. These are the things that truly matter not how you stack up against others.
