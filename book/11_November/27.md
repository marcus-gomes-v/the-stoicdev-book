## November 27th - FIND PEACE BY TUNING OUT THE NOISE

***“How easy it is to repel and to wipe away every impression which is troublesome or unsuitable, and immediately to be in all tranquillity.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 5.2

There’s an argument in the team channel about whether the new lint rule is an outrage. Your phone shows strangers fighting about your favorite language. Someone has left a passive-aggressive comment on your design doc. None of it needs you, and all of it is pulling at you.

Marcus writes how easy it is to push away an impression that is troublesome or doesn’t belong, and be at peace straight away. Easy may be generous. Still, it’s simpler than we make it. Most noise only gets in because we leave the door open.

So close a few doors. Mute the channel that exists for arguments. Leave the comment until tomorrow morning, when you can answer without heat. Put the phone in a drawer while you work through the hard part of the problem.

You’re protecting the attention good work needs, and attention is much easier to lose than to get back.
