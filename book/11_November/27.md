November 27th - FIND PEACE BY TUNING OUT THE NOISE

“How satisfying it is to dismiss and block out any upsetting or foreign impression, and immediately to have peace in all things.” —MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 5.2

The Stoics understood a truth that feels even more relevant in today’s hyperconnected world: peace comes not from controlling external events but from mastering your reaction to them. With endless notifications, social media debates, and distractions at our fingertips, the ability to tune out negativity has become a superpower.

In tech, this might mean muting unnecessary Slack channels, avoiding toxic debates about tools or processes, or stepping back from frustrating conversations that derail focus. Publilius Syrus’s advice to “shun that which makes you angry” is not avoidance—it’s discipline. It’s knowing when engagement adds no value and choosing to prioritize your energy and peace instead.

Today, practice selective attention. Identify one thing that consistently disrupts your calm—a conversation, a piece of news, or a recurring annoyance—and consciously dismiss it. By tuning out what doesn’t serve you, you create space for clarity, focus, and serenity. That’s not weakness—it’s wisdom.
