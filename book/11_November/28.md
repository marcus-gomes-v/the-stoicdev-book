November 28th - TAKE RESPONSIBILITY FOR HOW YOU RESPOND
“If someone is slipping up, kindly correct them and point out what they missed. But if you can’t, blame yourself—or no one.” —MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 10.4
When someone frustrates you—a teammate missing a deadline, a friend letting you down—it’s easy to place the blame entirely on them. But Marcus Aurelius urges us to shift our perspective. If someone is falling short, the first step is to help, teach, or support them. If that fails, reflect inward: could your expectations be misaligned, or could you offer more patience and understanding?
In tech, where collaboration is key, miscommunication or unmet expectations are common. Instead of jumping to criticism, approach the situation with empathy. Have you clearly communicated? Have you set realistic goals? If you’ve done your part and the issue persists, let it go rather than dwelling on blame.
Today, practice tolerance and understanding. Correct with kindness when you can, and if correction isn’t possible, take responsibility for your reaction. Blame solves nothing—constructive action and self-reflection lead to better outcomes for everyone.
