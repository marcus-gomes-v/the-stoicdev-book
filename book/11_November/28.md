## November 28th - TAKE RESPONSIBILITY FOR HOW YOU RESPOND

***“If a man is mistaken, instruct him kindly and show him his error. But if thou art not able, blame thyself, or blame not even thyself.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 10.4

A new developer on your team keeps making the same mistake: migrations that lock a whole table on deploy. It’s the third time this month. You can feel the irritation building, and the sarcastic review comment is already half written.

Marcus gives a simple order of operations. If someone is mistaken, show them, kindly. If you can’t get through, blame yourself, or blame no one.

Blaming yourself here doesn’t mean beating yourself up. It means looking honestly at your part. Maybe nobody ever explained why table locks matter, and all they got was a red X in review. Maybe the guideline lives only in your head. Maybe a check in CI could catch it before a person has to.

Usually some of the fix is yours. Pair with them on the next migration. Write the two paragraphs of documentation. Add the check. And if it still doesn’t land after all that, drop the blame. It was never going to help anyone write a better migration.
