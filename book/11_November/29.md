## November 29th - TRUST YOUR RESILIENCE

***“Don’t lament this and don’t get agitated.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 7.43
When things go wrong, it’s easy to fall into despair, imagining the worst and feeling as though all is lost. But Marcus Aurelius reminds us of a simple truth: lamenting doesn’t change what has already happened, and agitation only worsens the situation. The future remains uncertain, and within that uncertainty lies the possibility of better days ahead.

In tech, where challenges like failed launches, bugs, or missed deadlines can feel catastrophic, remember this: setbacks are temporary, but your ability to adapt is enduring. Whatever the outcome, you have the tools and resilience to handle it.

Today, when faced with difficulty, pause and remind yourself: “This isn’t the end, and I will be OK.” Instead of wasting energy resisting the past, direct your focus toward navigating the present and preparing for what’s next. Resilience is your constant companion trust it.
