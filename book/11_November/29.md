## November 29th - TRUST YOUR RESILIENCE

***“No joining others in their wailing, no violent emotion.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 7.43

The incident channel at 3 a.m. has its own weather. Someone posts that this is the worst outage in company history. Someone else says the customers are going to leave. A third person starts guessing whose change it was. Within ten minutes the panic has more people in it than the fix.

Marcus wrote himself a note of two short phrases: no joining others in their wailing, no violent emotion. He doesn’t say the problem isn’t real. He says adding your voice to the lament won’t solve it.

Somebody in that channel needs to stay level. Read the graphs. Ask what changed in the last hour. Write down what’s known and what isn’t. It doesn’t have to be the most senior person, only someone who isn’t adding to the noise.

You’ve had bad nights before and you’re still here. Let that steady you enough to be that person.
