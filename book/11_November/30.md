## November 30th - EMBRACE THE PATH OF REASON

***“He who follows reason in all things is both tranquil and active at the same time, and also cheerful and collected.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 10.12

We tend to think calm and action pull in opposite directions. The calm developer never seems to hurry. The active one is always typing, always in three threads at once. Marcus says the person who follows reason gets both: tranquil and active at the same time, cheerful and collected.

The lines just before explain how. If you see clearly what should be done, do it, and don’t keep looking back. If you don’t see clearly, stop and ask the best advisers you have. If something gets in the way, carry on as far as you’re able, keeping to what seems right.

That is a fair description of a good engineer on a hard day. There’s no worry wasted on the decision, because there’s a way to make it, and no frantic motion, because each step has a reason. When it’s unclear, they ask. When it’s clear, they move, and they don’t spend the evening relitigating it.
