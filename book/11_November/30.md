## November 30th - EMBRACE THE PATH OF REASON

***“The person who follows reason in all things will have both leisure and a readiness to act—they are at once both cheerful and self-composed.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 10.12b

The Stoics believed in the logos, a universal guiding reason that governs the flow of life. Like a cart pulling a leashed dog, the logos moves forward whether we resist or not. We can choose to follow willingly, adapting with grace and composure, or we can resist, letting ourselves be dragged along painfully.

In tech, change and challenges often feel like the moving cart market shifts, deadlines, or team dynamics pull us in directions we didn’t choose. Resisting these forces only creates frustration. Accepting and aligning with them, however, allows us to respond thoughtfully, act effectively, and maintain our inner balance.

Today, choose to follow reason and accept what the logos has in store. Approach each situation with cheerful readiness, knowing that while you can’t control the cart, you can control your response. Acceptance doesn’t mean passivity; it means working with the flow of life instead of against it.
