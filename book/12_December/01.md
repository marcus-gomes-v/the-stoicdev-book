## December 1st - LIVE AS IF IT MATTERS

***“So let us shape our minds as if we had reached the very end. Let us put nothing off. Let us settle our accounts with life every day... Whoever puts the last stroke on his life each day does not need more time.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 101.7-8

Most developers end the day with loose threads. A branch that only exists on your laptop. A review you promised to get to. A thank-you you meant to post after someone stayed late to help you with the migration. You tell yourself tomorrow, and tomorrow brings its own threads.

Seneca’s image is an accountant’s. Settle with life every day, so nothing is left owing when the books close. He doesn’t mean you should work until midnight or treat every evening like a farewell. He means don’t carry debts you could pay now.

So before you shut the laptop, close what you can. Push the branch. Write the two lines of context someone will need if you’re out tomorrow. Answer the message you’ve been circling for a week. Tell the person who helped you that it mattered.

December is a good month to start, with the year closing its own books. A day finished that way doesn’t need a sequel to make sense, and you stop feeling that time is always running out on you.
