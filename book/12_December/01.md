December 1st - LIVE AS IF IT MATTERS

“Let us prepare our minds as if we’d come to the very end of life. Let us postpone nothing. Let us balance life’s books each day. . . . The one who puts the finishing touches on their life each day is never short of time.” —SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 101.7b-8a

Living each day as if it were your last doesn’t mean recklessness or indulgence. It means living with intention, clarity, and a focus on what truly matters. Like a soldier preparing for deployment, it’s about handling what needs to be done, expressing love and gratitude, and letting go of trivial distractions.

In tech, where deadlines and tasks can consume your energy, it’s easy to forget that time is finite. But Seneca’s reminder challenges us: Are you prioritizing what matters most? Are you addressing the important things—relationships, values, personal growth—or letting them wait for “someday”?

Today, act as if it’s your last. Don’t waste time on petty arguments or meaningless pursuits. Tell people you appreciate them. Focus on meaningful work. Balance your life’s books by living in alignment with your principles. If you do this daily, you’ll never feel like time has run out.
