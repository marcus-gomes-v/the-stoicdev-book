## December 2nd - LIVE LIKE YOU KNOW YOU’RE DYING

***“Let each thing you would do, say or intend be like that of a dying person.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 2.11.1

We often hear the question, “What would you do if you only had a few months to live?” Yet, the truth is, we’re all living under a terminal diagnosis. Death is not a distant possibility it’s an eventual certainty. Every passing second brings us closer to that reality, whether we acknowledge it or not.

In tech, it’s easy to get lost in the grind endless meetings, deadlines, and sprints. But Marcus Aurelius reminds us to bring urgency and meaning to everything we do. Don’t wait for a wake-up call like illness or loss to shift your priorities. Act now, with the awareness that life is finite.

Today, approach your actions, words, and decisions as if they were your last. Focus on what truly matters building something meaningful, mending relationships, or simply being present. Stop pretending you have endless time and start living with purpose, starting now.
