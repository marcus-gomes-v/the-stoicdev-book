December 3rd - LIFE IS YOUR MASTERPIECE
“Philosophy does not claim to get a person any external possession. To do so would be beyond its field. As wood is to the carpenter, bronze to the sculptor, so our own lives are the proper material in the art of living.” —EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 1.15.2
Philosophy is not an abstract intellectual exercise—it’s a craft, a practical discipline for shaping your life into its best form. As a sculptor carves marble or a carpenter shapes wood, you have the tools to refine your actions, decisions, and character. The goal? To live well and die well, creating a legacy of virtue and purpose.
In tech, you’re often focused on building products, systems, or solutions. But don’t forget the most important thing you’re building: yourself. Every decision—how you respond to stress, how you treat teammates, how you balance ambition with humility—is part of the sculpture you’re creating.
Today, see your life as a work of art in progress. Approach each choice and action with the care and precision of an artisan. Philosophy is your chisel, and with it, you can shape a life that’s both meaningful and enduring.
