## December 4th - WHAT DO YOU REALLY OWN?

***“The things that are subject to hindrance, deprivation, and compulsion are not a man’s own, but those which cannot be hindered are his own.”***
—EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 3.24.3

Run the test on your own life. Your job title can change in a reorg you hear about on a Thursday. The product you spent two years on can be sunset with one slide at an all-hands. Your laptop gets wiped the day you leave, and the repository you think of as yours locks you out the moment your badge stops working.

By Epictetus’s rule, none of that is yours. Anything another person can take away or stop you from using was only ever on loan.

What passes the test is smaller and harder to see. Your judgment about what matters. The care you put into the work. Whether you tell the truth in the postmortem. No reorg reaches those, and nobody can revoke your access to them.

You don’t have to stop caring about the title or the product. Care about them the way you care about a rented flat: keep it clean, enjoy it, and don’t confuse the lease with ownership. Then when the notice comes, and it usually does, you lose a place to work, not yourself.
