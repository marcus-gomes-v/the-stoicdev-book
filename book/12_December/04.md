December 4th - WHAT DO YOU REALLY OWN?

“Anything that can be prevented, taken away, or coerced is not a person’s own—but those things that can’t be blocked are their own.” —EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 3.24.3

The things we think we own—our possessions, titles, health, or even relationships—are not truly ours. They can be taken away by bad luck, circumstances, or time itself. As Epictetus and Marcus Aurelius remind us, what we hold onto so tightly is only ever temporary, borrowed from the universe for a short while.

In tech, where success often hinges on fleeting trends, ownership is equally fragile. A product may become obsolete, a title may shift, or a company may pivot. Clinging to these as if they are permanent only sets you up for disappointment. Instead, focus on what truly belongs to you: your principles, your reason, and your ability to adapt and grow.

Today, reflect on what you truly "own." Recognize that external things are temporary, while your choices, integrity, and mindset are firmly within your control. Let this perspective free you from unnecessary attachment and guide you toward a life centered on what truly matters.
