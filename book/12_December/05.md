## December 5th - STRENGTH IN FACING THE INEVITABLE

***“Keep death and exile before your eyes each day, along with everything that seems terrible—by doing so, you’ll never have a base thought nor will you have excessive desire.”***
—EPICTETUS, ENCHIRIDION, 21

The prospect of death or sudden misfortune isn’t meant to fill us with fear it’s a call to clarity and focus. When we acknowledge life’s fragility, we’re less likely to waste time on trivial pursuits or be consumed by greed and pettiness. Instead, we can center ourselves on what truly matters.

In tech, where the pace is fast and competition fierce, it’s easy to lose sight of the bigger picture. A failed launch feels like the end of the world; a missed promotion stings deeply. But when viewed against the backdrop of mortality, these worries shrink to their proper size. Remembering the impermanence of life can free you to act with purpose, calm, and resilience.

Today, take a moment to reflect on life’s impermanence. Use that awareness not to despair but to guide your actions. Let it remind you to focus on what’s meaningful, act with integrity, and cherish the time and opportunities you have. Sobering thoughts can be the foundation of a richer, fuller life.
