## December 5th - STRENGTH IN FACING THE INEVITABLE

***“Keep before your eyes day by day death and exile, and everything that seems terrible, but most of all death; and then you will never have any abject thought, nor will you yearn for anything beyond measure.”***
—EPICTETUS, ENCHIRIDION, 21

Epictetus knew exile firsthand. When Domitian expelled the philosophers from Rome, he moved to Nicopolis and taught there for the rest of his life. So when he tells you to keep exile and death in view every day, he isn’t speaking in the abstract.

Most of what keeps a developer awake is much smaller. The launch that might slip. The promotion committee that meets next week. The engineer on another team who seems to get every interesting project. Seen up close, any one of these can fill the whole sky.

Put them next to the larger facts and they shrink to their real size. You won’t work at this company forever. You won’t be alive forever. Neither thought is there to scare you. They keep you from groveling for things that don’t deserve it, and from wanting the next level or the next raise with the kind of hunger that makes people cut corners and throw colleagues under the bus.

A short look at the hard facts each morning is enough. The rest of the day tends to go better after it.
