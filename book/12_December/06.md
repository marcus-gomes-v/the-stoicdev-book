## December 6th - LIVE AS IF THE SWORD IS ALWAYS THERE

***“Do not act as if thou wert going to live ten thousand years. Death hangs over thee. While thou livest, while it is in thy power, be good.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 4.17

Every team has a “someday” column. Refactor the auth module someday. Write the onboarding guide someday. Most of it never moves, and for code that’s usually fine.

It’s less fine when the column is inside you. Someday you’ll apologize to the colleague you were short with in March. Someday you’ll stop taking credit in standup for work that was half someone else’s. Someday, when things calm down, you’ll be the kind of lead you meant to be when you took the job.

Marcus writes as if he’d caught himself doing exactly this. Death hangs over you, he says. The old picture is Damocles at the banquet, with a sword hanging by a single hair above his head. The sword is there to make the next sentence land: while you live, while it’s still in your power, be good.

There is no sprint in which goodness finally gets scheduled. The apology, the credit, the patient answer to a beginner’s question all fit into this week, and this week is the only one you can count on.
