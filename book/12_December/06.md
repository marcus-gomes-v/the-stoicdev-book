December 6th - LIVE AS IF THE SWORD IS ALWAYS THERE
“Don’t behave as if you are destined to live forever. What’s fated hangs over you. As long as you live and while you can, become good now.” —MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 4.17
The Sword of Damocles is a powerful reminder that life is fragile and fleeting. Like the courtier in the ancient tale, we may forget the constant presence of uncertainty. But Marcus Aurelius urges us to see that awareness not as a source of fear, but as a motivator to act now—to live virtuously and with purpose while we still can.
In the tech world, it’s tempting to put off meaningful work or important conversations for “someday,” assuming there’s always more time. But just as the sword hangs over a king, so too does it hang over every one of us. Tomorrow is not promised. The best time to do good, to contribute, and to be kind is now.
Today, remember the sword above you. Let it inspire urgency, not dread. Act with integrity, embrace the moment, and live as if this day might be your last. Because one day, it will be. Don’t wait to be good. Start now.
