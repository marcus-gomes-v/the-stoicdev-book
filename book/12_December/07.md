## December 7th - PLAY THE HAND YOU’RE GIVEN

***“Think of the life you have lived until now as over and, as a dead man, see what’s left as a bonus and live it according to Nature. Love the hand that fate deals you and play it as your own, for what could be more fitting?”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 7.56-57

Contemplating mortality doesn’t have to be depressing it can be energizing. When we accept that time is finite, life gains clarity and urgency. Marcus Aurelius urges us to treat every remaining day as a bonus, an opportunity to live in alignment with virtue and purpose.

In tech, where deadlines loom and long-term goals dominate, it’s easy to lose sight of what’s immediate and important. But what if you viewed each task, conversation, or project as part of your "bonus time"? You’d approach them with care, focus, and gratitude, knowing that every moment matters.

Today, embrace the cards fate has dealt you. Instead of dwelling on what you can’t control, focus on playing your hand well. Treat life as the gift it is and let the awareness of mortality inspire you to live with intention, purpose, and presence.
