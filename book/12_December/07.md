## December 7th - PLAY THE HAND YOU’RE GIVEN

***“Consider thyself to be dead, and to have completed thy life up to the present time; and live according to nature the remainder which is allowed thee. Love that only which happens to thee and is spun with the thread of thy destiny. For what is more suitable?”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 7.56-57

Try Marcus’s exercise for a minute. Suppose your life up to this morning were finished. The jobs, the projects, the bad reorg, the great team you had for two years. All of it closed, nothing left to defend.

What remains is extra, and that changes how you look at the hand you’re holding. You got put on the legacy billing system while your friends build the shiny new feature. The startup you joined is running out of runway. Your stack is one the job boards have stopped asking for. On a normal day those feel like bad cards.

Marcus asks you to love what is spun with your own thread, what actually came to you, not what you wished for. You don’t have to pretend the cards are good. Just stop treating them as a mistake in the deal. The billing system is where you are. Somebody has to understand it, and it might as well be the person who decided to play this hand properly.
