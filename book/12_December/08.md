December 8th - FACE YOUR FEELINGS, DON’T SUPPRESS THEM
“It’s better to conquer grief than to deceive it.” —SENECA, ON CONSOLATION TO HELVIA, 17.1b
When we experience loss or pain, it’s natural to want to escape from it—whether through distraction or denial. But Seneca reminds us that true healing doesn’t come from running away; it comes from facing our feelings head-on. Suppressing emotions only delays their eventual reckoning, while processing them allows us to grow stronger and wiser.
In the tech world, where speed and efficiency often take precedence, emotional challenges—like frustration with a project or disappointment in a failed goal—are easy to ignore. But avoidance doesn’t solve the problem; it only compounds it. Just as you debug code to find the root of an issue, you must examine your emotions to truly move forward.
Today, resist the urge to distract yourself from uncomfortable feelings. Instead, sit with them. Analyze them as you would a complex problem. What are they telling you? How can you grow from them? Facing your emotions is not a weakness—it’s a strength that leads to genuine understanding and resilience.
