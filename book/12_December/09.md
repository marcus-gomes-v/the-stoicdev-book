December 9th - PROTECT YOUR MOST PRECIOUS ASSET
“Were all the geniuses of history to focus on this single theme, they could never fully express their bafflement at the darkness of the human mind. No person would give up even an inch of their estate, and the slightest dispute with a neighbor can mean hell to pay; yet we easily let others encroach on our lives—worse, we often pave the way for those who will take it over. No person hands out their money to passersby, but to how many do each of us hand out our lives! We’re tight-fisted with property and money, yet think too little of wasting time, the one thing about which we should all be the toughest misers.” —SENECA, ON THE BREVITY OF LIFE, 3.1-2
Time is the one resource we can never replenish. Yet, as Seneca observes, we are often careless with it—allowing interruptions, distractions, and trivialities to consume our most valuable hours. We defend our money and possessions, but how often do we guard our time with the same vigilance?
In the tech world, where demands on your attention are relentless—emails, Slack messages, endless meetings—it’s easy to let others take over your day. But a philosopher knows that reflection, focus, and inner awareness are worth more than any task or report. Protecting your time isn’t selfish; it’s necessary for meaningful work and a meaningful life.
Today, be a miser with your time. Set boundaries, say no to distractions, and prioritize what truly matters. Treat every moment like the precious and irreplaceable asset it is—because once it’s gone, it’s gone for good.
