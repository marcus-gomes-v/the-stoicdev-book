## December 10th - VALUE YOUR TIME LIKE GOLD

***“I say, let no one rob me of a single day who isn’t going to make a full return on the loss.”***
—SENECA, ON TRANQUILITY OF MIND, 1.11b

Time is your most valuable currency. Yet, unlike money, which we guard carefully, we often spend our time thoughtlessly on unnecessary meetings, shallow commitments, or unproductive distractions. Seneca reminds us to treat our days as we would our wealth, weighing each transaction and asking: Is this worth the cost?

In tech, where demands on your time can pile up quickly, it’s easy to lose hours to interruptions or tasks that don’t align with your goals. But every hour you give away without purpose is one you can never get back. Start thinking of your time as finite and precious.

Today, take control of your schedule. Before saying yes to a meeting, task, or request, ask: What am I getting in return? Is this a meaningful investment of my time? Be deliberate with how you spend your days they’re worth far more than money.
