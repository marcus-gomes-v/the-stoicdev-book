## December 10th - VALUE YOUR TIME LIKE GOLD

***“‘No one,’ I say, ‘that will give me no compensation worth such a loss shall ever rob me of a day.’”***
—SENECA, ON TRANQUILITY OF MIND, 1.11

The words belong to Serenus, the younger friend whose restless letter opens this dialogue. Whenever public life gets messy, he retreats home and makes himself a rule: nobody gets a day of mine unless what they give back is worth the loss.

It’s a good rule for a calendar. Before you accept the invite, look at what you’re trading. A design review where your input will change the outcome is worth it. A status meeting that could have been a paragraph in a doc costs you an afternoon of focus for something you could read in two minutes. The side gig that pays little and eats your weekends is a trade too, and maybe not a good one.

Serenus admits he can’t keep his rule for long, and that’s honest. You won’t keep yours perfectly either. Some days the outage decides for you.

But asking the question still changes things. Once you start pricing your days, you notice how many you were giving away for nothing, often to people who never asked for that much.
