December 11th - TRUE BRAVERY NEEDS NO SECRETS

“As Cicero says, we hate gladiators if they are quick to save their lives by any means; we favor them if they show contempt for their lives.” —SENECA, ON TRANQUILITY OF MIND, 11.4b

Cowardice aims to protect, but it often brings shame and regret instead. True bravery isn’t about reckless violence or seeking danger—it’s about acting with dignity, fulfilling your responsibilities, and standing firm when it matters. As Seneca and Cicero observed, people admire those who face challenges with honor, even when the stakes are high.

In tech, bravery can mean owning up to a mistake, defending an unpopular decision because it’s the right one, or speaking up in a meeting when everyone else stays silent. It’s not about physical courage but moral strength—the willingness to endure discomfort for a greater purpose.

Today, face your challenges with dignity and resolve. Don’t cut corners, shirk responsibilities, or let fear dictate your actions. Bravery doesn’t require grand gestures—just consistent, honorable choices. In the end, it’s not safety but courage that earns respect and builds a lasting legacy.
