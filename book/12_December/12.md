## December 12th - EVERYTHING PASSES, EVERYTHING ENDURES

***“Consider the past—such great changes of political supremacies; thou mayest foresee also the things which will be. For they will certainly be of like form, and it is not possible that they should deviate from the order of the things which take place now; accordingly to have contemplated human life for forty years is the same as to have contemplated it for ten thousand years. For what more wilt thou see?”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 7.49

Spend enough years in this industry and you start to see the loops. Logic moves to the server, then to the client, then back to the server under a new name. Monoliths get split into services, and a few years later someone writes a popular post about merging them again. The framework everyone had to learn becomes the one nobody wants on their CV.

Marcus saw the same pattern with empires. Look back at how often power changed hands, he says, and you can predict what’s coming, because it will have the same shape. Forty years of watching is as good as ten thousand.

That can sound weary, but there’s relief in it. You don’t need to chase every new thing as if it were the one that finally changes everything. You already understand most of what you’ll see next.

What matters is how well you do the work in front of you, whichever turn of the cycle you land in, and how decent you are to the people beside you.
