December 12th - EVERYTHING PASSES, EVERYTHING ENDURES

“Walk the long gallery of the past, of empires and kingdoms succeeding each other without number. And you can also see the future, for surely it will be exactly the same, unable to deviate from the present rhythm. It’s all one whether we’ve experienced forty years or an aeon. What more is there to see?” —MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 7.49

Everything has its time—the rise and fall of empires, the success of companies, the arc of individual lives. Nothing is permanent, yet everything is part of a continuous rhythm. To Marcus Aurelius, this understanding wasn’t a source of despair but of perspective: knowing that all things come and go allows us to focus on the present, where our choices and actions matter most.

In tech, where industries evolve rapidly, remember that even the most successful innovations are temporary. Products will become obsolete, and trends will shift. What endures is the way you approach your work—with integrity, care, and effort.

Today, reflect on the impermanence of things, not with sadness but with clarity. Focus on what you can do right now to contribute meaningfully to the rhythm of life. Accept that everything passes and, in doing so, find peace and purpose in your fleeting but valuable role.
