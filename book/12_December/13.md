December 13th - MAKE YOUR YEARS COUNT
“You aren’t bothered, are you, because you weigh a certain amount and not twice as much? So why get worked up that you’ve been given a certain lifespan and not more? Just as you are satisfied with your normal weight, so you should be with the time you’ve been given.” —MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 6.49
The length of your life is less important than how you spend it. Instead of obsessing over numbers—your age, your years left, or your ambitions for more—focus on making each moment meaningful. Marcus Aurelius reminds us that life is sufficient when lived well. It’s not about how long you live but how purposefully you use the time you have.
In tech, where long hours and future goals often dominate, it’s easy to lose sight of the present. You might think, “I’ll focus on what matters once I hit this milestone or solve that problem.” But life isn’t about waiting for someday—it’s about fully engaging with today.
Today, stop worrying about how much time you have left. Instead, pour your energy into making the time you have worthwhile. Use it wisely, purposefully, and with gratitude. Life is long enough when each day is lived with intention.
