December 14th - WHAT WILL YOU LEARN BEFORE THE END?
“Soon you will die, and still you aren’t sincere, undisturbed, or free from suspicion that external things can harm you, nor are you gracious to all, knowing that wisdom and acting justly are one and the same.” —MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 4.37
Marcus Aurelius, facing his mortality, used his remaining time to reflect on his shortcomings and strive for wisdom, justice, and tranquility. He knew that even as the emperor of Rome, he was far from perfect. His message to himself, and to us, is clear: start now. Don't wait until life’s final chapters to learn and live as you should.
In tech and in life, we often defer growth, telling ourselves, “I’ll improve once this project is done” or “I’ll focus on being better when I have more time.” But life doesn’t always give us the luxury of time. Waiting only delays the peace and fulfillment that come from aligning with virtue and justice.
Today, reflect on the areas of your life where you’re still unsettled, insincere, or reactive. What small changes can you make now to grow closer to the person you aspire to be? Don’t put it off—life is fleeting. Start the work today and reap its rewards sooner rather than later.
