## December 14th - WHAT WILL YOU LEARN BEFORE THE END?

***“Thou wilt soon die, and thou art not yet simple, nor free from perturbations, nor without suspicion of being hurt by external things, nor kindly disposed towards all; nor dost thou yet place wisdom only in acting justly.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 4.37

This is one of the harshest notes Marcus left himself. No comfort, just an inventory of what he still hadn’t fixed. He was the most powerful man in the world when he wrote it, and he didn’t let himself off.

Most of us push this kind of work into a calmer future. After the launch I’ll stop being so defensive in code reviews. After the promotion I’ll be more patient with the support team. Once the reorg settles I’ll stop assuming the worst about the new VP. The calmer future never comes. There is always another launch.

So pick one item from his list and look at it this week. Maybe it’s the suspicion, the habit of reading every vague message from your manager as a threat. Maybe it’s the kindness that somehow stops at people who can help your career.

You won’t finish the list, and neither did he. He just refused to pretend there would be a better time to start.
