December 15th - STRIVE FOR COMPLETENESS

“This is the mark of perfection of character—to spend each day as if it were your last, without frenzy, laziness, or any pretending.” —MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 7.69

Perfection may not be achievable, but striving for it—living each day with purpose, effort, and authenticity—gets us closer to a life well-lived. Marcus Aurelius challenges us to approach every moment with sincerity, avoiding frantic busyness, procrastination, or pretense.

In tech, where constant deadlines and performance metrics can overwhelm, this mindset is grounding. Instead of rushing to check off tasks or putting things off until “later,” aim to make meaningful progress today. Not with frenzy, but with calm and focus.

Today, measure your day not by accomplishments but by the quality of your actions. Were you present? Did you act with integrity? Did you give your best effort? You may not achieve perfection, but each small improvement brings you closer to it. That’s what matters.
