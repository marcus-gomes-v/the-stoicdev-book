## December 15th - STRIVE FOR COMPLETENESS

***“The perfection of moral character consists in this, in passing every day as the last, and in being neither violently excited nor torpid nor playing the hypocrite.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 7.69

Marcus names three ways to spoil a day, and every developer has met all of them.

The first is frenzy. Ten tabs, three half-fixed bugs, a reply fired off before you finished reading the message. It feels like productivity because your pulse says so.

The second is torpor. You open the ticket, read it, and somehow an hour later you’re deep in a thread about a framework you’ll never use.

The third is playing a part, and we rarely admit it. Writing “LGTM” on a pull request you skimmed. Sounding confident in standup about work you haven’t started.

His alternative is plain. Live the day as if it were the last one, which takes away both the panic and the drift, and drop the act. Do the next real thing at a steady pace. It’s an easy standard to describe and a hard one to meet, which is probably why he called it perfection.
