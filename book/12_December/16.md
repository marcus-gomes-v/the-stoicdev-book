December 16th - TRUE HEALTH IS IN YOUR CONTROL

“I tell you, you only have to learn to live like the healthy person does... living with complete confidence. What confidence? The only one worth holding, in what is trustworthy, unhindered, and can’t be taken away—your own reasoned choice.” —EPICTETUS, DISCOURSES, 3.26.23b-24

Physical health is fleeting—it can fail suddenly or erode over time. But the health of your mind and your reasoned choices? That’s entirely within your control. As Epictetus reminds us, this is the only form of “health” that remains untouchable by external forces. It’s the foundation of a life lived with confidence and clarity.

In tech, where problems and setbacks are frequent, external confidence in systems, outcomes, or people can waver. But your ability to choose how you respond to these challenges is yours alone. No outage, deadline, or rejection can take it away unless you allow it.

Today, focus on maintaining the "health" of your reasoned choice. Nurture your ability to act wisely, make clear decisions, and remain steady amid uncertainty. This is the one form of strength you can carry through all circumstances—and the one that truly matters.
