## December 17th - DISCOVER YOURSELF BEFORE TIME RUNS OUT

***“Death lies heavy upon one who, known exceedingly well by all, dies unknown to himself.”***
—SENECA, THYESTES, 400

In a world that rewards outward success and external validation, it’s easy to lose sight of who we truly are. Many of the most accomplished people celebrities, CEOs, and public figures are often deeply disconnected from themselves. They are known by everyone but understand little of their own desires, fears, and values.

In tech, where metrics, achievements, and public recognition can dominate, it’s tempting to focus on what others think of us rather than what we think of ourselves. But as Socrates taught, self-awareness is the foundation of a meaningful life. Who are you beyond the titles, tasks, or accolades?

Today, pause and reflect. Ask yourself: What truly matters to me? What do I enjoy? What do I value? Make time to explore your inner self. Don’t let the world know you better than you know yourself. Start that journey of self-discovery now, before it’s too late.
