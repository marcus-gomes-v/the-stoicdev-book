## December 17th - DISCOVER YOURSELF BEFORE TIME RUNS OUT

***“On him does death lie heavily, who, but too well known to all, dies to himself unknown.”***
—SENECA, THYESTES, 400

Every company has someone everybody knows. The one who gets paged when payments break, whose name is on half the architecture docs, who is invited to any meeting where a decision might go wrong. People know exactly what to expect from them.

Ask that person what they want from the next five years and you’ll sometimes get a long silence.

Seneca gave this line to the chorus in a play about a royal family tearing itself apart over a throne. Death lies heavy, they sing, on the man known too well by everyone and not at all by himself. You don’t need a crown to be known that way. A big following, a talk at a conference, a reputation as the person who never says no will do it.

None of those tell you which work leaves you better at the end of the day, or what you keep saying yes to out of fear. Finding out takes an evening and an honest notebook, not an audience. It’s much easier to start at thirty-five than at the end.
