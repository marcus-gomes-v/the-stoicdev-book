December 18th - THE GREAT EQUALIZER
“Both Alexander the Great and his mule-keeper were both brought to the same place by death—they were either received into the all-generative reason, or scattered among the atoms.” —MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 6.24
Death spares no one. Whether a world-conquering emperor or a humble servant, everyone meets the same fate. It’s the ultimate equalizer, stripping away titles, wealth, and accomplishments. This truth isn’t meant to depress us but to humble us and inspire gratitude for the life we have.
In tech, where hierarchies and competition often dominate, it’s easy to let ego or insecurity take over. You might feel superior because of a promotion or bitter over someone else’s success. But Marcus Aurelius reminds us that these distinctions are fleeting and meaningless in the grand scheme. What matters is how we live, not how we compare.
Today, let the inevitability of death be a reminder to treat others—whether above or below you on the ladder—with dignity and respect. Focus less on external achievements and more on the character you cultivate, knowing that in the end, that’s all that truly endures.
