## December 18th - THE GREAT EQUALIZER

***“Alexander the Macedonian and his groom by death were brought to the same state; for either they were received among the same seminal principles of the universe, or they were alike dispersed among the atoms.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 6.24

One man conquered most of the known world. The other looked after his horses. Marcus notes, without much ceremony, that death put them in exactly the same place.

Org charts are a lot smaller than empires, and they still go to our heads. You get the staff title and notice that you’ve stopped answering juniors’ questions as patiently as you used to. Or it runs the other way: a peer gets promoted over you, and for months you can barely sit through their demos.

Both reactions treat the ladder as if it measured something permanent. It doesn’t. The director and the contractor who started last week will both, one day, be names nobody at the company recognizes, and then not even names.

What survives the comparison is how you treated people while the ladder was standing. The intern who got a real answer from you. The peer you congratulated and meant it. That part stays true after the org chart is gone.
