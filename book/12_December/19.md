December 19th - FIND PERSPECTIVE IN THE BIGGER PICTURE
“Think of the whole universe of matter and how small your share. Think about the expanse of time and how brief—almost momentary—the part marked for you. Think of the workings of fate and how infinitesimal your role.” —MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 5.24
The universe is vast beyond comprehension, and our time within it is fleeting. Marcus Aurelius reminds us that our existence, though significant to us, is but a blink in the timeline of the cosmos. This perspective isn’t meant to diminish us but to liberate us—freeing us from the illusion that everything depends on us or that our struggles are as monumental as they seem.
In tech, where deadlines loom and pressure feels immense, it’s easy to believe the world hinges on the success of a project or product. But taking a step back reveals the truth: your work is important, but it’s not everything. Understanding your small place in the vast universe allows you to approach challenges with calmness and humility.
Today, when stress creeps in or your ego flares up, remember your human scale. Focus on doing your best without attaching yourself to outcomes or overinflating your role. You’re one person in a vast, interconnected world—and that’s more than enough.
