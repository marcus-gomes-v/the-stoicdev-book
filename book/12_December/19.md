## December 19th - FIND PERSPECTIVE IN THE BIGGER PICTURE

***“Think of the universal substance, of which thou hast a very small portion; and of universal time, of which a short and indivisible interval has been assigned to thee; and of that which is fixed by destiny, and how small a part of it thou art.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 5.24

It’s midnight before the release and the whole world seems to fit inside one terminal window. If this migration fails, everything fails. Your chest is tight and you’re reading the same log line for the fourth time.

Marcus had a habit for moments like this. He zoomed out. All the matter in the universe, and your small share of it. All of time, and the sliver handed to you. Everything fate has in store, and how little of it is you.

Written down, it sounds bleak. In practice it works like stepping outside for some air. The migration matters. It’s also one of millions of things happening tonight, on a planet that was here long before the database and will be here long after it’s retired.

You still have to fix the migration. You’ll just fix it better with a steady hand, and the steadiness comes from knowing it isn’t the whole universe. It’s a job, and tonight it happens to be yours.
