## December 21st - MEASURE YOUR LIFE BY HOW YOU LIVE IT

***“Many times an old man has no other evidence besides his age to prove he has lived a long time.”***
—SENECA, ON TRANQUILITY OF MIND, 3.8b

It’s easy to let the years slip by, busy with routines, distractions, or the pursuit of material things. But as Seneca reminds us, longevity isn’t the same as living well. A long life full of wasted hours and mindless activities isn’t a life worth bragging about. What truly matters is how those hours were spent on growth, connection, wisdom, and purpose.

In tech, it’s tempting to measure your life by launches, promotions, or milestones. But do those accomplishments reflect a life well-lived? Or are they just trophies on a shelf, gathering dust? Instead, focus on progress in areas that matter: building meaningful relationships, solving real problems, and growing as a person.

Today, reflect on what you’ve done with your time and what you want to do with what remains. Don’t let the days pass without purpose. Use your hours wisely, so when the end comes, you can say with pride that you didn’t just live you truly lived.
