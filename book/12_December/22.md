## December 22nd - MAKE YOUR OWN MARK

***“For it’s disgraceful for an old person, or one in sight of old age, to have only the knowledge carried in their notebooks. Zeno said this . . . what do you say? Cleanthes said that . . . what do you say? How long will you be compelled by the claims of another? Take charge and stake your own claim—something posterity will carry in its notebook.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 33.7

Quoting others is easy, but true wisdom lies in forming your own ideas and expressing them clearly. The great thinkers we admire Zeno, Seneca, Marcus Aurelius didn’t simply repeat what they’d heard. They wrestled with life’s questions, reflected on their experiences, and left a legacy of original thought. Now, it’s your turn.

In tech, it’s easy to rely on others' insights quoting experts, following established patterns, or repeating industry wisdom. But innovation and leadership come from staking your own claim, learning from experience, and voicing your perspective. What have you learned from your work, your failures, your journey?

Today, stop deferring to what others have said or done. Reflect on your own life and experiences, and articulate what you think and know. Your voice matters, and your contribution can inspire others just as those you’ve admired have inspired you.
