December 23rd - IS IT REALLY A LIFE YOU’RE AFRAID TO LOSE?
“You are afraid of dying. But, come now, how is this life of yours anything but death?” —SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 77.18
Fear of death grips many of us, but Seneca’s question forces us to reflect: What exactly are we so afraid of losing? Is it a life filled with purpose and fulfillment? Or is it a life wasted on distractions, idle pursuits, and missed opportunities? To cling desperately to a mediocre existence isn’t living—it’s merely surviving.
In tech, it’s easy to fall into the trap of busyness—endless emails, meetings, or chasing goals that don’t align with your values. But are these the things you’d fight to protect? Or is it the meaningful contributions, the deep relationships, and the growth you’re working toward?
Today, examine your life honestly. Are you truly living, or just going through the motions? Fear of death becomes irrelevant when you’re living a life worth dying for—one of purpose, action, and presence. If that’s not your reality yet, start building it now.
