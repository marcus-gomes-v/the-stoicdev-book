## December 23rd - IS IT REALLY A LIFE YOU’RE AFRAID TO LOSE?

***“You fear dying. And what then? Is the life you are living not itself a death?”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 77.18

Seneca is talking to a man who badly wants to keep living and has no idea how to live. You’re afraid to die, he says. Fine. But look at the days you’re protecting.

It’s an uncomfortable question to bring to work. Picture a week that looks exactly like the last forty. The same standup, the same tickets dragged from left to right, the same evening scroll until it’s late enough to sleep. Nothing terrible happens in it. Nothing you’d miss, either.

Most people in that state aren’t lazy. They drifted. The job was exciting for a while, then safe, then just there.

Treat the question as a check rather than a reason to quit on Monday. If losing these days wouldn’t feel like losing much, the days need something worth losing: a problem you care about, someone you’re teaching, work you’d stay late for because you want to. Often you can find that in the job you already have. Sometimes you can’t, and then you know what the question was telling you.
