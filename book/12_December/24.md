## December 24th - PUT PLEASURE IN ITS PLACE

***“You know the taste of wine and of honeyed wine. Whether a hundred jars pass through your bladder or a thousand changes nothing: you are a strainer.”***
—SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 77.16

Seneca isn’t being polite here. You already know what wine tastes like, he tells the pleasure-seeker. A thousand more jars won’t teach you anything new. You’ll just be the cloth the wine runs through.

Most developers aren’t in danger from wine, but we have our own jars. The fourth mechanical keyboard. The monitor upgrade that was going to change everything. A new framework every quarter, learned to the depth of the tutorial and then dropped. Tech talks watched at double speed while answering email. Each one is pleasant enough. After the first few, none of them changes you much. They pass through.

Nothing is wrong with a good keyboard. The trouble starts when collecting becomes the point, and you mistake the flow of new things through your life for growth.

The test is simple. After this one, will you be able to do something you couldn’t do before? If yes, enjoy it. If not, you already know the taste.
