December 24th - PUT PLEASURE IN ITS PLACE

“You know what wine and liqueur tastes like. It makes no difference whether a hundred or a thousand bottles pass through your bladder—you are nothing more than a filter.” —SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 77.16

It’s easy to be seduced by the finer things—collecting the best gadgets, savoring rare wines, or chasing the latest trends. But as Seneca reminds us, these indulgences don’t define us. At their core, they are fleeting, passing through us without adding lasting value to our lives.

In tech, this might mean chasing the newest frameworks, obsessing over hardware upgrades, or diving deep into niche knowledge just for status. But does it make you better? Does it bring you closer to a meaningful life? Being a "conduit" for possessions or accolades is not the same as being fulfilled.

Today, reflect on what truly matters. Enjoy life’s pleasures, but don’t let them consume you or define your worth. Focus instead on what endures—virtue, relationships, and the contributions you make. Let those be your lasting legacy, not just the fleeting things you’ve consumed.
