## December 25th - REST IS A TOOL, NOT A WEAKNESS

***“Men’s minds ought to have relaxation: they rise up better and more vigorous after rest. We must not force crops from rich fields, for an unbroken course of heavy crops will soon exhaust their fertility, and so also the liveliness of our minds will be destroyed by unceasing labour, but they will recover their strength after a short period of rest and relief: for continuous toil produces a sort of numbness and sluggishness.”***
—SENECA, ON TRANQUILITY OF MIND, 17.5

Seneca compares the mind to a rich field. Force it to produce crop after crop and it wears out. Let it lie fallow for a while and it comes back stronger.

Most developers have felt the proof without naming it. You fight a bug all evening and get nowhere. You give up, sleep, and the answer arrives while you’re making coffee. Nothing changed except that the field got a night off.

Our industry still tends to treat rest as weakness. The person who answers messages on holiday gets quiet credit for it. The green dot at eleven at night looks like commitment. Seneca would call it what it is, a mind worked so hard that it goes numb and dull, and then wonders why the ideas stopped coming.

For many people today is a day off. Take all of it. Turn the notifications off instead of glancing at them between courses. The codebase will be exactly where you left it, and you’ll see it more clearly when you come back.
