December 26th - MAKE LIFE COUNT
“It’s not at all that we have too short a time to live, but that we squander a great deal of it. Life is long enough, and it’s given in sufficient measure to do many great things if we spend it well. But when it’s poured down the drain of luxury and neglect, when it’s employed to no good end, we're finally driven to see that it has passed by before we even recognized it passing. And so it is—we don’t receive a short life, we make it so.” —SENECA, ON THE BREVITY OF LIFE, 1.3-4a
We often complain that life feels too short, but Seneca reminds us: the problem isn’t the length of our lives but how we use our time. The hours slip by, wasted on distractions, trivial pursuits, or commitments that don’t align with our values. Time, unlike money, can never be earned back once it’s spent.
In tech, where constant deadlines and the grind of productivity reign, it’s easy to confuse being busy with using time wisely. But are those long hours and packed calendars truly meaningful? Are you spending your days on work and relationships that matter—or are they being frittered away on unnecessary tasks?
Today, take stock of how you spend your time. Where are the hours slipping through your fingers? What could you cut to focus on what truly matters? Life is long enough if you live it deliberately. Make every moment count.
