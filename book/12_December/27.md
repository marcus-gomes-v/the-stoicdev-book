## December 27th - LET YOUR SOUL OUTLAST YOUR BODY

***“It is a shame for the soul to be first to give way in this life, when thy body does not give way.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 6.29

Marcus was not a healthy man. He suffered from chest and stomach trouble, spent years in army camps on the Danube frontier, and still found the will to write notes like this one. The shame, he says, is when the soul gives up first while the body keeps going.

At work it takes the form of a particular kind of cynicism. The developer who is perfectly well, sleeps fine, earns a good salary, and has stopped caring. “They’ll rewrite it next year anyway.” “Nobody reads the docs, so why write them.” “I just do the tickets.” The body shows up every morning. Something else stayed home.

Sometimes that’s burnout, and burnout needs rest, not a lecture. But sometimes it’s a slow surrender that nobody noticed, including you.

If you catch yourself saying those lines, treat it as a signal. Pick one thing in your work you still care about, however small, and do it properly this week. The caring tends to come back faster than you’d expect.
