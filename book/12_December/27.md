December 27th - LET YOUR SOUL OUTLAST YOUR BODY

“It’s a disgrace in this life when the soul surrenders first while the body refuses to.” —MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 6.29

Marcus Aurelius faced illness, betrayal, and the weight of an empire, yet he never allowed his spirit to falter. His body may have weakened, but his resolve remained steadfast. It’s a reminder that life’s challenges, no matter how daunting, are no excuse to abandon our principles or give in to despair.

In tech or leadership, the grind can take a toll—failed launches, endless hours, or dealing with setbacks might tempt you to throw in the towel. But even when the body is tired, the soul can stay firm. Your commitment to doing the right thing, staying resilient, and leading with integrity matters most, even when the circumstances are far from ideal.

Today, let Marcus’s example inspire you. Don’t let frustration, fatigue, or difficulty break your spirit. You may not control what happens to your body or your circumstances, but you always control your soul. Keep it strong, no matter what.
