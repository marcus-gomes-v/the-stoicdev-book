## December 28th - WHAT WILL TRULY LAST?

***“Everything lasts for a day, the one who remembers and the remembered.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 4.35

The names carved into stone, the headlines, the accolades all fade with time. Even the most renowned figures of history are eventually forgotten by the masses. Marcus Aurelius understood this truth and reminded himself that no matter how grand the legacy, both the remembered and those who remember are temporary.

In the tech world, where achievements are celebrated and obsolescence is swift, this lesson resonates deeply. That groundbreaking product, award, or promotion you’re chasing will it matter in 10 years? 100? Likely not. But the integrity, kindness, and dedication you bring to your work and relationships will leave ripples that matter far beyond any title or recognition.

Today, let go of the need to be remembered and focus instead on being meaningful in the present. Be good to the people around you, do the best work you can, and live with purpose. These actions, though perhaps fleeting, are the most lasting impact you’ll ever leave.
