## December 28th - WHAT WILL TRULY LAST?

***“Everything is only for a day, both that which remembers and that which is remembered.”***
—MARCUS AURELIUS, MEDITATIONS, 4.35

Run git blame on any file that has been around for a few years. Half the names belong to people who left long ago, and you’ve never met most of them. A few wrote the parts everyone still depends on, and nobody on the team could tell you who they were.

Marcus fits the whole idea into one line. Everything lasts a day, the one who remembers and the one who is remembered. Your code will be rewritten. The people who praised it at the demo will move on, and the people who replace them won’t know the story.

That could be depressing. It can also be a relief. If being remembered is off the table, you can stop working for it. No more polishing a project for the promotion packet, or sulking when someone else presents your idea as theirs.

What’s left is the work and the people in front of you this week. A clean fix, a patient review, a colleague who had a better afternoon because you were there. Nobody will remember those either. They still happened, and they were good.
