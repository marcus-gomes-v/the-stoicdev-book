December 29th - BEGIN AND END WITH GRATITUDE
“In all things we should try to make ourselves be as grateful as possible. For gratitude is a good thing for ourselves, in a manner in which justice, commonly held to belong to others, is not. Gratitude pays itself back in large measure.” —SENECA, MORAL LETTERS, 81.19
Gratitude isn’t just for the recipient; it transforms the giver. When we actively acknowledge the good in our lives—big and small—it shifts our perspective, radiates positivity, and deepens our sense of fulfillment. Gratitude is its own reward, and it pays dividends by enhancing how we see and experience the world.
In tech, where stress and problem-solving dominate, it’s easy to overlook the little things: the colleague who helped debug an issue, the satisfaction of solving a complex problem, or even the luxury of having the tools to create and build. But these small moments are worth acknowledging—they ground us and remind us of the joy in the journey.
Today, start and end your day with gratitude. Reflect on what went well, on the people who made a difference, and on the opportunities you have. Even if it’s your last day on earth, you’ll find plenty to be thankful for. Let that gratitude touch every part of your life.
