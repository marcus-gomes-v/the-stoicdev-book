December 30th - FIND CALM IN THE CHAOS

“To bear trials with a calm mind robs misfortune of its strength and burden.” —SENECA, HERCULES OETAEUS, 231-232
The people who handle adversity with grace and poise don’t possess some unattainable quality. Their secret is simple: they meet challenges with a calm mind. Whether it’s an unexpected setback, sharp criticism, or even the inevitability of death, their discipline and preparation allow them to stay steady and navigate forward.

In tech, where failure and uncertainty are common, this mindset is invaluable. A product launch might go wrong, a bug might derail progress, or a career path might take an unexpected turn. Panic won’t fix these situations—but clarity and calm will. By anticipating difficulties and practicing composure, you take the bite out of even the toughest trials.

Today, prepare your mind for whatever may come. Reflect on challenges calmly and don’t let misfortune shake you. When adversity strikes, let your reasoned and steady mind carry you through. That’s your greatest asset—now and always.
